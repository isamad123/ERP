# Handoff: S1.09a settings and feature flags (slice S1.09 Build items 1 to 5)

- **Done:**
  - **Manifests (KRN-SET-01, KRN-SET-03, Q10).** One manifest per module, `<module>.Infrastructure/Settings/settings.yaml`.
    - The generator checks it against the kernel's own setting rules (`Erp.Kernel.Domain/Settings/SettingRules.cs`, compiled into the generator).
    - It writes `Access/settings.g.json`, which the migrator already ships, and `KernelSettingKeys.g.cs`.
    - `SettingsSetup` registers the manifests on `migrate` and provisioning, replacing `IdentitySettings.SeedAsync` and `NumberingSettings.SeedAsync`.
    - The 22 existing settings keep their key, module, type, default, validation, layers, approval flag and description. This is proven against `tests/Erp.IntegrationTests/Settings/settings-before-s1-09a.json`, captured from the old seed code.
    - Names, help texts and areas are new; the Urdu needs a native speaker's review.
    - A setting no longer declared is kept hidden, never deleted.
  - **Resolver (KRN-SET-02, Q11, CR-037).** `SettingResolution` in `Erp.Kernel.Domain/Settings` decides every value: product default, then pack, customer, company, role, user; the most specific valid value wins.
    - Invalid stored values are skipped and reported in the layered view.
    - Several roles combine by the setting's role rule: any, all, lowest or highest.
    - `tighten_only` settings (`iam.mfa.required`) can only be switched on by role and user values.
    - `SettingsStore` reads on a given transaction for identity, numbering and access, which now use the resolver.
    - `SettingsService` (`ISettings`, `IFeatureFlags`) reads from the cache.
  - **Changes (KRN-SET-04, ADM-SET-04, ADM-SET-06, Q12).** `SettingsAdministrationService` handles set, reset to inherited and revert.
    - Every change has a reason and writes a history row, an event (`cfg.setting_changed` / `cfg.setting_reset`) and audit rows.
    - All writes go through `SettingWrites`.
    - A setting that needs approval becomes a maker-checker request (`AccessChange` kind `cfg.setting.change`). It is approved at `/api/iam/maker-checker-requests/{id}/approve`; self-approval is refused, and the approver must cover the company.
    - `iam.*` keys go through the access settings service: S1.06 rules, the second-tenant-admin rule, and the new `ResetAsync`.
  - **Propagation (KRN-SET-06, Q13 A).** `cfg.config_versions` counters are raised by statement triggers.
    - Each host's `SettingsCache` re-reads them at most every `Settings:RefreshMilliseconds` (default 1000).
    - The host that made a change drops its cache at once.
    - Measured with a real second API process: 53 ms for a setting and 984 ms for a flag.
  - **Flags (KRN-SET-05, Q14).** The registry comes from manifests, with overrides for the customer, a company or a user group; when the user's groups disagree, on wins.
    - A flag whose entitlement key is not licensed is off. `ILicensedFeatures` reads `claims.features` of `meta.local_entitlement`; S1.09b replaces this.
    - No product flag exists in 1.0; tests use test flags.
  - **Endpoints (Q16):** `Erp.Kernel.Api/Settings/SettingsEndpoints.cs`. The list is in `docs/registers/contracts.md`.
    - Company Admin now holds `cfg.setting_values.update` and can change the company level of its own companies only.
    - Changing an `iam.*` setting needs all-company rights (CR-038).
    - Texts are in `Erp.Kernel.Contracts/Settings/Localization/settings.{en,ur}.json`.
- **Data design and generator:**
  - Migrations `V202611150306__kernel__design_s1_09a.sql` (CR-036 parts 1 to 4, CR-037) and `V202611150336__kernel__functions_s1_09a.sql` (trigger function).
  - Generated writes are closed on values, overrides and history.
  - Generator keys: slice part letters, required-with-default columns added later, and function `triggers` (CR-038; documented in `docs/data-design/README.md`).
- **Rulings this session:** M, F, H (CR-037). Deviations are in CR-038; risk R-019 (time zone not checked at write).
