## Built (3 lines)
Positions are created, changed from a date (a new row), frozen, closed and read with their holders (`/api/hrm/positions/...`); grades and headcount budgets use the generated endpoints, with value rules in the database.
The vacancy rule (BR-04) refuses a hire or change that fills a position beyond its budgeted FTE, or a unit and grade beyond its sanctioned count, unless an approved strength exception (`POST /api/hrm/strength-exceptions`, document type `hrm.strength_exception`) covers it; the event names the exception.
The approver directory answers department heads (head position of the unit, walking up) and position holders by code; the company setting `hrm.org.model` is guarded by a new kernel hook (`ISettingChangeGuard`).
