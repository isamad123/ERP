# 2026-11-26 S1.41 loans and advances with payroll recovery (Build items 1 to 7)
Lane (CR-082). Model: Sonnet 5.5, medium effort (class C, no fresh review). Rulings Q0 to Q6 (CR-083) applied; detail in CR-097 (S1.41 detail) a to k; risks R-181 (existing) and R-182.

## Built
HR sets up loan and advance types (each names a `loan` policy and a deduction component; no number has a default). An employee applies, or HR applies for an employee (found by employee number, recorded as on behalf); the application is checked first and shown with its installments (service, grade, loans open, the most to lend, the longest repayment, guarantor), then goes to the employee's own line manager, the guarantor when the type asks, and finance; nobody approves their own. On approval the schedule is built (none, flat or reducing balance, rounded to the currency's minor unit, last installment takes the remainder, principals add up exactly). Payroll recovers the installment due in the run's period (and any an earlier run could not take); the ledger, the `deducted` status and the event come when the run locks. A repayment received outside payroll (extra or full) waits for a second person. Balance, schedule, ledger, the outstanding report and a reconciliation report show the state; amounts are left out for anyone without the salary field class.
