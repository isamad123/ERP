## Built
HR defines leave types (paid or unpaid, calendar days, working days or hours, unit, document and comp-off flags; retired, never deleted). One `leave` policy per population
holds each type's rules (entitlement model, pro-rata, rounding, carry-forward, encashment, negative limit, notice, longest leave, eligibility, sandwich, combining); no
number is filled in. The job `hrm.leave_accrual` grants and accrues by policy once per employment, type and period; HR posts openings, grants and adjustments and corrects
only by reversal; the database refuses any change to an entry. Balances as of a date list the entries that make them; `hrm.leave_recompute` and an endpoint compare ledger and
policies and list each difference with its cause, changing nothing.
