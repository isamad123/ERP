## Built (3 lines)
The strength report (`/api/hrm/strength-report`, drill, CSV) counts sanctioned, filled, vacant, over, planned and frozen per unit, grade and location as of any date, exactly as the vacancy rule counts; the chart (`/api/hrm/org-chart`, export PDF or CSV) lists reporting lines of positions (managers in a headcount company) with holders and vacant boxes.
A unit move keeps positions and people (they reference the unit), the kernel writes `org.unit_moved` and an HR consumer writes `hrm.org_structure_changed`; a unit with active positions or current people cannot be switched off (`IOrgUnitUseGuard`, an EF save interceptor).
Screens: positions (list, form with change from a date, freeze, close, holders, history), grades, headcount budgets, strength report, chart by position (keyboard tree, search, zoom, print, export), and the `PositionPicker` component for the employee change dialog.
