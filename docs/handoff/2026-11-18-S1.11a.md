# Handoff: S1.11a jobs and events (slice S1.11 Build items 1 to 7)

- **Done:**
  - **Job catalogue:** manifest per module `<module>.Infrastructure/Access/jobs.json` (kernel: `src/Platform/Erp.Kernel.Infrastructure/Access/jobs.json`, embedded). Checked by `JobCatalog` when a host starts. Seeded into `job.definitions` by `migrate`, provisioning and licence changes (`JobSetup`); a customer's schedule and on/off choice are kept once they change them.
  - **Schedules:** the simple shapes of ruling Q3 (`JobSchedule`, Domain): every N minutes, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly or the last day, in `org.time_zone`, with optional working days only (P5).
  - **Scheduler and runner:** `JobWorker`, worker host.
    - Runs once per schedule slot (unique `(definition_id, scheduled_for)`).
    - Runs are taken under a lease (`for update skip locked`, a per-database claim lock for the concurrency limit), renewed every third of it, and taken over after the lease expires.
    - Chunk work and checkpoint commit together after the lease and cancel checks (`IJobExecution.CommitChunkAsync`).
    - Time limit, retry with back-off (`next_attempt_at`, P1), cancel at the next chunk, priority.
    - Customers are taken in turn, 4 slots per worker and 2 per customer (Q5).
    - Access modes (Q6): `runs_in`; the licence is asked in full and read-only mode.
  - **Job actor:** `JobRunContext`, `JobCurrentActor`, `JobAuditContext`, and the fixed grant in `AccessEngine` (`AccessSnapshot.ForGrant`). R-009 closed.
  - **Event dispatcher:** `EventDispatcher`, every schema's outbox the application may update; inbox per consumer in the same transaction; back-off from 10 s up to 1 h.
  - **Kernel jobs:** `KernelJobs.cs`: licence refresh, escalation, anchor copy, audit check with the clock check, partitions (`meta.ensure_partitions`), maker-checker expiry, operational checks, clean-up.
  - **Alerts:** `OpsAlerts`, as `kernel.alert.raised` events with audience and mandatory flag. The same key is raised at most once an hour.
  - **Job centre API:** `/api/jobs/...` (`JobEndpoints`, `JobCentreService`).
  - **Loops removed:** `LicenceLoop` and `WorkflowEscalationLoop` are gone; the worker host is composed in `WorkerHost.Configure`.
- **Rulings:** plan points P1 to P9 were approved with the plan as recommended and are recorded as CR-046. New risks R-025 (nothing alerts if every worker stops) and R-026 (the audit schema's outbox is read-only for the application, so it is skipped).

  - `job.runs`: `parameters`, `priority`, `attempt`, `scheduled_for`, `cancel_requested_at`, `result_file_id`, `actor_user_id`, `next_attempt_at`.
  - Every outbox gets `attempts`, `next_attempt_at`, `last_error`.
  - Generated job writes are closed.
  - Files: `V202611150308__kernel__design_s1_11a.sql`, `V202611150308__hrm__design_s1_11a.sql` (the first hrm design file), `V202611150338__kernel__functions_s1_11a.sql`.
