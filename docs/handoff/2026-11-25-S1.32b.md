## Built
An employee (or HR for them, recorded as the one who applied) asks for full, half-day or hourly leave and sees the days and the balance before and after; days are counted from
the employee's plan (roster, weekly off, holidays) with the policy's sandwich mode; every check of the policy runs (eligibility, notice, longest, overlap, combining, balance with
waiting requests and expiring leave, locked periods, closed years). The request goes to the line manager, then HR where the policy says; nobody approves their own. Approval
posts the usage; cancellation and change give it back by reversal (with approval where the policy says). Unpaid days and paid-out units are read by payroll through
`ILeaveDeductions` and `ILeaveEncashments`. Comp-off is earned once from `hrm.attendance.comp_off_earned` and expires by the daily `hrm.leave_expiry`. The year-end job makes a
preview; HR confirms (a second person approves) or rolls back; confirmation posts once. Packs install editable leave types; a transfer between companies carries, pays out or
lapses each balance as recorded.

## Recipe: files on an HR record (CR-114)
Write a class implementing `Erp.Kernel.Contracts.Documents.IAttachmentOwner` with `OwnerType` = the table name (for example `hrm.duty_requests`). In `AccessAsync(id)` return null when the record
does not exist. Otherwise return its company and who may read, add or replace, and read sensitive files. These follow the record itself: its parties, the readers of the table in their data scope, and the
approvers with an open task (`IWorkflowQueries`). Writing is allowed only while the record is still open. Register it with `services.AddScoped<IAttachmentOwner, …>()` in the registration both hosts call.
Clients upload with `/api/doc/attachments` (owner type and id); delete and versions follow the owner. To keep ids on the record, read `IRecordFiles.CurrentAsync(type, id, tx)` on save or submit.
Set `retention_until` (and `document_type`) for retention rules as S1.11c1 describes. Copy `LeaveRequestAttachmentOwner` (`Leave/LeaveRequestHandlers.cs`).
