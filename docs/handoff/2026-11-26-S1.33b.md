## Built
HR sees and manages leave from five screens under Time: leave types (list, create, change, retire, colour token), leave rules (the `leave` policy shown per leave type in grouped sections, drafts edited with the
schema's messages beside the fields, who the policy applies to), the employee ledger (filters, source documents as links, running balance checked against the server, reverse, post an opening, grant or
adjustment) with the balance check that lists each difference and its cause and posts nothing by itself, year-end (runs, prepare a preview, preview table, confirm, rollback, applied runs read-only) and three
leave reports (balance summary by unit and type, leave taken by type and month, requests waiting) exported through the export centre. English and Urdu, right to left; R-306 and R-307 are warned about.
