## Built
HR defines an `attendance` policy (grace, half-day and absence limits, breaks, rounding, the shift window, rest-day work; no number is filled in). Every night the job
`hrm.attendance_process` turns each employee's last two days (once their shift and window have ended) into one result (present, absent, late, half day, on leave, holiday, weekly off, on duty, incomplete) from the
published roster, the policy, approved leave and duty and the counted punches, with an explanation stored as keys and values ("Late 22 minutes: shift starts 09:00, first
punch 09:22, grace 10." in English and Urdu). Missing, odd, out-of-window and unscheduled punches open exceptions; the queue also lists unmapped device punches. A changed
input writes a new row version (history kept, the database refuses edits); a late punch re-queues only its day; locked days are never reprocessed.
