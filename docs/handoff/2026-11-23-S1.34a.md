# 2026-11-23 S1.34a pay groups, pay periods, payroll calendar and readiness (Build items 1 to 3)
Lane C (CR-082). Model: Sonnet 5.5, medium effort (class C; no Opus review for this part, only S1.34b has one). Deviations in CR-087 (S1.34a detail a to i); risks R-150 to R-152.

## Built
Pay groups (frequency, currency one per group and the company's by default, tax-year start month, cut-off and payment offsets in days, paying account, approval threshold as salary-class data) and their periods: the periods of a tax year are generated from the group's rules (monthly, twice a month, weekly and two-weekly from a chosen weekday), never overlap, change their cut-off and payment dates only before they open, and a closed period never changes (database trigger). An employee's pay group is an assignment field of an employment event (`payGroupId`, also at hire and transfer): a move out of a group with an unfinished run is refused, a group with current employees cannot be switched off. Payroll calendar (periods with days to the cut-off), reminder job `hrm.pay_cutoff_reminders` raising `hrm.pay.cutoff_due`, and the readiness checklist: a registry (`pay-readiness.json`) plus `IPayReadinessCheck` classes; bank data and structures are checked from existing data, attendance, loans and claims show "not available" until S1.31, S1.41 and S1.42 register theirs. Web: pay groups (engine list and form) and the calendar with generate, change dates and readiness (English and Urdu).
