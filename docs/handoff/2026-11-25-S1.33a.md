# 2026-11-25 S1.33a leave screens for employees and managers: apply, my leave, approval card, team calendar (Build items 1 to 5)
Lane (CR-082). Model: Sonnet 5.5, medium effort (class D, no fresh review). Rulings: CR-092 S1.33a detail a to f; risks R-169 to R-172.

## Built
An employee picks a leave type and dates (or part of a day), sees the days counted by the server rules, the balance before and after and every refusal in plain words, attaches a document
where the type needs one, and sends. My leave shows balances with "why this number" (the ledger entries) and the requests with cancel, change and withdraw as the S1.32b rules allow. HR applies on
behalf of an employee and the screen says who applied. The approval card in the shared inbox shows the days, balance after, document, who applied and a coverage warning (how many of the line manager's
team are away each day against `hrm.leave.coverage_warning_percent`, default 30; a warning only). The team calendar shows a month by person with leave (type colour token, waiting requests hatched), holidays,
weekly offs and a coverage row, filters, paging, keyboard navigation and a print stylesheet. English and Urdu.
