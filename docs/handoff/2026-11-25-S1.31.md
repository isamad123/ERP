## Built
A pay period's attendance and leave lock together, by payroll's calculation (`IAttendancePeriodLock.LockAsync` with the run, S1.35 calls it) or by HR after the
cut-off date with someone else's approval. Days not processed and open exceptions refuse the lock unless accepted, and the counts are recorded. The database then
refuses any change to the period's day results, penalties, counted leave days, payable overtime, approved regularizations and leave usage or encashment; accruals and
other entitlement postings still post (R-146). Punches that arrive later are kept and listed, never worked into the locked days. A period reopens only when the run that
locked it is cancelled before it is locked itself (one approved request cancels the run and reopens); an HR lock never reopens. A later change is an adjustment: its
impact is shown first (days, leave given back, overtime, penalties, pay periods touched), someone else approves, it is carried into the next open payroll period and the
locked days stay as they were; a leave cancellation gives the leave back by a reversal dated in that period. A day corrected again starts from its newest approved correction; one correction of a day waits at a time; a period with waiting or approved, not yet applied adjustments does not reopen. Events `hrm.attendance.locked`, `.reopened` (with the
employments) and `.adjustment_approved`; payroll reads `IAttendanceAdjustments`; the readiness item `attendance_locked` is real. Web: `/time/periods`, `/time/adjustments`.
