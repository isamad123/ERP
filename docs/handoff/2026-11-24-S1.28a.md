## Built

The `shift` policy type gives the weekly-off pattern (fixed days, rotating cycle, alternating weeks), the grace minutes (shown only) and an optional minimum rest. Rosters: create for a unit and period, assign a shift to named people or a group (unit with sub-units, grade, custom field, everyone; resolved to one line per person and day), copy forward, edit single days, a check list returned together, publication that tells each person once and a change after publication that needs a reason and tells again.
