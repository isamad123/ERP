## Built
Pay components (kind, payslip order, recurring or one-time, switched on or off) with effective-dated versions: method with what it needs (fixed, percent of components, formula, table lookup, per day, per hour, attendance-linked, manual), the pro-rata rule with the parameters of a custom rule, free statutory-flag codes and the tax-treatment label. Formulas and percentages are stored and name-checked (names exist, no cycle) through `IFormulaValidator`, never evaluated; a component with no version or with a missing statutory table shows as incomplete. Salary structures: the full component list from a date with order of evaluation, defaults and mandatory flags; the assign rule by grade, employee type or individual. Compensation: a structure with an amount per component from a date and a reason; a later one is a revision (the old row ends, never edited; the database refuses edits); a backdated one records the opened periods it reaches on the event and in `hrm.compensation.changed`. Structure and compensation changes wait for a second person while `hrm.pay.change_approval` is on. Amounts are salary-class data (hidden without the field class, not settable without edit access). Bonus definitions are the policy type `bonus`. The fresh Opus review (`docs/reviews/S1.34b-review.md`, 3 high, 6 medium, 9 low) is answered in its Response section: the highs and mediums are fixed or ruled.
