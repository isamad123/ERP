## Built
HR adds to the `attendance` policy how many late arrivals and early departures a month are allowed and what each further one costs: leave deducted
(a usage entry in the leave ledger), a pay deduction for payroll, or a warning. When the engine writes day results it plans the month again: the
fourth late day beyond three allowed gets its penalty; a corrected earlier day moves or cancels penalties (the record stays). Anyone in scope may ask
for a waiver with a reason; the line manager (HR when there is none) approves; the employee never does; the leave comes back and the record shows
"waived". Overtime per day type (working day, weekly off, holiday): multiplier, daily and monthly caps, minimum minutes, asked before the day or claimed
after it; the engine puts the minutes on the day and on an entry; the approval keeps to the caps; approved overtime is paid (through `IOvertimeInputs`)
or becomes compensatory off (event to the leave engine, earned once). Locked days refuse claims, waivers and approvals.
