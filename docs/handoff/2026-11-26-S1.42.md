## Built
An employee (or HR or a manager for them, recorded as the one who made it) makes a claim of lines: category, date, amount, currency, days or kilometres for per-diem and mileage, project and cost centre, a receipt. A line in
another currency is converted at the company's own rate table and the rate used stays on the line. Every save and the submit work out the policy flags: over the limit (the `expense` policy: a limit per line or a rate per day or
kilometre, optionally for one grade; none is shipped), receipt used before (same file checksum, amount and date in another live claim), receipt missing, and dated outside the claim window or in the future. A flag never stops a claim
unless its category says so. The claim goes to the line manager (the subject's manager, R-305), and to the finance role when its total reaches the threshold setting. Approvers record the amount accepted per line with a reason;
nobody decides their own claim. Approval pays through payroll (one payroll input of the approved total, consumed by the run through the provider `expenses`) or leaves the claim waiting for a payment batch (S1.40a), and writes
`hrm.expense_claim.approved` to the outbox. Amounts are hidden from everyone but the claimant, the approver while the claim waits, and salary-class readers.
