## Goal
HR controls how many people each department may have and which jobs are open. A customer that plans by positions sees
every post, who fills it and which are vacant; a customer that plans by numbers sets the sanctioned count per unit and
grade. Either way the strength report shows sanctioned, filled and vacant at a glance, nobody is placed in a post beyond
its budget without an approved exception, the org chart shows reporting lines as of any date, and approval routes can
send requests to a department head or to whoever holds a named position.

## What exists already (and stays)
- Tables `hrm.grades`, `hrm.positions` (effective-dated, cycle check on reporting position), `hrm.headcount_budgets`;
  generated reads; generated writes closed.
- Assignments reference `position_id` and `grade_id` (nullable); units have `head_position_ref`.
- The unit move endpoint and org chart components of S1.14b; `IApproverDirectory` with manager kinds (S1.20b).

## Found while preparing (needs a ruling)
1. **Department head in the headcount model.** The unit's head is a position; a headcount-based company has no positions
   (Q3).
2. **"Pay ranges" on grades** need money columns that the approved `hrm.grades` lacks (it has `defaults json`); pay bands are
   in the compensation design (S1.44) (Q4).
3. **What a unit move does to positions and people** is not specified beyond "consistently" (Q6).

## Build
### Part S1.22a: structure and rules
1. **Model per company (FR-HRM-POS-01).** Company setting `hrm.org.model` (`position` or `headcount`, default `headcount` for
   the Micro and SME size profiles, `position` otherwise, D-40); changing it later is allowed only while no assignment
   references a position (position to headcount) or after every active assignment has one (headcount to position).
2. **Grades (FR-HRM-POS-06, Q4).** Code, names, rank, defaults (leave entitlement key and policy defaults as named values
   the policy framework reads in S1.23); pay ranges stay with compensation (S1.44).
3. **Positions (FR-HRM-POS-02).** Create, change from a date (effective-dated rows, as S1.20 does for assignments), freeze,
   close; status planned, open, filled, frozen, closed derived from holders and budget where it can be (filled when holders'
   FTE reaches the budget); reporting position (no cycles).
4. **Headcount budgets (FR-HRM-POS-01).** Sanctioned count per unit and optional grade, effective-dated.
5. **Vacancy rule (BR-04, Q5).** An assignment event that places someone in a position whose filled FTE would exceed its
   budgeted FTE, or in a unit and grade beyond its sanctioned count, is refused unless an exception is approved: document
   type `hrm.strength_exception` through the workflow engine; the approved exception is recorded on the event.
6. **Approver directory (Q3).** Department head: the holder(s) on the date of the unit's head position (`head_position_ref`),
   walking up the unit hierarchy when the unit has none; position holders by position code in the company; both through the
   employments' user links.

### Part S1.22b: reports, chart, coordination
7. **Strength report (FR-HRM-POS-03).** Sanctioned, filled, vacant (and over) per unit, grade and location, as of a date;
   drill into the positions or people; export.
8. **Org chart by position (FR-HRM-POS-04).** Reporting lines of positions (or of managers, in the headcount model) as of a
   date or a future date: holder names and photos (as permitted), vacant boxes marked; filter by unit and location, search a
   person or position, zoom, keyboard navigation, print (page layout through the browser's print, Q7) and export (PDF
   through the document service, Q7).
9. **Unit change coordination (FR-HRM-POS-08, Q6).** When a unit moves (S1.14b), its positions and their holders move with it
   (no change to assignments, which reference the unit); when a unit is deactivated, the move is refused while active
   positions or assignments reference it; `hrm.org_structure_changed` raised for cost-centre mapping (outbox only, D-43).
10. **Screens.** Grades, positions (list, form, holders, history), headcount budgets, strength report, chart; position picker
    added to the employee change dialog (S1.21).

## Acceptance
- A position-model company: a position with budgeted FTE 1 filled by one person shows "filled"; placing a second person is
  refused with "over budget"; an approved strength exception lets it through and the event names the exception.
- A headcount-model company: sanctioned 5 analysts in Finance, 5 hired, the sixth refused without exception.
- The strength report for 1 January next year shows a future-dated position as planned and its budget counted then.
- An approval route with "department head" resolves the holder of the unit's head position on the document date; a unit
  without a head walks up to its parent's head; a requester who is the head goes to the next level (engine rule).
- A unit moved under another division keeps its positions and people; the chart as of the day before shows the old line.
- The chart prints on A4 landscape and exports to PDF in English and Urdu.
- Every screen: accessibility, keyboard, English and Urdu, three widths, states, screenshots.
- New endpoints pass the tenant-isolation suite and have permission and data-scope tests; tests cite the FR ids.

## Questions for the product owner
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts (a structure and rules, b reports and chart). Alternative: one.
- **Q1 Menu.** *Recommendation:* group "People": "Positions", "Headcount", "Grades", "Strength report", "Org chart".
- **Q2 Model default.** *Recommendation:* headcount for the Micro and SME profiles, position for Mid and Enterprise, set by
  the size profile preset (D-40), changeable per company with the rule of Build item 1. Alternative: headcount everywhere.
- **Q3 Department head.** *Recommendation:* the holder of the unit's head position; in a headcount company, head positions
  may still be created (one per unit, without budgets); without one, walk up the hierarchy; with none at the top, the route
  cannot resolve and the engine's escalation applies. Alternative: a "head employment" column on units (kernel data design
  change; kernel would point into HR, against D-10).
- **Q4 Grade pay ranges.** *Recommendation:* grades keep rank and named defaults here; pay ranges arrive with the salary
  bands of compensation (`hrm.salary_bands`, S1.44), linked to grades there. CR-078 records that FR-HRM-POS-06's pay-range part is delivered by
  S1.44. Alternative: add min and max money columns to grades now (data design change, then duplicated by bands).
- **Q5 Exceeding budget (BR-04).** *Recommendation:* refusal with a request for a strength exception (its own document type
  and route, default route: HR head); the exception covers one placement. Alternative: warning only.
- **Q6 Unit changes.** *Recommendation:* positions and people follow their unit on moves (they reference the unit, nothing to
  copy); deactivating a unit with active positions or people is refused until they are moved; a restructure tool that does
  several moves at once is 1.1 (FR-HRM-POS-05). Alternative: none.
- **Q7 Print and export.** *Recommendation:* print through the browser with a print stylesheet; export to PDF through the
  document service (S1.11c2) for up to 500 boxes; larger charts export as a CSV of reporting lines. Alternative: image
  export (needs a canvas library).
- **Q8 Photos on the chart.** *Recommendation:* shown only to people allowed to read the person's photo; otherwise initials.
- **Q9 Urdu texts.** *Recommendation:* drafted with the glossary and marked for review (R-062).
- **Q10 Change request CR-078.** *Recommendation:* approve: setting `hrm.org.model` (Q2); document type
  `hrm.strength_exception` (Q5); department head and position kinds of the approver directory (Q3); event
  `hrm.org_structure_changed` (Q6); pay ranges delivered with S1.44 (Q4); contracts register rows added at build.
