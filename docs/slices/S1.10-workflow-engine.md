## Goal
One approval engine for every module. A module (leave, regularization, overtime, profile change, letters, loans and
so on) hands a document to the engine; the engine finds the right people step by step, shows them the request in one
inbox, records every decision with who, when and why, and tells the module the outcome. Routes are data, not code: an
administrator can say "manager first, then HR; skip HR when the leave is three days or less", and a new version of a
route applies only to new requests. Nobody can approve their own request, not even through a delegate. A person on leave
can hand their approvals to someone else for a date range, and every decision shows "Ali for Sara". A request that
waits too long moves up to the next level; it is never approved automatically.

## Acceptance
- Spec 02 WFL AC, with a test document type (no purchase requisitions in 1.0): a request above a threshold routes to the
  department head then to a second role; it skips the department-head step when the department head is the requester;
  it escalates after two working days (weekend and a company holiday not counted); the full decision history is shown.
- A step whose condition is false is skipped and the history says why; a step with no condition always applies.
- The requester and the people the document is about can never decide a task of that request: not as assignee, not
  from a role queue, not as a delegate, not by reassignment; the database trigger refuses an assignee who is the
  requester even when the engine is bypassed. If every possible approver of the last step is excluded, the request goes
  to the next level; it is never approved without a decision by someone else (FR-XM-32).
- Approve, reject (reason required), return for correction (reason required), reassign (reason required), ask for
  information and answer each write a history row with actor, acting-for, time and comment; a returned request resubmitted
  by the module starts the route again on the same version.
- A new version activated while requests are running changes nothing for them; new requests use it.
- A route for a document type marked sensitive (payroll and the like) is activated only after a second administrator
  approves (maker-checker); activation is refused when a step cannot resolve any approver (BR of spec 02 section 14).
- Delegation: during its dates the delegate sees and decides the delegator's tasks within the delegation's document types
  and companies, the decision records "acting for", the delegate's own rights do not widen the delegator's; outside the
  dates, after revocation, or for a request the delegate raised, nothing is delegated. Delegations are not passed on.
- Escalation: a task past its time limit becomes escalated once, a new task goes to the next level, the original assignee
  can no longer decide it, and an event is published; a task with nobody above stays open and is listed as overdue for
  administrators; nothing is approved automatically.
- Two approvers deciding the same task at the same time, or claiming the same queue task, cannot both succeed.
- The inbox lists exactly the caller's own, delegated and claimable tasks across document types, with the filters and
  counts of Q12; bulk approval approves only tasks where it is allowed and reports each result.
- Disabling a user reports their open tasks; an administrator reassigns them in one call.
- The outcome reaches the module's handler in the same transaction as the final decision; events reach the outbox.
- Each new endpoint passes the tenant-isolation suite and has permission tests; an endpoint of an unlicensed document
  type's module is not served; tests cite the FR ids.

## S1.10a plan rulings (product owner, 2026-11-17; recorded in CR-042)
- **R1** Several people found for one step (position or another directory kind) each get a task; the first decision
  closes the others.
- **R2** A route needs at least one step without a condition, so every request reaches someone (FR-XM-32).
- **R3** With no active route (after deactivation), new requests of that type are refused until a route is activated
  again; running requests are unaffected; nothing is approved automatically.

## S1.10b plan rulings (product owner, 2026-11-17; recorded in CR-043)
The plan was approved with every recommendation:
- **P1** Company Admin reads requests and reassigns tasks within its companies: `wf.workflow_instances` gets the company
  locator in its access key (metadata only); no generic task or history read for Company Admin.
- **P2** A delegation covers tasks assigned to the delegator personally (including queue tasks they claimed); unclaimed
  queue tasks are not delegated.
- **P3** A task is late from the start of the day after its N-th working day (company time zone; the arrival day does not
  count). Example: arrives Thursday 15:00, two days, weekend Saturday and Sunday, Monday a holiday: late from Wednesday 00:00.
- **P4** Public and religious holidays of the company's holiday calendar stop the clock; optional and restricted do not.
- **P5** Delegation dates are whole days in the customer's time zone (`org.time_zone`).
- **P6** A question waiting when a task escalates (or is reassigned) moves to the new task.
- **P7** Disabling or archiving a user answers 200 `{openWorkflowTasks: n}`; other status changes answer 204 as before.

## Questions as put to the product owner

- **Q0 One slice or two.** *Recommendation:* one slice file, two sessions and two pull requests, as S1.09:
  **S1.10a** document types, routes and versions, resolution, conditions, the engine with its decisions, self-approval
  and a basic inbox (Build items 1 to 5 and the inbox list); **S1.10b** delegation, escalation, queue claim, bulk
  approval, inbox filters and counts, administration (items 6 to 10). Alternative: one session.
- **Q1 Escalation in 1.0 (finding 1).** *Recommendation:* FR-XM-32 is a 1.0 baseline rule and outranks the register tag of
  KRN-WFL-06, so 1.0 builds what FR-XM-32 needs: every step has a time limit, and when it passes the task moves to the
  next level (Q9), never auto-approved. The rest of KRN-WFL-06 stays Later: reminders before the limit, priority, and
  several service levels per step. Recorded in CR-041 so the register and FR-XM-32 agree.
- **Q2 How routes are edited in 1.0 (finding 2).** *Recommendation:* routes are data in the route format of this slice.
  Each module ships default routes (for example leave: line manager, then HR when more than three days); the API lets an
  administrator copy a route, change steps, conditions, approvers and time limits, check and activate it. A simple
  **step-list editor screen** (a form listing steps top to bottom, no canvas) is added to **S1.15** (settings centre),
  which edits through this API. The visual canvas stays Later (ADM-STU-40). Alternative: no screen in 1.0; only default
  routes and API (the provider edits for the customer).
- **Q3 What "conditional" means in 1.0.** D-33 says "linear and conditional approvals"; KRN-WFL-04 (branching) is 1.1.
  *Recommendation:* a route is a list of steps run top to bottom; each step may carry a condition and is skipped when
  the condition is false (KRN-WFL-03 "choose routes and skip steps"). No branches that rejoin, no parallel steps.
- **Q4 Who can be an approver (KRN-WFL-02, finding 3).** *Recommendation:* in 1.0:
  - **named user**; **role** (any active holder of the role whose company scope covers the request's company; the task
    sits in the role's queue until one of them **claims** it, and can be released back); **user field of the document**
    (for example "the HR business partner" field the module fills in).
  - **line manager, manager's manager (any number of levels up), department head, position holder**: through a kernel
    contract `IApproverDirectory` that HR implements in S1.20 (manager, department head) and S1.22 (position). Until a
    module provides it, a route that uses these kinds cannot be activated and says why. Tests use a test-only directory.
  - Each step may name a **fallback** (role or user) used when the kind finds nobody.
  - **Expression** resolution and **partner field** are not built: conditions on steps (Q5) cover choosing approvers by
    amount or department, free expressions come with the rules engine (Phase 2) and partners with Phase 2 modules.
  Recorded as the 1.0 reading of KRN-WFL-02.
- **Q5 How conditions are written (finding 5).** *Recommendation:* structured conditions, not a free-text expression
  language: `field`, `operator` (equals, not equals, greater, greater or equal, less, less or equal, in list, not in
  list, is empty, is not empty), `value`, combined with all-of / any-of. Fields are the typed fields the document type
  declares (number, money, date, text, choice, yes/no, company, org unit). The values are frozen when the request starts
  (the approved `context` column), so a later edit of the document never changes its route. No expression library is
  added (keeps the spec 02 section 26 choice open for Phase 2, which can add expressions behind the same shape).
- **Q6 Nobody approves their own request (KRN-WFL-09, HRM-ROL-04, FR-XM-32).** *Recommendation:*
  - Excluded from every task of a request: the requester and **the people the document is about** (an employee whose
    leave a manager entered on their behalf cannot approve it either). The module names them when it starts the request
    (new column `subject_user_ids`, CR-041).
  - The database trigger (the S1.10 placeholder) refuses an assignee who is the requester or a subject; the engine also
    refuses a claim, a delegated decision and a reassignment to an excluded person.
  - When a step resolves only to excluded people: if a later step will be decided by someone else, the step is
    **skipped** and the history says why (spec 02 AC "skips a step when the approver is the requester"); if it is the
    last deciding step, it goes **one level up** (the excluded person's manager, else the step's fallback). If nobody
    can be found the request waits in the administrators' "cannot assign" list. A request is never completed without at
    least one decision by someone else.
- **Q7 Decisions (KRN-WFL-07, finding 4).** *Recommendation:*
  - **Approve** (comment optional); **reject** (reason required; request ends rejected); **return for correction**
    (reason required; the request becomes **returned**, a new status in CR-041; the module lets the requester correct the
    document and resubmit, and the route **starts again from the first step** on the same version, history kept);
  - **reassign** (reason required; to an eligible person; by the assignee, or by an administrator with the reassign
    permission); **ask for information** (a question to the requester; the task stays with the approver and is marked
    waiting; the requester answers from "my requests"; the time limit keeps running);
  - the requester can **withdraw** a running or returned request (status cancelled);
  - every action writes `wf.workflow_history` (append-only) with actor, acting-for, time, comment.
  - **Attachments** on decisions come with file storage in S1.11 (kernel documents), which adds them to these actions.
- **Q8 Delegation and out-of-office (KRN-WFL-05, HRM-ROL-05, HRM-SSP-07, ADM-ROL-09).** *Recommendation:*
  - A user creates their own delegation (self-service); an administrator with the permission creates one for anyone.
    Fields: delegate, from and to (dates in the delegator's time zone, start of the first day to end of the last day),
    document types and companies (empty = all), reason. Limits: delegate must be an active user and not the delegator;
    at most `wf.delegation.max_days` days (setting, default 90); no overlapping delegations of the same documents to two
    people; revoke at any time; it ends by itself after the last day (no job needed).
  - It applies at once to open and new tasks: the delegate sees the delegator's tasks in their inbox while the
    delegation is in force; the delegator can still decide them too.
  - The delegate acts with the **delegator's** right to decide, not their own (HRM-ROL-05); the history records
    "acted by the delegate, for the delegator". A delegate never decides a request they raised or are the subject of.
  - Not passed on: tasks the delegate holds only through a delegation are not delegated further.
  - Creating, changing and revoking a delegation is in the change log.
- **Q9 Time limits and escalation (FR-XM-32, finding 6).** *Recommendation:*
  - Every step has a time limit in **working days** (route value; default setting `wf.escalation.default_working_days` =
    **2**, as in the spec 02 AC). Working days skip the company's weekly off days (new company setting
    `org.weekly_off_days`, default **Saturday and Sunday**, to be confirmed for Pakistan customers) and the holidays of
    the company's holiday calendar. Time runs in the company's time zone.
  - When the limit passes, the task becomes **escalated** (it can no longer be decided) and a new task goes to the next
    level: the current approver's manager (`IApproverDirectory`), else the step's escalation role or user. The new task
    gets a fresh time limit and may escalate again, up to 3 levels; after that, or when nobody is above, the task stays
    open and is listed as overdue for administrators.
  - Never auto-approve, never auto-reject.
  - Checked by a small worker loop every minute (the S1.09b pattern) until S1.11 moves it into jobs. Event
    `kernel.workflow.task_escalated` lets S1.11 send the escalation notice (HRM-NTF-11).
- **Q10 Versions and activation (KRN-WFL-10, spec 02 BR).** *Recommendation:* drafts can be edited; an active version is
  frozen; activating a new version retires the old one; running requests finish on the version they started with; no
  migration of running requests in 1.0 (ADM-STU-43 is Later). One active route per document type for the whole
  customer; company differences are conditions on the company field (no company column, no CR). Activation check: every
  step has an approver kind available for this customer, named users and roles exist and are active, a role step's
  role has at least one holder, conditions use declared fields. Document types marked sensitive (payroll, payments,
  posting) need maker-checker to activate (the S1.06 mechanism, new action type `wf.activate_version`).
- **Q11 How a module uses the engine.** *Recommendation:* the module starts a request inside its own save (same
  database transaction) through a kernel contract `IWorkflowService.StartAsync(documentType, documentId, company,
  subjects, values)`. When the request ends (approved, rejected, returned, cancelled) the engine calls the module's
  outcome handler (a contract each document type implements) **in the same transaction** as the final decision, so the
  document's status and the request never disagree; it also writes `kernel.workflow.*` events to the outbox for others
  (S1.11 notifications). Inbox rows show the title and link the module declares; the details screen is the module's own.
  Document types of an unlicensed module are not offered and their tasks are not shown.
- **Q12 Inbox and my requests (KRN-WFL-08, HRM-SSP-02).** *Recommendation:*
  - Inbox: tasks assigned to me, delegated to me (marked "for Sara"), and queue tasks I may claim; filters by module,
    document type, company, requester, step, waiting-for-information, overdue, received between dates; sort by received
    or due; counts per document type for menus and badges.
  - **Bulk approval** only where the document type and the step allow it, at most `wf.bulk_approval.max_items` (default
    100) per call, each task checked and decided on its own, a result per task.
  - "My requests": requests I raised, with status, current step and approver, questions waiting for my answer.
  - Pending maker-checker requests stay on their own S1.06 endpoints; the inbox counts them in a separate "approvals of
    changes" group so administrators see one total. Alternative: leave them out of the inbox.
  - The inbox screen itself is S1.12 ("first approval screen").
- **Q13 Administration (ADM-USR-03, FR-XM-29).** *Recommendation:* a monitor of running requests (by document type,
  status, overdue, cannot-assign); cancel a request with reason; reassign any task with reason; list a user's open tasks
  and reassign them all to one person (disabling a user answers with their open-task count so the screen can prompt,
  ADM-USR-03); a kernel query "open approvals of a user" for final settlement (FR-XM-29, used by S1.25).
- **Q14 Endpoints, permissions and roles.** *Recommendation:*
  - Routes: `GET /api/wf/document-types`, `GET /api/wf/definitions`, `GET /api/wf/definitions/{id}` (versions),
    `POST /api/wf/definitions/{id}/versions` (new draft, copy of a version or posted body), `PUT /api/wf/versions/{id}`
    (edit draft), `POST /api/wf/versions/{id}/check`, `POST /api/wf/versions/{id}/activate` (202 when maker-checker),
    `POST /api/wf/definitions/{id}/deactivate`.
  - Inbox: `GET /api/wf/inbox`, `GET /api/wf/inbox/counts`, `GET /api/wf/tasks/{id}` (with request and history),
    `POST /api/wf/tasks/{id}/approve|reject|return|reassign|ask|claim|release`, `POST /api/wf/tasks/bulk-approve`.
  - Requester: `GET /api/wf/my-requests`, `POST /api/wf/instances/{id}/answer`, `POST /api/wf/instances/{id}/withdraw`.
  - Administration: `GET /api/wf/instances`, `GET /api/wf/instances/{id}`, `POST /api/wf/instances/{id}/cancel`,
    `GET /api/wf/users/{id}/open-tasks`, `POST /api/wf/users/{id}/reassign-open-tasks`.
  - Delegation: `GET`/`POST /api/iam/me/delegations`, `POST /api/iam/me/delegations/{id}/revoke`; administrators
    `GET`/`POST /api/iam/delegations`, `POST /api/iam/delegations/{id}/revoke`.
