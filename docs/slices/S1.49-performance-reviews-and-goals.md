## Goal
HR opens a review cycle for the right people, employees and managers set weighted goals that cascade from the company down, progress is updated through the year, and at review time each person gets the right reviews (self, manager, skip-level, peers). Scores are signed off and become suggestions for increments and promotions.

## Build
### Part S1.49a
1. **Cycles (PRF-01).** Type, period, form, eligibility by attribute rules (S1.23 pattern), phases (goal setting, self review, manager review, calibration input, sign-off) with deadlines; open and close; participants generated and editable with reasons; reminders as events.
2. **Goals (PRF-02).** Frameworks KPI, OKR, scorecard; parent-child cascade with weight sums checked; target, actual, due date; approval of goals by manager through workflow; edits after approval versioned.
3. **Progress.** Updates with comments and evidence; percentage computed in decimal; history append-only.
4. **Screens.** Cycle list and set-up, my goals, team goals, goal tree; English and Urdu.
### Part S1.49b
5. **Review assignments (PRF-04, Q2).** Per participant: self, manager, skip-level, peers (chosen by employee, approved by manager, or assigned by HR), 360 raters; each uses the cycle form (S1.48).
6. **Anonymity (PRF-04, CR-113).** Peer and 360 results are shown only as aggregates (average scores and comments without names) when at least the minimum
   number of respondents (setting, no default) answered; below it nothing from those raters is shown, neither scores nor comments. For anonymous assignments the
   rater is never returned: the generated reads of `hrm.review_assignments` and `hrm.form_responses` are closed (`api_actions: []`) and replaced by hand-written reads
   that omit `reviewer_employment_id` and `respondent_employment_id` when `is_anonymous`; both columns are field class restricted, so audit search and exports mask them.
7. **Sign-off and outcomes (PRF-09).** Manager and employee sign-off, HR final; overall rating by the form's weights; `hrm.review.completed` event; outcome suggestions list for S1.44a increment proposals with eligibility.
8. **Screens.** My reviews, team reviews, rater inbox, results view with field security (ratings hidden from others by default).

## Acceptance
- PRF-01: eligibility rules pick the right participants; a phase deadline passing raises reminders; closing stops edits.
- PRF-02: weights of child goals sum to the parent's weight or the save is refused; progress history cannot be edited.
- PRF-04: with a minimum of 3 and only 2 peer answers, scores and comments are withheld; raters' identities never appear in API, export or audit search for anonymous
  types (a test reads every endpoint, export and the audit search for a known rater id).
- PRF-09: completed reviews appear as suggestions in the increment cycle with eligibility respected.
- Tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; permission tests; FR ids in tests.

## Questions (recommendations are rulings, CR-083)
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts.
- **Q1 Rating scales.** *Recommendation:* customer-defined in the form; no default.
- **Q2 Peer selection.** *Recommendation:* employee proposes, manager approves, HR may override.
- **Q3 Minimum respondents.** *Recommendation:* setting `hrm.prf.min_respondents`, no default; unset means anonymous results are never shown.
- **Q4 Visibility.** *Recommendation:* ratings are field class sensitive personal, visible to the employee after sign-off, the manager and HR.
- **Q5 Continuous cycles.** *Recommendation:* modelled as repeating short cycles; no separate engine.
- **Q6 Goal edits.** *Recommendation:* after approval a new version needs manager approval.
- **Q7 Change request CR-104.** *Recommendation:* approve: document types `hrm.goal`, `hrm.review_signoff`; events `hrm.review.completed`, `hrm.review.phase_due`; setting `hrm.prf.min_respondents`; field classes for ratings; expand-only.
