# S1.50 Learning and development: courses, sessions, enrolments, records, mandatory training, certifications, bonds
Status: APPROVED by the standing ruling (CR-083): every recommendation (Q0 to Q6) is the ruling; CR-105 approved. Reserved numbers: CR-105, R-197 to R-198.
Class: C        Model: **Sonnet 5.5, medium effort**. No fresh review.
Parts (Q0): **S1.50a** catalogue, sessions, enrolment with approval and budget, learning records (items 1 to 4); **S1.50b** mandatory training, certifications and expiry, bonds and recovery (items 5 to 8). One session and pull request per part.
Requirements (all release 1.0, D-49): FR-HRM-LND-01 (catalogue: internal, external, online; description, provider, duration, cost, prerequisites, competencies), LND-02 (sessions: schedule, venue, trainer, capacity, waitlist; enrolment or nomination with approval and budget check), LND-03 (attendance, assessments, certificates, feedback in the learning record),
LND-04 (mandatory training by role, location or rule with due dates, reminders, compliance reports), LND-05 (certifications and licences with expiry tracking and renewal reminders, linked to required training), LND-06 (training bonds: obligation period and recovery terms flowing into final settlement), GHR-09, FR-XM-29 (bond balance in settlement, S1.43), FR-XM-32.
Not built here: online course hosting and content (links only), assessments engine beyond a recorded score, e-learning standards, budgets by cost centre beyond a simple budget per period (Q3).
Decisions: D-10, D-29, D-35, D-39, D-45, D-49. Spec to read (only these): `docs/specs/06a-…` section 20 (LND lines); `hrm-talent.yaml` `hrm.courses`, `hrm.course_sessions`, `hrm.session_enrollments`, `hrm.learning_records`, `hrm.mandatory_training_rules`, `hrm.training_requirements`, `hrm.training_bonds`; S1.23 (rules), S1.43 (settlement contract), S1.11c1 (certificates files), S1.11b.
Depends on: S1.20b, S1.23a, S1.10, S1.11b, S1.11c1; bonds also S1.43.

## Goal
HR publishes courses and sessions, employees enrol or are nominated with the right approvals, attendance and results are recorded in each person's learning record, required training is tracked with due dates and reminders, certifications warn before expiry, and a training bond that must be repaid on early exit is carried into the final settlement.

## Build
### Part S1.50a
1. **Catalogue (LND-01).** Courses with delivery type, provider, duration, cost and currency, prerequisites, competencies, certification awarded and validity.
2. **Sessions (LND-02).** Schedule, venue, trainer, capacity; waitlist promotes in order when a seat frees; cancel session notifies enrolled.
3. **Enrolment (LND-02, Q3).** Self-enrolment or nomination by manager; approval through workflow; budget check against the unit's training budget (a simple per-period amount setting) with an override approval; prerequisites enforced.
4. **Learning record (LND-03).** Attendance, assessment score, pass or fail, certificate file, feedback; append-only with corrections as new rows; screens: catalogue, my learning, session roster, manager team view; English and Urdu.
### Part S1.50b
5. **Mandatory training (LND-04).** Rules by role, location, grade or attribute (S1.23 pattern) create requirements per employee with due dates; reminders; compliance report by unit; overdue appears in the HR workspace.
6. **Certifications (LND-05).** Awarded certificates and licences with expiry; renewal reminders `hrm.certification.expiring`; link to the course that renews.
7. **Bonds (LND-06, Q4).** Bond per enrolment: amount, obligation until, recovery rule (full, pro-rata by months remaining as a customer-chosen rule); outstanding bond exposed to the final settlement through `ISettlementCalculator` (S1.43); waiver by approval.
8. **Reports.** Training hours and cost by unit, compliance, expiring certifications.

## Acceptance
- LND-02: capacity 10 with 12 enrolments gives 2 on the waitlist; a cancellation promotes the first; a missing prerequisite refuses enrolment; budget overrun needs approval.
- LND-04/05: a role-based rule creates requirements for the right people; reminders fire before due and expiry dates.
- LND-06: an employee leaving during the obligation period shows the pro-rata bond in the settlement draft; waiver needs approval.
- Tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; permission tests; FR ids in tests.

## Out of scope
See "Not built here".

## Questions (recommendations are rulings, CR-083)
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts.
- **Q1 Online courses.** *Recommendation:* external link and completion entered or uploaded; no content hosting.
- **Q2 Competencies.** *Recommendation:* a simple option list shared with S1.49 forms.
- **Q3 Budget.** *Recommendation:* setting per unit and period, warning and override only.
- **Q4 Bond recovery.** *Recommendation:* rules named `full`, `pro_rata_months`, `none`, parameters customer values.
- **Q5 Reminders.** *Recommendation:* lead times in settings, events `hrm.training.due`, `hrm.certification.expiring`.
- **Q6 Change request CR-105.** *Recommendation:* approve: document types `hrm.enrolment`, `hrm.bond_waiver`; settings `hrm.lnd.budget_per_unit`; option list `hrm.competencies`; events above; expand-only.
