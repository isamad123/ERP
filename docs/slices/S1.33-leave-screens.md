## Goal
An employee picks a leave type and dates, sees at once how many days it will take, what the balance will be afterwards and why (holidays,
weekends, sandwich rule), attaches a document if the type asks for one, and sends it. They see their balances, the ledger behind each, their
requests, and can cancel or change approved leave. A manager sees on one calendar who in the team is away, remote or on duty, with a warning
when many are out together. HR sets up leave types and rules, grants and corrects balances with a reason, loads opening balances when going
live, applies for someone who cannot, and runs the year end with a preview before confirming.

## What exists already (and stays)
Leave engine and its API (S1.32: types, `leave` policies, ledger, balances as of a date, requests with checks, cancellation and modification,
comp-off, year-end preview, confirm and rollback, recompute); generic policy screens with schema-driven parameter forms and the resolution
view (S1.23a); approvals screen (S1.12d); portal hosts (S1.26); duty requests (S1.30a); import centre (S1.16a).

## Found while preparing (needs a ruling)
1. **Balance impact before submitting** needs a dry run of the request checks without saving (Q2).
2. **Coverage warnings** have no threshold in 1.0 (LVE-09's limits are 1.1) (Q3).
3. **Opening balances at go-live** are not named by any slice, yet every customer needs them (Q4).
4. **BR-07 approver** for adjustments is "where policy requires" with no policy field (Q5).

## Build
### Part S1.33a: employee and manager
1. **Apply (LVE-04, LVE-16).** Form: leave type (only types with a policy that applies, S1.32 item 2), dates, first or second half, hours for
   hourly types, reason, attachments (required when the type says; S1.24a limits); live preview panel (item 2) with day list, counted days,
   balance before and after, warnings and refusals in plain language; submit through the S1.32 request endpoint.
2. **Preview endpoint (Q2).** `POST /api/hrm/leave/requests/preview` runs the S1.32 request checks and day counting in a read-only transaction and
   returns days, balance before and after, the rules applied (sandwich, counting, notice, consecutive days) and any refusal; nothing is written.
3. **My leave (LVE-06, LVE-10).** Balances per type as of today and as of a chosen date; ledger per type and leave year with each entry's kind,
   quantity, date, source document link, expiry; requests with status, cancel (pending or approved) and modify (opens the form linked to the
   original, S1.32 item 9); comp-off earned with expiry.
4. **Team calendar (LVE-05, LVE-15, Q3).** Month and week views of the manager's team (data scope): approved and pending leave, work from home
   and on duty (S1.30a), holidays and weekly off; per day the count away of the team; a warning badge when the setting
   `hrm.leave.coverage_warn_percent` is set and exceeded; the approval task shows the same calendar strip for the requested dates.
5. **Approvals fields.** `workflow.json` fields for the S1.32 leave document type (`hrm.leave_request` unless the S1.32b handoff names
   another; type, dates, days, balance after, team away on those days) so the S1.12d
   approvals screen shows them; no separate approval screen.
6. **Portal panels (SSP-01, -02).** Employee panel "My leave" (balances and next leave) and manager widget "Team away this week" registered in
   `web/apps/web/src/modules/hrm.ts` (S1.26 hosts).
### Part S1.33b: HR administration
7. **Leave types (LVE-01).** Engine list and form over the S1.32 type endpoints (code, names in English and Urdu, paid, counting, unit, document
   requirement, comp-off, deduction flag, source shown; pack types editable as tenant overrides, retire not delete).
8. **Leave policies (LVE-03).** The S1.23 policy screens for type `leave`, with English and Urdu labels and help for every field of the S1.32
   schema; the resolution view answers "which leave policy applies to this employee for this type on this date".
9. **Grants and adjustments (BR-07, Q5).** HR posts a manual grant or adjustment with a reason (required); when the company setting
   `hrm.leave.adjustment_approval` is on (default on), the entry waits for a second person through the S1.06 maker-checker and is posted on
   approval; never by the requester.
10. **Opening balances (Q4).** Import type `hrm.leave_opening_balances` (employee number, leave type code, leave year, quantity, expiry) through the
    import centre: preview, errors, load as `opening` ledger entries; a second load for the same employee, type and year is refused.
11. **On behalf (LVE-05, GHR-09).** HR applies, cancels or modifies for an employee from the employee's record; the acting user is recorded and
    shown; the employee is notified.
12. **Jobs screens (LVE-02, LVE-11, LVE-14).** Run accrual for a period (S1.32 job, progress from the job centre); year-end: start a preview,
    see the per-employee carry-forward, lapse and encashment units, confirm (maker-checker setting of S1.32) or roll back; recompute: choose
    employees or a unit, see differences with the entry that explains each, and post an adjustment from a difference (goes through item 9).

## Acceptance
- LVE-04 (AC of section 10): a mid-year joiner applies for two days around a public holiday under a sandwich rule and the preview shows three
  counted days, the holiday named as sandwiched, and the balance after; nothing is saved by the preview.
- LVE-16: a type requiring a document refuses submission without one; a file over the size limit is refused with the limit shown.
- LVE-06: cancelling approved leave shows the restored balance; modifying creates a linked request.
- LVE-15: the manager sees two of five team members away on a date with the warning when the setting is 30 percent; a user outside the scope
  sees nobody.
- BR-07: an adjustment without a reason is refused; with the setting on it waits for a second person and the requester cannot approve it.
- Opening balances: 200 rows load with a preview; a repeated row is refused.
- LVE-11, LVE-14: preview, confirm and rollback screens drive the S1.32 jobs; a planted difference is listed with its cause.
- Browser tests (`Suite=WebE2E`): apply with preview, manager approves from approvals, cancel; HR loads opening balances and previews year-end;
  English and Urdu screenshots; axe checks; route chunks within budget.
- Tenant-isolation test for every new endpoint; FR ids in test names.

## Questions (each recommendation is the ruling, CR-083)
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts as above. Alternative: one (over the CR-047 size).
- **Q1 Menu.** *Recommendation:* "Me": My leave, Apply for leave; "Team": Team calendar; "Leave" (HR): Leave types, Leave policies, Balances and
  adjustments, Opening balances, Year end, Recompute. Alternative: one "Leave" page with tabs.
- **Q2 Preview.** *Recommendation:* a read-only preview endpoint over the same S1.32 services (one code path for checks). Alternative: compute in the
  browser (duplicates the rules).
- **Q3 Coverage warning.** *Recommendation:* company setting `hrm.leave.coverage_warn_percent` with no default (empty means no warning); the count
  is always shown. Alternative: a product default (an invented rule).
- **Q4 Opening balances.** *Recommendation:* import type here (go-live needs it; S1.32's ledger kind `opening` exists). Alternative: hand entry only.
- **Q5 Adjustment approval.** *Recommendation:* company setting `hrm.leave.adjustment_approval` (default on) using maker-checker. Alternative: a policy
  field per leave type (more set-up).
- **Q6 Policy form.** *Recommendation:* the generic S1.23 schema form with leave-specific labels and help; no separate leave policy editor.
  Alternative: a dedicated editor (more code, same data).
- **Q7 Calendar sources.** *Recommendation:* leave, duty kinds from S1.30 (work from home shown as "working remotely", on duty and official visit as
  "on duty"), holidays and weekly off from S1.28. Alternative: leave only.
- **Q8 Notifications.** *Recommendation:* S1.32's decision events; plus `hrm.leave.adjustment_posted` to the employee. Alternative: none.
- **Q9 Change request CR-092.** *Recommendation:* approve: preview endpoint; settings `hrm.leave.coverage_warn_percent`, `hrm.leave.adjustment_approval`;
  import type `hrm.leave_opening_balances`; event `hrm.leave.adjustment_posted`; `workflow.json` fields for `hrm.leave_request`; portal panel and widget.
  Expand-only; no table.
