Depends on: S1.35b, S1.38, S1.29b, S1.27b, S1.51, S1.40b.

## Goal
Prove, with numbers, that a customer with 50,000 employees can run payroll, process attendance and read reports without one customer slowing another, and fix what the proof exposes.

## Build
1. **Data generator.** Deterministic synthetic tenant at 50,000 employees (units, grades, structures with ten components, a month of punches on three shifts, leave, loans) generated through the real APIs and generators where possible, otherwise bulk SQL kept in `tests/Load`; runs in the benchmark CI job manually triggered (not on every PR).
2. **Scenarios.** Payroll for 10,000 and for 50,000 (chunk and worker settings tuned); attendance processing for 500 employees on three shifts for a month, and for the whole 50,000 nightly run; 10,000 punches in 15 minutes ingestion; dashboards and the 10 slowest list and report queries; approvals inbox at 1,000 open tasks.
3. **Isolation under load (Q2).** Two tenants on one server: tenant A runs the payroll load while tenant B's latency and the tenant-isolation suite run unchanged; B's p95 stays within the limit and no cross-tenant data appears.
4. **Findings and fixes.** Each miss is a defect with a number: indexes, chunk size, connection pool, query shape; fixes go in this slice when small, otherwise as new slice items with an owner.
5. **Report.** `docs/performance/release-1.0-load-report.md` with environment (CPU, memory, PostgreSQL settings), method, results table against each target, and the tuning settings that produced them.

## Acceptance
- GHR-01 targets met on the recorded environment, or each miss is written up with the decision of the product owner.
- Isolation test: no cross-tenant read under load; B's p95 within 1.5 times its idle value (Q3).
- The load harness is re-runnable and deterministic (same seed, same data hash).
- Fresh Opus review; FR ids in test names where tests exist.
