# S1.41 Loans and advances with payroll recovery
Status: APPROVED by the standing ruling (CR-083): every recommendation (Q0 to Q6) is the ruling; CR-097 approved. Reserved numbers: CR-097, R-181 to R-182.
Class: C        Model: **Sonnet 5.5, medium effort**. No fresh review (money moves only through the reviewed payroll engine; the loan ledger is checked by reconciliation tests).
Requirements (all release 1.0): FR-HRM-LON-01 (types: eligibility by tenure, grade, existing loans; maximum fixed or multiple of salary; interest none, flat or reducing; maximum tenure), LON-02 (application through workflow with
finance review and optional guarantor), LON-03 (installment schedule on approval; recovery flows into payroll), LON-05 (outstanding loans appear in clearance and final settlement; the clearance contract is S1.25's, the settlement use S1.43),
LON-07 (disbursement publishes `LoanDisbursed`; recovery reduces the receivable; loan ledger reconciles to the Finance control account **when Finance exists**: here an export and a reconciliation report only, D-43), GHR-06, GHR-09, FR-XM-32, FR-XM-29 (open loans block final settlement, S1.43).
Not built here: payment of the disbursement (a payment batch of S1.40 or manual, Q3); early settlement through final settlement (S1.43); loans against provident fund (S1.44); mobile (1.1).
Decisions: D-10, D-29, D-35, D-39, D-43, D-45. Spec to read (only these): `docs/specs/06a-…` section 15 (LON lines); `hrm-pay.yaml` `hrm.loan_types`, `hrm.loans`, `hrm.loan_installments`, `hrm.loan_transactions`; S1.35 handoff (`IPayInputProvider`), S1.34 (components), S1.23 (policy types), S1.10 (workflow).
Depends on: S1.35a, S1.34b, S1.20b, S1.23a, S1.10, S1.40a (payment lines for disbursement).

## Goal
Staff apply for a loan or salary advance; the line manager, finance and any guarantor approve; the system builds the installment schedule and takes each installment out of salary automatically, showing the employee and HR the balance at any time.

## Build
1. **Types and policy (LON-01, Q1).** Loan type with deduction component and policy type `loan` (schema: eligibility rules, maximum amount fixed or salary multiple, interest method, maximum installments); no default numbers.
2. **Application (LON-02).** Employee or HR on behalf; checks eligibility and shows the schedule preview; workflow route employee, manager, finance, with guarantor consent step when the type requires one; nobody approves their own.
3. **Schedule and disbursement (LON-03, LON-07).** On approval: installments by method (none, flat, reducing balance on `numeric(19,4)` with the rounding of S1.35; last installment absorbs the remainder); disbursement recorded with date and payment reference; event `hrm.loan.disbursed`.
4. **Recovery (LON-03).** Implements `IPayInputProvider`: due installments of the period become deductions; the result line id is stored on the installment; skipped (no pay) installments carry forward by the type's rule (Q4); the ledger (`loan_transactions`) records every movement, append-only.
5. **Balance and early repayment.** Balance and schedule screens; extra repayment or full settlement through a manual receipt entry (approval); `GET` outstanding for the clearance contract (LON-05).
6. **Reconciliation report (LON-07).** Loan ledger total against disbursed less recovered per company; export file for Finance later.
7. **Screens.** Types, applications, my loans, approvals cards, outstanding report; English and Urdu.

## Acceptance
- A 12-installment reducing-balance loan sums to the principal exactly; each payroll run recovers the right installment; an employee with no pay that month carries it forward.
- Ineligible applicant gets the reason; a guarantor step is required when set; own approval refused.
- Outstanding loan appears in the clearance query; ledger entries cannot be edited (database).
- GHR-06: amounts hidden without permission. Tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; FR ids in tests.

## Out of scope
See "Not built here".

## Questions (recommendations are rulings, CR-083)
- **Q0 Single part.** *Recommendation:* one part.
- **Q1 Policy type.** *Recommendation:* `loan` through S1.23, no default numbers.
- **Q2 Interest.** *Recommendation:* none, flat or reducing balance only; rate as an annual percentage entered by the customer.
- **Q3 Disbursement payment.** *Recommendation:* recorded manually with a reference now; S1.40 batches may carry it later.
- **Q4 Missed installments.** *Recommendation:* carry forward to the next period, capped by a setting for the share of net pay recoverable per period (customer value, no statutory number).
- **Q5 Events.** *Recommendation:* `hrm.loan.disbursed`, `hrm.loan.installment_recovered` in the outbox for Finance.
- **Q6 Change request CR-097.** *Recommendation:* approve: policy type `loan`; provider for payroll; settings `hrm.loan.max_recovery_percent`; events above; document type `hrm.loan_application` in `workflow.json`; expand-only.
