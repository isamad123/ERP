## Goal
Make the product trustworthy in Urdu. Every text a person can see, on screens, in e-mails and in documents, exists in
English and Urdu; the Urdu uses one agreed set of terms and has been read by a native speaker; every screen built so far
reads correctly right to left; and checks in CI stop a later slice from adding an untranslated text or a layout that
breaks in Urdu.

## What exists already (and stays)
- Web catalogues `web/packages/i18n/messages/{en,ur}.json` (ICU), label catalogues `labels/<module>.ur.json` with the check
  `modules/labels.test.ts` (Urdu labels of tables on screens), server catalogues `*/Localization/*.{en,ur}.json` collected at
  build time, a pseudo-locale used in tests, number and date formatting with the digit and grouping settings
  (`loc.number.digits`, `loc.number.grouping`, S1.12 Q6), Noto Sans and Noto Naskh Arabic on screens, Nastaliq in PDFs.
- Every Urdu text so far is drafted by Claude and marked for review (R-062); a glossary was started in S1.12.
- Browser tests take screenshots in both languages (CI artifact `web-screenshots`).

## Build
### Part S1.19a: languages, completeness, glossary, review
1. **Language management (FR-ADM-LOC-01, Q5).** Settings `loc.languages.enabled` (English, Urdu in 1.0), `loc.language.default`
   and `loc.language.fallback` at customer and company layers; a "Languages" screen in the settings centre showing
   completeness per module and per catalogue (web, server, labels, notifications, documents) for each language.
2. **Completeness gate (Q4).** One check run in CI over every catalogue: every key has English and Urdu; ICU placeholders
   and plural forms match between languages; every notification event and every product document template has both
   languages; label keys of every table on a screen have Urdu (extends `labels.test.ts`); the build fails on a gap.
3. **Glossary (FR-UX-CNT-06, FR-UX-I18-14).** `docs/glossary/ur.md` (English term, Urdu term, notes, where used) for the HR,
   payroll and administration vocabulary; a check warns when a catalogue uses a non-glossary variant of a glossary term.
4. **Review round trip (Q2).** `tools/` command exporting every Urdu text with key, English text, screen and screenshot
   name to a spreadsheet for the reviewer, and importing the reviewed file back (changed texts only, with a review date
   per key in a sidecar file); R-062 closes for the reviewed keys.

### Part S1.19b: right-to-left sweep and fixes
5. **Pseudo-locale and Urdu sweep (FR-UX-I18-08, -13).** A browser test visits every screen in the menu in pseudo-locale and
   in Urdu, at 360, 768 and 1280 pixels: no untranslated text, no horizontal overflow, no clipped button or label,
   directional icons mirrored, non-directional icons not mirrored, focus order follows reading order.
6. **Bidirectional text (FR-UX-I18-04).** Codes, numbers, e-mail addresses, document numbers and mixed names inside Urdu
   sentences and table cells keep their order (tests with sample data).
7. **Documents and notifications (FR-KRN-LOC-07, FR-UX-I18-11).** Every product notification and document template rendered
   in Urdu with sample data: e-mails right to left with correct shaping; PDFs with embedded fonts and correct joining (text
   extracted from the PDF compared with the source).
8. **Fixes and the Nastaliq decision (Q7).** Fix every finding; record screenshots of every screen in Urdu for the reviewer.

## Acceptance
- Removing one Urdu key, or giving it a different placeholder than English, fails the CI completeness check with the key's
  name; a notification event without an Urdu text fails it too.
- The Languages screen shows 100 percent for English and Urdu in every module after the slice.
- The pseudo-locale sweep finds no hard-coded text on any menu screen; the Urdu sweep finds no overflow at 360 pixels.
- An Urdu sentence containing an employee code, an e-mail address and an amount shows them in the right order (browser
  test); a payslip-style sample PDF in Urdu extracts to the same text it was given.
- The reviewer's spreadsheet round-trips: export, change three texts, import, and only those three change, with a review
  date.
- Every screen touched: accessibility, keyboard, three widths, screenshots in Urdu light and dark.

## Out of scope
Everything under "Not built here". Changing English product wording beyond what the glossary requires.
