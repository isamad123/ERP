## Goal
HR staff work with employees comfortably in English or Urdu: find anyone quickly, hire with one guided form that is never
lost if the browser closes, see everything about a person on one profile, look back at how an employment was on any date,
make changes with a reason and see the effect of a backdated date before saving, move someone to a sister company with
the choices the rules require, and load hundreds of employees from a spreadsheet with every duplicate caught.

## What exists already (and stays)
- S1.20's endpoints: persons and sections, identifiers (masked, reveal with permission), duplicates and merge, hire,
  events, as-of and timeline, impact check, transfer, rehire, contracts, probation, bank accounts and statutory
  registrations, user link.
- The list and form engine, saved views, history panel, approvals inbox (S1.12), import engine and centre (S1.16a).

## Build
### Part S1.21a: list, hire, profile
1. **Employee list.** Name (with Urdu name), employee number, company, unit, title, manager, status, joining date, type;
   filters on each, as-of date, saved views, export (S1.16b); "Hire" and "Import" buttons with their permissions.
2. **Hire form (FR-HRM-EMP-02, -04, -07, -18).** Steps: person (find an existing person first: rehire path), identifiers,
   contacts and address, employment and first assignment, bank account (optional), review; the duplicate warning shown
   as soon as an identifier or name and date of birth is entered, with "this is the same person" (rehire) or "different
   person" (stored with the review case).
3. **Drafts (FR-ADM-UXA-05, Q7).** The hire form saves a draft every few seconds and on leaving a step; drafts are listed as
   "Unfinished hires" for the person who made them; opened again where they stopped; deleted on hire or by the owner;
   expire after `cfg.drafts.keep_days` (default 30).
4. **Profile (FR-HRM-EMP-01, -05, -06, -09, -10).** Header (photo, name in both scripts, number, title, unit, manager, status,
   company); tabs: personal, contacts and addresses, family and emergency contacts, qualifications, identifiers (masked;
   "show" with the permission, Q5), employment (current assignment), contracts, probation, bank accounts (masked, pending
   approvals marked), statutory registrations, custom fields, history panel. A role without a field's permission sees it
   hidden or masked as the server sends it.
5. **Section editing.** Each section edits through its S1.20 endpoint, with server errors next to fields in both languages.

### Part S1.21b: timeline, changes, import
6. **Timeline and as-of (FR-HRM-EMP-11).** Events newest first with what changed (old and new), reason, who and when, filter
   by kind; an "as of" date picker that redraws the profile as it was (or will be) on that date; future events marked.
7. **Change and correct (FR-HRM-EMP-03, GHR-04).** "Change" dialog: kind, reason code, effective date, the fields that kind
   may change; for a past date the impact answers are shown and must be confirmed; approval-needed kinds say "sent for
   approval"; "Correct" on an effective event opens a correction with the original's values.
8. **Transfer and rehire (FR-XM-30).** Transfer wizard: target company, date, new assignment, the three carry-over choices
   with their company defaults, summary of what ends and what starts; rehire from a former employee's profile.
9. **Employee import (FR-HRM-EMP-07, -18).** Import type `hrm.employees` declared in the HR `imports.json`: person, first
   identifier, contact, employment and first assignment per row; duplicates found by the same check (exact identifier
   blocks the row; possible matches listed in the error file and the review queue); creates through S1.20's hire service.
10. **Duplicate review and user link (FR-HRM-EMP-18, -17).** Review queue: pairs side by side, "not a duplicate" or "merge"
    (merge through S1.20a, held for approval when configured); user link on the profile (pick a user through the people
    lookup) and on the user record of S1.13 (pick an employment).

## Acceptance
- **EMP AC from the screens:** hire, promote with a future date, correct the date backward, and see the as-of view, the
  timeline with audit details and the impact check at every step (browser test, real API and PostgreSQL).
- Closing the browser in the middle of a hire and signing in again offers the unfinished hire at the same step; another
  person cannot see it; after hiring, the draft is gone.
- Entering a CNIC already on file shows "already belongs to …" (name shown only to roles allowed to see that person);
  same name and date of birth shows a possible duplicate and creates a review case when "different person" is chosen.
- A role with masked identifiers sees `*****1234`; "show" (with permission) reveals it and the access log records it.
- A transfer to a sister company shows what ends and starts, asks the three choices, and the profile then shows both
  employments linked.
- Importing 300 employees with 5 duplicate identifiers and 4 possible matches: 291 hired, the error file lists the 5 and
  the 4, the review queue has the 4.
- A manager with "Team, no salary" scope sees only their team and no bank or salary data.
- Every screen: accessibility, keyboard, English and Urdu (glossary terms), three widths, states, screenshots.
- The draft endpoints pass the tenant-isolation suite (a draft is visible only to its owner) and have permission tests;
  tests cite the FR ids.

## Questions as put to the product owner
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts (a list, hire and profile; b timeline, changes and import). Alternative: one.
- **Q1 Menu.** *Recommendation:* group "People": "Employees", "Import employees", "Possible duplicates", "Unfinished hires".
  Alternative: duplicates under Administration.
- **Q2 Profile layout.** *Recommendation:* header plus tabs in the order of Build item 4; sections a customer hides by field
  security disappear; custom fields in their own tab unless a definition names a section. Alternative: one long page.
- **Q3 Hire form steps.** *Recommendation:* the five steps of Build item 2; bank details optional at hire (can be added
  later). Alternative: one long form.
- **Q4 Who sees the duplicate's name.** *Recommendation:* the warning names the existing person only when the user may read
  that person; otherwise "this number is already registered; ask HR administration". Alternative: always show the name
  (leaks people outside one's scope).
- **Q5 Revealing masked values.** *Recommendation:* a "show" button beside a masked value for holders of the field's
  permission; the server returns the value once and logs the view; it hides again on leaving the page. Alternative: no
  reveal in 1.0 (HR could never read a full CNIC on screen).
- **Q6 As-of view.** *Recommendation:* any date, past or future; future dates show approved future events; drafts and
  pending events are listed but not applied. Alternative: past dates only.
- **Q7 Server drafts (S1.12 ruling Q13, D-35).** *Recommendation:* new kernel table `cfg.form_drafts` (owner, form key,
  record id or none, data encrypted, updated at, expires at) with owner-only endpoints `/api/cfg/drafts`; personal data in a
  draft is encrypted with the customer key; used by the hire form now and any long form later. CR-077 records it.
  Alternative: no autosave (warning only, as today).
- **Q8 Import columns.** *Recommendation:* one row per employee with one identifier, one contact and the first assignment;
  further sections are added on the profile or by later import types. Alternative: several sheets in one workbook.
- **Q9 Urdu texts.** *Recommendation:* drafted with the S1.19 glossary and marked for review (R-062).
- **Q10 Change request CR-077.** *Recommendation:* approve: `cfg.form_drafts` and its endpoints (Q7); import type
  `hrm.employees` (Q8); reveal pattern (Q5); contracts register rows added at build.
