## Goal
An administrator sets up the organization the rest of the product hangs on: legal companies with their registrations
and salary bank accounts, sites with addresses and time zones, holiday calendars, and the unit structure (divisions,
departments, teams) as a tree and a chart that can be re-arranged from a chosen date and compared between two dates.

## What exists already (and stays)
- Generated read and write endpoints for `org.companies`, `org.branches`, `org.locations`, `org.org_unit_types`,
  `org.org_units`, `org.org_hierarchies`, `org.org_unit_links`, `ref.calendars`, `ref.holidays`; generated **writes are
  closed** on `org.company_registrations` and `org.company_bank_accounts` (encrypted values with blind index; CR-020).
- Branches screens (S1.12c) and the rule "every company keeps at least one branch" (SC-2 trigger creates the first one).
- `org.org_unit_links` is effective-dated with an exclusion constraint and a cycle-check trigger.
- Provisioning seeds numbering schemes per company (`NumberingSetup`).

## Found while preparing (needs a ruling)
1. **A company created later gets no numbering schemes** until the next `migrate`: provisioning seeds them, the generated
   company create does not (Q2).
2. **Operating hours have no column.** FR-KRN-ORG-02 and FR-ADM-ORG-02 name them; the approved data design has none (Q5).
3. **A map pin cannot use a map service**: assets must be bundled and nothing may be fetched from another host
   (CLAUDE.md front-end rules) (Q3).
4. **Hierarchy kinds are fixed** (`reporting`, `legal`, `geographic`) while FR-ADM-ORG-04 says "maintainable by the tenant"
   (Q7).
5. **Re-parenting needs two rows changed at once** (end the old link, start the new one); the generated endpoints change
   one row at a time (Q9).

## Build
### Part S1.14a: companies, sites, calendars
1. **Companies (FR-ADM-ORG-01).** List and form: code, names (English, Urdu), legal name, country, base currency, default
   language, holiday calendar, logo upload (S1.11c1 file store), status; "settings that differ for this company" panel with
   a link into the settings centre filtered to the company (FR-ADM-ORG-07).
2. **Company create service (Q2).** `POST /api/org/companies` stays generated but gains, in the same transaction, the
   company limit check and the default numbering schemes; the first branch already comes from the trigger.
3. **Registrations and bank accounts (Q4).** Hand-written endpoints for `org.company_registrations` (kind, authority, value,
   valid from and to; value encrypted with its blind index) and `org.company_bank_accounts` (bank from `ref.banks`, title,
   account number, IBAN, currency); values masked by field security; a new or changed bank account goes through
   maker-checker when `iam.maker_checker.enabled` is on (FR-KRN-IAM-14); every view of an unmasked value is in the access log.
4. **Locations (FR-ADM-ORG-02, Q3, Q5).** List and form: branch, address (structured: lines, city, province code, postal
   code, country), latitude and longitude as numbers with range checks, time zone (IANA picker), holiday calendar,
   operating hours by weekday.
5. **Holiday calendars (FR-KRN-LOC-06, FR-ADM-LOC-04).** Calendars list; holidays per calendar by year (date, names,
   kind, tentative mark for moon-sighting dates, confirm action); copy a year forward; assigned to companies and locations.
6. **Screens use the engine** (lists, forms, saved views, history panel); Urdu labels for every new table.

### Part S1.14b: units and the org chart
7. **Unit types and hierarchies (FR-ADM-ORG-04).** Lists and forms; renaming standard terms (for example Department to
   Wing) links to the labels editor of S1.15.
8. **Units (FR-KRN-ORG-03).** List and form: code, names, type, company, location; deactivate.
9. **Move service (Q9).** `POST /api/org/org-units/{id}/move` {hierarchyId, newParentId, effectiveFrom, reason}: ends the
   current link the day before and starts the new one in one transaction; refuses cycles, dates before the current link
   start and moves across companies (FR-XM-30 is for people, units stay in their company); audited.
10. **Org chart (FR-ADM-ORG-03, Q10).** Per hierarchy and "as of" date: an expandable tree (keyboard navigable) and a
    top-down chart; drag and drop to re-parent with a date prompt, and a "Move to…" dialog that does the same by keyboard;
    "compare two dates" shows units added, removed and moved. Bulk import of units arrives with S1.16.

## Acceptance
- An administrator creates a second company with another currency; it has a branch and default numbering schemes at once;
  at the company limit the plain refusal is shown.
- A registration's value shows masked to a role with the field masked and in full to an administrator; the full view is in
  the access log. With maker-checker on, a new bank account waits for a second administrator and appears in approvals of
  changes; after approval it is active.
- A location with a latitude outside -90 to 90 is refused next to the field; time zone and operating hours are saved.
- A holiday calendar for 2027 is copied from 2026; a tentative Eid date is confirmed later; the company uses the calendar
  and the workflow due dates skip it (S1.10b working calendar).
- A five-level structure is built; moving a department to another division from 1 January 2027 shows the old parent for
  31 December 2026 and the new one for 1 January 2027; compare lists the move; a cycle is refused with a clear message;
  the same move works by keyboard.
- Every screen: accessibility, keyboard, English and Urdu, three widths, states, screenshots, as in S1.13.
- New endpoints pass the tenant-isolation suite and have permission tests; tests cite the FR ids.

## Questions as put to the product owner
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts (S1.14a companies and sites, S1.14b units and chart). Alternative: one session.
- **Q1 Menu.** *Recommendation:* group "Administration", entries "Companies", "Branches", "Locations", "Organization units",
  "Org chart", "Holiday calendars". Alternative: one "Organization" page with tabs.
- **Q2 New company set-up.** *Recommendation:* creating a company also creates its default numbering schemes and checks the
  company limit in the same transaction (CR-070). Alternative: leave schemes to the next `migrate` (documents of the new
  company could not be numbered until then).
- **Q3 Map pin.** *Recommendation:* latitude and longitude typed as numbers (paste from any map) with a range check; no map
  on the screen, nothing fetched from outside. Alternative: an "open in a map" link to an outside map site (it sends the
  location to that site when clicked).
- **Q4 Bank accounts and registrations.** *Recommendation:* hand-written endpoints with encryption, masking, access-log
  entries and maker-checker on bank changes, built by Sonnet, **with a fresh Opus review of that part** (payment details;
  same reason as CR-052 Q7). Alternative: no review (automated checks only); or postpone bank accounts to the bank file
  slice S1.40.
- **Q5 Operating hours (D-35).** *Recommendation:* add `operating_hours jsonb` (per weekday: open, close, closed) to
  `org.locations` in a new migration (data design change under CR-070). Alternative: treat operating hours as 1.1 (no use
  in 1.0: attendance uses shifts, S1.28).
- **Q6 Letterhead.** *Recommendation:* the company page links to the company's letterhead template in the template editor
  (S1.15; templates of S1.11c2); no separate letterhead field. Alternative: a letterhead image upload on the company.
- **Q7 Hierarchy kinds.** *Recommendation:* the tenant maintains hierarchies (records) of the three fixed kinds; a new kind
  needs a change request. Alternative: make the kind free text (data design change).
- **Q8 National holidays.** *Recommendation:* typed by the administrator now; the Pakistan pack (S1.23) later installs them
  into a pack calendar that companies can use. Alternative: wait for S1.23 (no holidays until then).
- **Q9 Moving units.** *Recommendation:* the move endpoint of Build item 9 (one transaction, audited). Alternative: let the
  screen call two generated endpoints (a failure between them leaves a unit without a parent).
- **Q10 Org chart drawing (D-09).** *Recommendation:* drawn by our own components (no new package), lazily expanded, at
  most 200 boxes shown at once with "show more"; drag and drop has a keyboard equivalent. Alternative: a chart package such
  as d3-hierarchy (ISC licence).
- **Q11 Change request CR-070.** *Recommendation:* approve: numbering schemes and limit check at company create (Q2);
  registrations and bank-account endpoints (Q4); `org.locations.operating_hours` (Q5); unit move endpoint (Q9); contracts
  register rows added at build.
