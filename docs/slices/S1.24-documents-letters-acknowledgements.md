## Goal
Every employee's papers in one safe place: HR files and asks for documents, employees upload what is asked, HR checks
them, and reminders go out before a CNIC or licence expires. Letters (appointment, confirmation, experience, salary
certificate and the rest) are produced from the customer's templates in English or Urdu, approved when the customer
wants, numbered without gaps, e-mailed and acknowledged. A new policy document reaches exactly the people it is meant for,
and HR sees who has acknowledged it and who has not.

## What exists already (and stays)
- File store with per-customer encryption, scan hook, versions, retention, legal hold and expiry reminders (S1.11c1);
  attachments with permissions inherited from the record.
- Templates (Scriban), versions, letterhead, PDF generation, `doc.generated_documents` (S1.11c2); template editor (S1.15d).
- Tables listed above, generated reads, writes closed; `hrm.employee_letters.letter_no` is gap-free, assigned at issue.
- Notification catalogue and rules; workflow manifests; the attribute-rule evaluator (S1.23a).

## Found while preparing (needs a ruling)
1. **Document types and categories have no table.** `hrm.employee_documents.document_type` is a code (Q2).
2. **Who may see which document type** (FR-HRM-DOC-01 "access rules by role") needs a rule per type (Q3).
3. **Policy publication audiences** are `audience_rule` JSON; the same attribute rules as policy assignments fit (Q7).

## Build
### Part S1.24a: vault (fresh Opus review)
1. **Document types (Q2).** An option list `hrm.document_types` with category (identity, education, employment, medical,
   other), whether it expires, reminder days before expiry, and the field class that guards it (personal or restricted).
2. **Vault (FR-HRM-DOC-01, Q3).** Upload, replace (new version), view, download and remove per employment; issue and expiry
   dates; uploads scanned before use; views and downloads of personal and restricted documents in the access log; access by
   the document type's field class through field security; the employee sees their own documents.
3. **Document requests (FR-HRM-DOC-02).** HR requests a type with a due date from one employee or a selection; the employee
   is notified, uploads in "My documents"; HR verifies or rejects with a reason (notified); overdue requests remind the
   employee and HR.
4. **Expiry reminders (FR-HRM-DOC-03).** Documents and identifiers (S1.20) with expiry dates join the kernel expiry job with
   the audiences employee, line manager and HR (company setting for which), at the type's intervals (default 60, 30 and 7
   days before).

### Part S1.24b: letters
5. **Letter types (FR-HRM-DOC-04).** Letter types (option list: offer, appointment, confirmation, increment, promotion,
   transfer, warning, show-cause, experience, salary certificate, NOC, resignation acceptance, relieving) mapped to a
   template per company and language; merge fields from the employment, person, assignment, company and the source event,
   declared per letter type.
6. **Generation and approval (FR-HRM-DOC-05).** Draft from a template (merged values frozen in `data`), preview as PDF;
   letter types the customer marks need approval (document type `hrm.letter`); issue assigns the gap-free number, generates
   the PDF and stores it; e-mail to the employee's work or personal address (setting); the employee acknowledges in
   "Letters and policies to acknowledge"; cancel and reissue for corrections.
7. **Letters from events and requests (FR-HRM-DOC-07).** An employment event (S1.20) may propose its letter (promotion
   letter); a letter request service used by self-service (S1.26) generates automatically when the letter type is marked
   "self-service, no approval" (for example experience letter), otherwise routes to HR.
8. **Salary rule (FR-HRM-DOC-08).** A letter type with any salary-class merge field is `contains_salary`; only holders of
   `hrm.payroll.view` in the employee's scope may generate or view it; every view in the access log. Tested with a test merge
   field of the salary class until payroll provides real ones.

### Part S1.24c: policy publications
9. **Publish (FR-HRM-DOC-06, Q7).** A policy document (file, title in two languages, version) published to an audience
   defined by attribute rules (company, location, grade, employee type, unit; the S1.23a evaluator); new version supersedes
   the old and asks again; withdraw.
10. **Acknowledgements and report.** Employees acknowledge in "Letters and policies to acknowledge"; reminders at set
    intervals; report per publication: audience count, acknowledged, not yet (names, units), percent; export.

## Acceptance
- **DOC AC (spec 06a section 8):** a policy published to one company reaches only its employees; reminders go to those who
  have not acknowledged; the report shows 90 percent after nine of ten acknowledge, and names the tenth.
- HR requests a CNIC copy due in 7 days; the employee uploads it from "My documents"; HR rejects it with a reason; the
  employee uploads again; HR verifies; an infected test file is refused at upload.
- A document of a restricted type is invisible to a line manager, visible to HR, and each HR view is in the access log.
- A CNIC expiring in 30 days produces reminders to the employee and HR on the configured days.
- A confirmation letter in Urdu needing approval is approved by HR head (requester cannot approve), issued with the next
  gap-free number, e-mailed (`TestMailServer`), acknowledged by the employee; cancelling it keeps its number as void.
- An experience letter marked "self-service, no approval" is generated immediately through the letter request service.
- A user without `hrm.payroll.view` cannot generate or open a salary certificate (refused and not listed).
- Every screen: accessibility, keyboard, English and Urdu, three widths, states, screenshots.
- New endpoints pass the tenant-isolation suite and have permission, scope and field-security tests; tests cite the FR ids.
- Fresh Opus review of S1.24a with high and medium findings fixed or ruled on before its merge.

## Questions for the product owner
- **Q0 Parts and review.** *Recommendation:* three parts (a vault with a fresh Opus review because it accepts uploads; b
  letters; c publications). Alternative: two parts (b and c together).
- **Q1 Menu.** *Recommendation:* "People": "Documents", "Document requests", "Letters", "Policy publications"; the profile
  gains "Documents" and "Letters" tabs; employees get "My documents" and "To acknowledge" under "Me" (S1.26 completes it).
- **Q2 Document types.** *Recommendation:* a system option list `hrm.document_types` extended by the customer, with category,
  expiry flag, reminder days and field class kept in the item's attributes; the pack (S1.23c) seeds Pakistan types (CNIC,
  passport, degree, experience certificate, police character certificate, medical certificate). Alternative: a new table
  (data design change).
- **Q3 Access by type.** *Recommendation:* each type has a field class (personal or restricted); field security on that class
  decides who sees documents of the type; the employee always sees their own except restricted types HR marks "HR only".
  Alternative: a role list per type (second access model beside field security).
- **Q4 File limits.** *Recommendation:* PDF, JPEG and PNG only; 10 MB per file; settings `hrm.documents.max_mb` and allowed
  types; scanned before use (S1.11c1 hook). Alternative: any type.
- **Q5 Employee pages before self-service.** *Recommendation:* the two small pages "My documents" and "To acknowledge" are
  built here (the feature is not usable without them); S1.26 places them in the self-service home. Alternative: wait for
  S1.26 (requests and acknowledgements unusable until then).
- **Q6 Letter requests.** *Recommendation:* the generation service here; the employee request screen and its service request
  record (`hrm.service_requests`) in S1.26. Alternative: build the request screen here.
- **Q7 Audiences.** *Recommendation:* the S1.23a attribute rules (same conditions, same plain-sentence view) evaluated at
  publication and again daily for new joiners until the publication is withdrawn. Alternative: audience fixed at
  publication time only.
- **Q8 Letter e-mail.** *Recommendation:* issued letters are e-mailed as a link to sign in and download, never as an
  attachment, unless the company setting `hrm.letters.email_attachment` is on (password-protected PDFs come with payslips,
  S1.40). Alternative: PDF attached by default.
- **Q9 Acknowledgement.** *Recommendation:* a button "I have read and accept" recorded with time; no signature. Alternative:
  typed name as signature.
- **Q10 Urdu texts and sample templates.** *Recommendation:* product sample templates in English and Urdu for each letter
  type, marked "sample, to be adapted by the customer and reviewed"; Urdu marked for review (R-062).
- **Q11 Change request CR-080.** *Recommendation:* approve: option lists `hrm.document_types` and `hrm.letter_types` with
  attributes (Q2, Q5); document type `hrm.letter`; letter request service (Q6); settings of Q4 and Q8; contracts register
  rows added at build.
