## Goal
Joining and leaving are run by checklists nobody can forget. When someone is hired, the right onboarding tasks appear for
HR, IT, admin, the manager and the new person, each with a due date and reminders. When someone resigns, or HR records a
termination, retirement or other exit, the notice period comes from the policy, the last working day is worked out, the
acceptance goes through approval, the right letters are proposed, and an offboarding checklist with department clearances
starts. Payroll can see what is still outstanding, so no final settlement is paid while items are open, and the person's
pending approvals are handed to someone else before they leave.

## Build
### Part S1.25a: checklists and onboarding
1. **Templates (FR-HRM-ONB-01, -04).** Onboarding and offboarding templates per company with an applies rule (employee type,
   location, unit, grade; S1.23a conditions) and items: title in two languages, owner kind (HR, IT, admin, manager, employee,
   finance, department), owner role for kinds that need one, due offset in days from joining or last working day, clearance
   flag (offboarding), order.
2. **Checklist creation.** On hire (or on the `pre_joining` status) the matching onboarding template creates a checklist;
   on separation acceptance the matching offboarding template does; owners resolved to users (Q3); due dates computed;
   re-created never (changes to the template affect new checklists only).
3. **Working the list.** "My checklist tasks" for every owner (scope: own items), done, not applicable (with note) or waived
   (HR only, with reason); reminders before and on the due date and to HR when overdue; checklist progress on the profile.

### Part S1.25b: separations and clearance
4. **Resignation (FR-HRM-ONB-03, -14).** The employee submits a resignation (date, requested last day, reason) on a web page
   under "Me"; or HR records one; notice period from the `notice_period` policy resolved on the submission date; last working
   day computed (submission plus notice, or the requested earlier day with the shortfall recorded); status `submitted`;
   acceptance through the workflow (document type `hrm.separation`; the employee's manager first, then HR by default);
   withdrawal allowed before acceptance.
5. **Other exits (FR-HRM-ONB-06, Q5).** HR records termination, end of contract, retirement, death, absconding or other,
   each with its own route (or none, per company settings) and its letters proposed from S1.24 (resignation acceptance,
   relieving, experience).
6. **On acceptance.** Employment status `on_notice`; offboarding checklist created; the employee's open approval tasks listed
   to HR with "reassign" (S1.13 people lookup), and new tasks routed by delegation rules (Q9).
7. **Exit taxonomy and alumni (FR-HRM-ONB-13).** Exit reason from the option list (pack proposal, S1.23c); rehire eligibility
   (yes, no, with note) recorded by HR; former employees remain searchable as "alumni" with that flag, visible to HR only.
8. **Probation outcome (FR-HRM-ONB-12, Q8).** At the probation end (S1.20c reminders), the manager proposes confirm, extend or
   not confirm with comments, HR signs off through the workflow (document type `hrm.probation_review`); confirm makes a
   confirmation event, extend updates the record, not confirm starts a separation of kind termination.
9. **Clearance contract (FR-XM-29).** HR contract `ISeparationClearance.OpenItemsAsync(employmentId)`: incomplete clearance
   items, open approval tasks in the person's queue, and the items other modules register (`ISeparationOpenItemSource`:
   loans S1.41, claims S1.42, assets later); shown to HR and payroll on the separation page; S1.43 refuses settlement while
   any remain.
10. **Completion (Q7).** A daily job on the last working day: status `separated` with `separation_date`, event
    `hrm.employee_separated`, the linked user disabled when `hrm.user_link.disable_on_separation` is on (after reassignment
    of open tasks or, if any remain, a high-priority notice to HR instead of disabling), checklist reminders continue until
    done.

## Acceptance
- **ONB AC (spec 06a section 11.1):** triggering offboarding creates all clearance tasks, notifies their owners, and the
  clearance contract reports open items until every mandatory clearance is done (the settlement block is tested again in
  S1.43).
- A hire of a permanent employee at head office creates the head-office onboarding checklist with due dates relative to the
  joining date; IT's item goes to the holders of the IT role; reminders arrive; HR waives one item with a reason.
- An employee resigns on the web with a 30-day notice policy and asks to leave after 20 days: the last working day and a
  10-day shortfall are shown; the manager accepts, HR accepts; the employee sees the status; the employee cannot accept
  their own resignation.
- A retirement recorded by HR follows its own route and proposes the relieving letter.
- On acceptance the person's two open approval tasks are shown to HR and reassigned; on the last working day the
  employment is separated, the event is raised, and the user is disabled.
- A probation "extend by 2 months" signed off by HR moves the probation end; "not confirm" starts a termination separation.
- Every screen: accessibility, keyboard, English and Urdu, three widths, states, screenshots.
- New endpoints pass the tenant-isolation suite and have permission and scope tests; tests cite the FR ids.

## Questions as put to the product owner
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts (a checklists and onboarding; b separations and clearance). Alternative: one.
- **Q1 Menu.** *Recommendation:* "People": "Onboarding", "Separations", "Checklist templates", "Alumni"; everyone: "My
  checklist tasks"; employees: "Resign" under "Me".
- **Q2 Template choice.** *Recommendation:* the first template (in order) whose applies rule matches; a company default
  template (empty rule) last; HR may choose another at creation. Alternative: combine items of every matching template.
- **Q3 Owners.** *Recommendation:* kinds HR, IT, admin, finance and department resolve to the users holding the item's owner
  role in the employee's company (first to claim takes it, like a workflow queue); manager resolves to the line manager;
  employee to the employee's user. Alternative: one named person per item in the template.
- **Q4 Resignation approval route.** *Recommendation:* default route manager then HR, changeable per company in the route
  editor (S1.15c); the employee is never an approver of their own separation (FR-XM-32). Alternative: HR only.
- **Q5 Termination kinds.** *Recommendation:* each kind may have its own route (company setting maps kind to route; default:
  HR head) and its own letter types; absconding and death need no employee action. Alternative: one route for all kinds.
- **Q6 Notice shortfall.** *Recommendation:* this slice records days only (shortfall or waived by HR with reason); money
  effects (notice pay or recovery) are final-settlement lines in S1.43. Alternative: none.
- **Q7 Last working day.** *Recommendation:* the daily job of Build item 10; disabling the user waits until open approval
  tasks are reassigned (HR is told). Alternative: disable at once and let escalation move tasks.
- **Q8 Probation review without forms.** *Recommendation:* a fixed outcome form (confirm, extend with new end date, not
  confirm; comments) with manager proposal and HR sign-off now; when the form designer exists (S1.48), a customer form can be
  attached and its response stored in `review_form_response_id`. CR-081 records it. Alternative: wait for S1.48 (no probation
  outcomes until then).
- **Q9 Delegation of a leaver's approvals.** *Recommendation:* from acceptance, HR is prompted to reassign open tasks and to
  set a delegation from the last working day; nothing is approved automatically (FR-XM-32). Alternative: none.
- **Q10 Mistaken separations.** *Recommendation:* before the last working day a separation can be withdrawn (with approval if
  accepted); after it, the person is rehired (S1.20c) or a correction event records the error; a completed separation is
  never reopened. Alternative: allow reopening by HR (breaks FR-XM-05).
- **Q11 Urdu texts.** *Recommendation:* drafted with the glossary and marked for review (R-062).
- **Q12 Change request CR-081.** *Recommendation:* approve: document types `hrm.separation` and `hrm.probation_review`;
  contracts `ISeparationClearance` and `ISeparationOpenItemSource` (FR-XM-29 data); daily job for last working days; owner
  resolution by role (Q3); probation outcome without forms until S1.48 (Q8); contracts register rows added at build.
