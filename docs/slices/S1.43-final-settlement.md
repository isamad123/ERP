## Goal
When someone leaves, HR produces one settlement showing everything owed to them and by them: pay up to the last day, leave encashment, gratuity as the rules say, notice shortfall, loans, assets, bonus, tax. It cannot be completed while anything is open, and every open item is closed in one of three recorded ways. The employee gets a statement and signs a full-and-final acknowledgement.

## Build
1. **Open-items check (FR-XM-29).** Calls the S1.25 clearance contract and the loan, expense, task and approval queries; `open_items` listed with owner; `Complete` refuses while any item is unresolved.
2. **Resolutions.** Recover (becomes a settlement line), write off (approval through workflow, reason, authorised role), or reassign (tasks and approvals to a named person); each recorded.
3. **Lines (PAY-26).** Pending salary through an off-cycle run (S1.39) for the broken period; leave encashment from `ILeaveEncashments`; gratuity and notice rules through policy and pack calculators registered by S1.37 (**no value here**: absent, the line is "pending rule" and completion is refused); notice shortfall or payment by policy; loan balances; manual asset recovery; bonus; tax adjustment from S1.36.
4. **Statement and letter (PAY-27).** Settlement statement and full-and-final letter generated with the S1.11c2 templates; acknowledged by the employee through self-service with date; stored in the vault.
5. **Approval and payment.** Maker-checker approval; payment through S1.40a; final settlement becomes immutable after payment (FR-XM-05); a mistake is corrected by an adjustment run.
6. **Employee view (ONB-17).** Status and statement in self-service.

## Acceptance
- FR-XM-29: for each of the six open-item kinds, completion is refused; each of the three resolutions then allows it; a write-off needs approval and a reason.
- PAY-26: a worked example with every line kind (using test-only calculators registered by the test) gives the expected net; a missing gratuity rule blocks completion with a clear message.
- PAY-27: statement and letter generated, acknowledged, stored; the paid settlement cannot be edited.
- Tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; permission tests; GHR-06; FR ids in tests; fresh Opus review.

## Questions (recommendations are rulings, CR-083)
- **Q0 Single part.** *Recommendation:* one part.
- **Q1 Missing rule.** *Recommendation:* never default to zero; the line shows "rule not configured" and blocks completion.
- **Q2 Write-off authority.** *Recommendation:* permission `hrm.settlement.writeoff` and a threshold setting for a second approver.
- **Q3 Timing.** *Recommendation:* settlement opens when the separation is approved and can be drafted before the last day.
- **Q4 Asset recovery.** *Recommendation:* manual line with reason until the asset module (1.1).
- **Q5 Letter.** *Recommendation:* template type `full_final_letter` through S1.24.
- **Q6 Reversal.** *Recommendation:* before payment, cancel and redo; after payment, adjustment (FR-XM-27).
- **Q7 Calculator registry.** *Recommendation:* `ISettlementCalculator` contract; S1.37 registers gratuity.
- **Q8 Change request CR-099.** *Recommendation:* approve: contract `ISettlementCalculator`; permission `hrm.settlement.writeoff`; document types `hrm.final_settlement`, `hrm.settlement_writeoff`; template type `full_final_letter`; settings `hrm.settlement.writeoff_threshold`; expand-only.
