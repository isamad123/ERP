## Goal
Payroll staff define how the company pays: pay groups with their calendars and cut-off dates, the earning, deduction and
contribution components, the salary structures that bundle them, and each employee's salary from a date with the reason. A
payroll calendar shows every period's deadlines and a checklist of what must be ready before a run. Nothing is calculated here;
this slice makes the data the engine reads reliable, protected and effective-dated.

## What exists already (and stays)
Tables listed above (generated reads, writes closed); `pay_group_id` on assignments; field security `field_class`; maker-checker (S1.06).

## Found while preparing (needs a ruling)
1. **Formulas (PAY-03)** need a language; the engine (S1.35) evaluates it (Q3).
2. **Statutory-base flags "configured by pack"** (PAY-02): no pack content exists (Q4).
3. **Money fields and salary visibility:** every amount here is restricted data (Q5).
4. **Periods:** generating a year of periods needs the fiscal start and the group's frequency and cut-off rule (Q6).

## Build
### Part S1.34a
1. **Pay groups (PAY-01).** Frequency (monthly, semi-monthly, bi-weekly, weekly), currency, tax-year start month, cut-off day,
   payment day, paying bank account (company account), approval threshold (used by S1.38); an employee's group changes through an
   employment event (S1.20 assignment), never by edit; moving a group with an open run is refused.
2. **Periods (PAY-01, PAY-38, Q6).** Generate periods for a tax year; cut-off and payment dates by rule with manual change before the
   period opens; periods cannot overlap; a closed period cannot change.
3. **Payroll calendar and readiness (PAY-38).** List of periods with deadlines; reminder notification events before cut-off
   (`hrm.pay.cutoff_due`); checklist items: attendance locked, loans updated, claims approved, bank data complete, structures assigned
   (each item is a registered check; later slices register theirs; unavailable items show as such, never as passed).
### Part S1.34b
4. **Components (PAY-02, CMP-03).** Kind (earning, deduction, employer contribution, reimbursement, informational), payslip order,
   recurring or one-time, flags (taxable, pensionable, EOBI-able, other base flags as free pack-defined codes), source (product
   or tenant). New version = new effective-dated row (`pay_component_versions`), the old row stays.
5. **Methods (PAY-03, Q3).** Fixed, percentage of components, formula, table lookup, per-day, per-hour, attendance-linked, manual
   input: the definition is stored and validated (names exist, no cycle, tables exist); it is not evaluated here.
6. **Pro-rata and bonuses (PAY-05, PAY-08).** Pro-rata rule (calendar days, working days, fixed 30, custom) chosen per component
   version; bonus definitions (Eid, annual, performance, ad hoc) with eligibility rule, basis and a tax-treatment code.
7. **Structures (PAY-04).** Components with order, defaults, mandatory flag; assign rule by grade, employee type or individual.
8. **Compensation and revisions (PAY-04, PAY-06).** Assign a structure and amounts from a date with a reason (employment event);
   lines per component; a revision creates a new effective row, never an edit; a backdated revision records the date and the affected
   periods for S1.39 (no arrears computed here); amounts are field class restricted and need permission `hrm.pay.compensation.read`.

## Acceptance
- PAY-01: periods for a tax year generated for two groups; overlapping or edited closed periods refused; group move via event only.
- PAY-02/03/04: a structure with 10 components in order; a formula naming a missing component, or a cycle, is refused; a second version applies from its date.
- PAY-06: a salary revision records old and new structure, date and reason; a backdated one lists the affected periods.
- PAY-38: checklist shows attendance "not available" until S1.31 exists, bank data missing for 2 employees with a list; a reminder event is raised.
- GHR-06: a user without the salary permission sees no amounts in API, list, export or audit search.
- Maker-checker (Q5) on structure and assignment changes when the setting is on; tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; FR ids in test names.

## Out of scope
See "Not built here". No statutory table, tax slab or contribution rate is created.

## Questions (each recommendation is the ruling, CR-083)
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts; fresh Opus review of S1.34b.
- **Q1 Statutory-base flags.** *Recommendation:* flags are codes the pack declares (S1.23c declares only the names, no values).
- **Q2 Currency.** *Recommendation:* one currency per pay group (company currency by default).
- **Q3 Formula language.** *Recommendation:* a small grammar (operands: component codes and named inputs; operators + - * / ( ), min, max,
  round) evaluated in S1.35; this slice checks only that names exist and there is no cycle, through the contract `IFormulaValidator`
  that S1.35 completes with the grammar; no package added.
- **Q4 Pack dependence.** *Recommendation:* statutory flags and tables are referenced by code; missing codes make the component "incomplete" and block runs.
- **Q5 Protection.** *Recommendation:* all amounts restricted; maker-checker on structure and compensation changes controlled by the setting `hrm.pay.change_approval` (default on).
- **Q6 Period generation.** *Recommendation:* from the pay group's frequency and tax-year start; semi-monthly 1st-15th and 16th-end; weekly from a
  configured weekday; cut-off offset in days.
- **Q7 Employee in two groups.** *Recommendation:* impossible by construction (one assignment row at a time per employment); tested.
- **Q8 Change request CR-087.** *Recommendation:* approve: settings `hrm.pay.change_approval`, permission `hrm.pay.compensation.read`, events
  `hrm.pay.cutoff_due`, `hrm.compensation.changed`, readiness-check registry (`pay-readiness.json`), expand-only migration files if any column is needed.
