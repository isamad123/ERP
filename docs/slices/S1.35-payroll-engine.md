## Goal
Payroll staff open a run for a pay group and period, bring in the period's inputs (attendance, unpaid leave, overtime, one-time amounts,
files), and calculate. Before anything is calculated the period's attendance and leave are locked. The engine freezes everything it uses in
a snapshot, works out every component of every employee in the order the structure says, with pro-rata for joiners, leavers, unpaid leave and
mid-month changes, and records for each result a trace of the inputs, formulas, versions and rounding that produced every figure. It runs in
chunks that survive a restart, finishes 10,000 employees inside 15 minutes, and gives exactly the same answer every time it is run on the
same snapshot.

## What exists already (and stays)
Tables above (generated reads, writes closed; lock triggers on results are S1.38's todo stubs); pay groups, periods, components and their
effective versions with method definitions, structures and compensation assignments with lines, readiness registry (S1.34); formula names
and cycles checked by `IFormulaValidator` (S1.34b); attendance summary and overtime pay inputs (S1.29); lock and reopen (S1.31); unpaid leave
days and encashment units (S1.32); chunked resumable jobs with progress (S1.11a); run numbers `PR-{yyyy}{mm}-{seq:2}` (gap-free, S1.08).

## Found while preparing (needs a ruling)
1. **The grammar** of S1.34 Q3 (operands, + - * / ( ), min, max, round) cannot express conditions most allowances need (Q1).
2. **Evaluation order** between earnings, deductions, statutory contributions and tax is not specified beyond "order of evaluation" (Q2).
3. **"Fixed 30 days"** pro-rata does not say how 31-day months, February and partial months count (Q3).
4. **Unapproved absence:** PAY-07 says inputs come from locked attendance, but whether an absent day is unpaid is the customer's rule (Q4).
5. **Rounding** of lines and of net pay is not specified (Q5).
6. **Inputs from slices not built** (loans, expenses, bonuses through runs) need a place to plug in (Q6).
7. **BR-09 override** must not let calculation run on unlocked attendance (S1.31 Q4) (Q7).
8. **Tax and contributions** cannot be calculated until S1.36 and S1.37, and their content until the professional validates it (Q9).
9. **10,000 employees in 15 minutes** in CI needs a test that does not slow every build (Q11).

## Build
### Part S1.35a: grammar, methods, pro-rata, rounding (Opus, fresh review)
1. **Grammar and evaluator (PAY-03, Q1).** Hand-written parser and evaluator (no package): decimal literals, component codes, named inputs
   (`att.*` from the attendance summary, `lve.*` unpaid and encashed units, `emp.*` employment facts such as service months and grade code,
   `pd.*` period facts such as calendar days and working days, `in.*` pay inputs by component), operators `+ - * /`, parentheses, comparisons
   `< <= > >= = <>`, `and or not`, functions `min max round floor ceil abs if(condition, then, else)`; `System.Decimal` only, invariant culture,
   no floating point; division by zero, unknown names and overflow are errors for that employee (never zero); completes `IFormulaValidator`
   (S1.34) with full parsing so a stored formula that would fail evaluation is refused at save (closes R-149).
2. **Methods (PAY-03).** Fixed amount; percentage of named components; formula; table lookup (a `hrm.statutory_tables` table of kind `lookup`,
   `slab` or `rate` by code, version valid on the period end, Q8); per day and per hour (rate times units from inputs or attendance); attendance
   linked (formula over `att.*`); manual input (the period's pay input for the component, none gives zero and a trace note).
3. **Order (Q2).** Stages in fixed order: earnings; deductions marked before tax; statutory employee contributions (calculator, S1.37); income
   tax (calculator, S1.36); other deductions (loans and the rest); employer contributions and informational lines (never change net); within a
   stage, structure order; a formula may name only components of earlier stages or earlier in its stage (checked at save and at run).
4. **Pro-rata (PAY-05, Q3).** The period is split into segments at joining, leaving, compensation revision and assignment changes; each
   segment's amount is the monthly amount times the factor of the component version's basis: calendar days (segment days over period days),
   working days (from the effective shift and calendars, S1.28), fixed 30 (Q3), custom (a formula over `pd.*`); unpaid leave days reduce the
   paid days of components whose pro-rata rule covers unpaid leave (`ILeaveDeductions`).
5. **Rounding (Q5).** Calculation at full decimal precision; each line rounded once to the pay group currency's minor unit with the pay group
   setting `hrm.pay.rounding_mode` (`half_up` default, `half_even`, `down`); net optionally rounded to `hrm.pay.net_rounding_unit` (empty means
   none) with the difference as an informational line; every rounding step recorded for the trace.
### Part S1.35b: runs, inputs, snapshot, lock (Opus, fresh review)
6. **Runs (PAY-10).** `POST /api/hrm/pay/runs` {payGroupId, payPeriodId} creates a regular run (`created`, number from the numbering service,
   one regular run per group and period by the unique key); stage log entries with user and time; a period that is not open is refused.
7. **Membership (PAY-01).** Employments whose assignment names the pay group on any day of the period and who are employed on any day of it;
   leavers included for their days unless a final-settlement run already holds the period (the exclusion constraint decides); each member a
   `pay_run_employees` row (`included`); HR may exclude one with a reason (stage log).
8. **Inputs (PAY-07, Q6).** Manual pay inputs (`POST /api/hrm/pay/inputs`, salary permission) and import type `hrm.pay_inputs` (S1.16a);
   registry contract `IPayInputSource` (name, `CollectAsync(run, employments)`), with sources now: overtime pay inputs (S1.29), unpaid leave
   (`ILeaveDeductions`), leave encashment units (`ILeaveEncashments`), attendance summary (`IAttendanceSummary`); loans, expenses and bonus
   batches register later; "Import inputs" moves the run to `inputs_imported` and marks consumed inputs with the run.
9. **Attendance lock (FR-XM-26, Q7).** Calculation first calls `LockForRunAsync` (S1.31) in its own transaction; refused when the lock is
   refused (for example blocked by open exceptions without the override); calculation never runs on an open period.
10. **Snapshot (PAY-31).** Per member: assignment, compensation lines, component versions, structure, method definitions, tables and policy
    versions by id, attendance summary, inputs, leave units, period facts; ids, codes and amounts only (no identifiers or bank numbers);
    canonical JSON (sorted keys, invariant numbers) and SHA-256 `snapshot_hash`; frozen when the run is calculated, replaced only by an
    explicit recalculation (item 14).
11. **Cancellation.** A run in `created`, `inputs_imported` or `calculated` can be cancelled with a reason: inputs released, status
    `cancelled`, the number kept (FR-XM-06), event `hrm.pay_run.cancelled`, and `ReopenForCancelledRunAsync` (S1.31) reopens the period it
    locked. Later stages are S1.38.
### Part S1.35c: calculation, results, trace, performance (Opus, fresh review)
12. **Calculation job (PAY-34).** Job `hrm.payroll_calculate` (requester's rights; chunks of 500 employees; run-once per run and chunk;
    resumable; progress in the job centre); set-based loading per chunk; each chunk writes its results in one transaction; an employee whose
    calculation fails is `error` with the message key and the others continue; the run becomes `calculated` with totals and count when every
    chunk is done; event `hrm.pay_run.calculated`.
13. **Results and lines.** `hrm.pay_results` (gross, deductions, net, employer cost, worked and paid days, year-to-date taxable income and tax
    from earlier results of the tax year plus this one) and `hrm.pay_result_lines` (component, amount, units, rate, cost allocation from the
    assignment, FR-XM-27); amounts are salary class (GHR-06): reads need `hrm.pay.compensation.read` in scope; negative net is calculated and
    kept for S1.38's flag (BR-10 recovery rules are S1.41).
14. **Recalculation and verification (PAY-14, PAY-31).** "Recalculate" for all or chosen employees of a `calculated` run takes a new snapshot
    for them (inputs may have changed) and replaces their results; "Verify" recalculates from the stored snapshot without saving and compares
    result hashes: any difference is a defect and is reported.
15. **Trace (PAY-32).** `hrm.pay_traces` per result: for every line the method, formula text, inputs with values, versions, pro-rata
    segments, rounding steps; `engine_version`; `GET /api/hrm/pay/results/{id}/trace` with permission `hrm.pay.trace.read` and the salary
    field class; every read in the access log.
16. **Statutory hook (Q9).** Contract `IStatutoryCalculator` (stage, `Supports(component version)`, `CalculateAsync(context)` returning lines and
    trace steps): S1.36 (tax) and S1.37 (contributions) implement it; a component that needs a calculator that is not installed puts the
    employee in `error` with "statutory calculation not installed" (never a guessed amount); tests use test calculators with test tables.
17. **Change impact (GHR-04).** Register an `IEmploymentChangeImpact`: "N employees have results in runs not yet locked and will need
    recalculation; M periods are in locked runs and will produce arrears (S1.39)".

## Acceptance
- PAY-03: every method on a test structure gives the expected amount; a formula with `if`, `min` and a table lookup evaluates; division by zero
  puts that employee in `error`; a formula that references a later stage is refused at save.
- PAY-05: a joiner on the 10th, a leaver on the 20th, a revision on the 16th and 2 unpaid leave days each give the expected amounts under
  calendar, working-day and fixed-30 bases (tables of expected figures in the test, worked by hand).
- PAY-31: verifying a calculated run of 1,000 employees gives zero differences; changing a pay input and verifying still gives zero (snapshot),
  recalculating changes exactly that employee.
- PAY-32: the trace of a result reproduces each line from its inputs and the rounding steps.
- PAY-34/GHR-01: 10,000 employees with 15 components each calculate in under 15 minutes (timed test in its own CI job, Q11); killing the worker
  mid-run and restarting resumes without duplicate results.
- FR-XM-26: calculation of a period whose lock is refused does not start; after calculation the period is locked by the run; cancelling the run
  reopens it.
- GHR-06: a user without the salary permission sees no amount in results, lines, trace, list, export or audit search.
- Tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; FR ids in test names; a fresh Opus review per part.

## Out of scope
See "Not built here". No tax slab, contribution rate, ceiling or minimum wage anywhere in code, seed data or tests other than clearly named
test tables.

## Questions (each recommendation is the ruling, CR-083, except Q9)
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* three parts, Opus with a fresh review each; S1.35b after S1.31a. Alternative: two parts (over the CR-047 size).
- **Q1 Grammar.** *Recommendation:* S1.34's grammar plus comparisons, `and or not`, `if`, `floor`, `ceil`, `abs`, and the named input families
  above; hand-written, decimal only. Alternative: S1.34's grammar only (conditions then need table lookups).
- **Q2 Evaluation order.** *Recommendation:* the six fixed stages of item 3, structure order inside a stage. Alternative: structure order only
  (tax could be computed before the deductions it depends on).
- **Q3 Fixed 30.** *Recommendation:* a full month is 30 days whatever its length; a partial month counts 30 minus the days not employed (never
  below zero), so February and 31-day months pay the same full amount. Alternative: calendar days capped at 30.
- **Q4 Unapproved absence.** *Recommendation:* the engine does not decide it; a customer that deducts absent days adds an attendance-linked
  deduction component (for example `basic / pd.basis_days * att.absent_days`), shown in the set-up guide. Alternative: a pay-group setting.
- **Q5 Rounding.** *Recommendation:* line rounding to the currency's minor unit with `hrm.pay.rounding_mode` (default `half_up`), optional net
  rounding `hrm.pay.net_rounding_unit` (default none). Alternative: no rounding setting (fixed half up).
- **Q6 Input sources.** *Recommendation:* the registry `IPayInputSource`; sources that exist register now; loans, expenses and bonus batches
  register in their slices. Alternative: only manual and imported inputs now.
- **Q7 BR-09 override.** *Recommendation:* no calculation on unlocked attendance; the override exists only at lock time for open exceptions
  (S1.31 Q4). Alternative: none possible without changing FR-XM-26.
- **Q8 Table versions.** *Recommendation:* the version valid on the pay period's end date, recorded in the snapshot; a table changed later never
  changes this run (BR-11). Alternative: version valid on the payment date.
- **Q9 Statutory content (open, needs the professional).** No recommendation is possible for what is taxable or pensionable, which deductions
  reduce the taxable base, the tax and contribution values, ceilings or the minimum wage: they come from the Pakistan pack after the
  professional validates them (AS-4, R-002). The engine only provides the hook and refuses to guess (R-173).
- **Q10 Year-to-date.** *Recommendation:* computed from the employee's results of the same tax year in runs not cancelled, plus previous-employer
  income when S1.36 adds it. Alternative: a stored running total (can drift).
- **Q11 Performance test.** *Recommendation:* the 10,000-employee test runs in its own CI job `payroll-performance` in parallel with the others,
  printing the time; the 1,000-employee determinism test runs in the normal integration suite; S1.52 does 50,000. Alternative: on demand only.
- **Q12 Permissions.** *Recommendation:* `hrm.pay.runs.manage` (create, import, calculate, cancel), `hrm.pay.inputs.manage`, `hrm.pay.trace.read`;
  amounts always behind the salary field class and `hrm.pay.compensation.read` (S1.34). Alternative: one payroll permission.
- **Q13 Change request CR-093.** *Recommendation:* approve: grammar extension of S1.34 Q3 (Q1); stage order (Q2); fixed-30 rule (Q3); settings
  `hrm.pay.rounding_mode`, `hrm.pay.net_rounding_unit`; contracts `IPayInputSource`, `IStatutoryCalculator`, the payroll
  `IEmploymentChangeImpact`, `IFormulaValidator` completed; job `hrm.payroll_calculate`; events `hrm.pay_run.calculated`,
  `hrm.pay_run.cancelled`; import type `hrm.pay_inputs`; permissions of Q12; endpoints for runs, inputs, results and trace; CI job
  `payroll-performance`. Expand-only.
