## Goal
Every employee has leave balances that always add up from a ledger nobody can quietly edit. HR defines the leave types and rules;
entitlements are granted or accrue by policy; an employee asks for leave, the right manager approves, the balance moves, and a
cancellation gives it back. Unpaid leave is passed to payroll as a deduction input. At year end the system shows what will carry
forward, lapse or be encashed, lets HR confirm or undo, and anyone can ask "why is this balance 7.5" and get the ledger explanation.

## What exists already (and stays)
Tables above (ledger is append-only, `app` trait); policy types and resolution (S1.23); workflow engine and inbox (S1.10); jobs (S1.11a).

5. **Comp-off source:** overtime exists only with S1.29 (Q6). 6. Locked periods (FR-XM-26) need S1.31 (Q9).

## Build
### Part S1.32a
1. **Leave types (LVE-01).** Code, names (English, Urdu), paid or unpaid, counting (calendar or working days), unit (day, half, hour),
   document requirement, comp-off flag, deduction flag, source (product, pack, tenant); retire not delete.
2. **Policy type `leave` (Q1).** Parameters per leave type through S1.23 (JSON Schema): entitlement model and its values, carry-forward cap
   and expiry, encashment cap and rate basis, negative balance allowed, minimum notice, maximum consecutive days, eligibility (gender, tenure,
   probation), sandwich mode, combining restrictions. No default numbers; a type with no policy for an employee refuses requests with
   "no leave policy applies; ask HR" (S1.23 ruling).
3. **Entitlement models (LVE-02).** Fixed yearly grant, monthly accrual, accrual per days or hours worked, tenure tiers, grade tiers,
   manual grant; pro-rata for joiners and leavers by a rule in the policy; granting and accrual run as the job `hrm.leave_accrual`
   (monthly and on demand, run-once per employee, type and period, resumable).
4. **Ledger (LVE-10).** Kinds: opening, grant, accrual, usage, adjustment, encashment, lapse, carry-forward, comp-off earn and expiry,
   reversal; quantity in the type's unit; `reverses_entry_id`; entries never change (database refuses update and delete); every entry
   names source document and policy version.
5. **Balances.** `GET` balance per employee and type as of a date, with the entries that make it; negative allowed only when the policy says.
6. **Recompute (LVE-14).** Job and endpoint recompute balances from ledger and policies; differences are listed with the entry that
   explains each; nothing is changed automatically (HR posts an adjustment).
### Part S1.32b
7. **Requests (LVE-04, LVE-05, LVE-16).** Full, half-day (first or second half) and hourly; days counted by the type's counting rule
   using calendars, weekly off and rosters (S1.28); documents attached through the file store; checks: balance, eligibility, notice,
   maximum consecutive days, overlap with other leave, combining restrictions; submit through the workflow engine (route "line manager",
   then HR where the policy says); nobody approves their own request (FR-XM-32); HR may apply on behalf (acting user recorded, GHR-09).
8. **Sandwich rule (Q5).** When the policy says so, holidays and weekly-off days between two leave days count as leave; shown in the day list.
9. **Cancellation and modification (LVE-06).** Approved leave can be cancelled or changed; a change is a new request linked to the old one;
   approval where the policy requires; the balance is restored by reversal entries.
10. **Leave without pay (LVE-07).** Unpaid leave days (`is_paid = false`) are exposed through the contract `ILeaveDeductions` (employment,
    period, days) for the payroll engine; locked with the period (FR-XM-26).
11. **Comp-off (LVE-08, Q6).** Consumer of the event `hrm.attendance.comp_off_earned` (published by S1.29 for approved overtime or holiday work);
    earns an entry with expiry per policy; until S1.29 exists the manual grant covers it.
12. **Year-end (LVE-11).** Job `hrm.leave_year_end` creates a preview run (carry-forward, lapse, encashment amounts in units per employee and
    type); HR confirms (maker-checker setting) or rolls back before confirmation; confirmed run posts entries; encashment hands units to payroll
    through `ILeaveEncashments`.
13. **Pack defaults (LVE-12).** Pack leave types install as editable defaults through the S1.23 object handler (mechanism and test types only).
14. **Transfer carry-over (FR-XM-30, CR-094).** On a transfer between companies (S1.20c) the leave choice recorded on the event (`carry_over`:
    carry, encash or lapse) is applied per leave type: carry posts `transfer_out` on the old employment and `transfer_in` on the new one with the
    same `source_id` (implements the todo stub `hrm.todo_leave_ledger_transfer()`); encash hands the units to payroll through `ILeaveEncashments`;
    lapse posts `lapse`; a leave type missing in the new company is reported to HR and nothing is posted for it until HR maps it.

## Acceptance
- LVE-02/03: each entitlement model gives the expected balance on a test timeline (join mid-year, tier change, carry-forward with cap and expiry, negative balance refused).
- LVE-04/05/06: a half-day request across a holiday and weekend with sandwich on counts correctly; approval moves the balance; cancellation restores it by reversal; own request cannot be approved.
- LVE-07: unpaid leave days appear in `ILeaveDeductions`; leave in a locked date is refused (double now, S1.31 later).
- LVE-10/14: ledger entries cannot be updated or deleted by any login; recompute reports a planted difference and its cause.
- LVE-11: preview does not change balances; confirm posts once (run twice, same result); rollback before confirm leaves no entry.
- FR-XM-30: a transfer with "carry" moves the balance by a matching `transfer_out` and `transfer_in` pair; "lapse" posts one lapse; run twice, same entries.
- Tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; permission tests; FR ids in test names; fresh Opus review per part.

## Questions (each recommendation is the ruling, CR-083)
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts, Opus with a fresh review each.
- **Q1 Parameters.** *Recommendation:* the `leave` policy type with the schema above; no default numbers.
- **Q2 Statutory leave.** *Recommendation:* mechanism and test types only; real Pakistani statutory minimums enter the pack after a professional validates them (AS-4).
- **Q3 Leave year.** *Recommendation:* company setting `hrm.leave.year_start_month` (default January); per type override in the policy.
- **Q4 Hourly leave.** *Recommendation:* working hours per day from the shift (S1.28) or the setting `hrm.leave.hours_per_day` when none.
- **Q5 Sandwich.** *Recommendation:* modes `off`, `holidays`, `weekly_off`, `both`; counted only between two approved leave days of the same request.
- **Q6 Comp-off.** *Recommendation:* event contract `hrm.attendance.comp_off_earned` defined here, published by S1.29; manual grant until then.
- **Q7 Rounding.** *Recommendation:* quantities `numeric(9,4)`; no floating point; rounding mode in the policy.
- **Q8 Concurrency.** *Recommendation:* balance check and entry in one transaction with a per-employee-and-type advisory lock.
- **Q9 Attendance lock.** *Recommendation:* contract `IAttendancePeriodLock` in `Erp.Hrm.Contracts` (corrected by CR-094: it is an HR concept, and
  the kernel holds no attendance; D-10); a test double here; S1.29a gives it the real read side (period status) and S1.31 adds locking and the
  late-change path that replaces the refusal for locked dates (FR-XM-26).
- **Q10 Change request CR-086.** *Recommendation:* approve: policy type `leave`, settings `hrm.leave.year_start_month`, `hrm.leave.hours_per_day`,
  jobs `hrm.leave_accrual`, `hrm.leave_year_end`, `hrm.leave_recompute`, contracts `ILeaveDeductions`, `ILeaveEncashments`, `IAttendancePeriodLock`, event
  `hrm.attendance.comp_off_earned`, ledger update-and-delete refusal trigger (rules.yaml `functions`), expand-only migration files.
