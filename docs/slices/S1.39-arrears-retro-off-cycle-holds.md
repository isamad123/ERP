## Goal
When a raise is approved from three months ago, or a missed punch in a closed month is put right, or leave in a locked month is cancelled, the employee gets exactly
the difference in the next payroll, line by line, each line saying which month it belongs to, and the closed months are never touched. A bonus can be paid with the
monthly salary or in its own run without disturbing it. A salary can be held with a reason and paid later exactly as it was calculated. One wrong payslip can be
reversed before the money leaves; afterwards the correction is an adjustment or an off-cycle run.

## What exists already (and stays)
`hrm.retro_adjustments` (pending, applied, cancelled; component, amount, original run and period, cause), `hrm.salary_holds` (active, released; "a release pays the
original result, never recalculated"), `hrm.pay_result_reversals` (append-only, one per result, before payment), run kinds regular, bonus, correction,
final_settlement, advance; snapshots and `Recalculate` (S1.35); attendance and leave adjustments after lock (S1.31 `IAttendanceAdjustments`); revisions with affected
periods (S1.34b); bonus definitions (S1.34b); stages and reopen rules (S1.38); todo stubs owned by this slice.

## Found while preparing (needs a ruling)
1. **How arrears are computed** is not specified beyond "automatically over affected periods" (Q1).
2. **Arrears across tax years** have a statutory tax treatment (Q5).
3. **Holds and the stage flow:** a held employee's result belongs to the run but is not paid with it (Q3).
4. **Off-cycle runs and the attendance lock:** bonus and advance runs need no attendance; correction runs relate to earlier periods (Q4).

## Build
### Part S1.39a: retroactive differences (Opus, fresh review)
1. **Retro calculation (PAY-06, PAY-16, Q1).** For each employee and each affected locked period (from a revision's affected periods, an approved attendance or leave
   adjustment, or a backdated employment change): recalculate the original period in "what-if" mode from the original run's snapshot with only the changed facts
   replaced (new compensation lines, corrected attendance or leave totals), nothing written to the original run; the difference per component becomes one
   `hrm.retro_adjustments` row (pending) with `original_run_id`, `original_pay_period_id`, cause type and id; a zero difference writes nothing; job
   `hrm.retro_calculate` (chunked, run-once per cause).
2. **Apply in the next open run (PAY-16).** The next regular run of the employee's pay group takes pending adjustments as inputs: one result line per component and
   original period (`period_start` set), the adjustment marked applied with `applied_in_run_id` (implements todo stub `hrm.todo_retro_adjustments_apply_once()`:
   set once, pending → applied one way); cancelling or reopening the run releases them (S1.38 reopen event).
3. **Tax of arrears.** Arrears lines carry the irregular kind `arrears` and their period; S1.36's tax rule decides the treatment, including a period in an earlier tax
   year (pack data, Q5).
4. **Sources.** Consumers of `hrm.compensation.changed` (backdated, S1.34b), `hrm.attendance.adjustment_approved` (S1.31), leave corrections dated in locked periods
   (S1.31 item 5) and `hrm.employee_changed` with a backdated effective date (CR-096); each cause is processed once (inbox).
5. **Impact (GHR-04).** Register the payroll `IEmploymentChangeImpact`: "periods P1..Pn are locked; the difference will be paid as arrears in the next open run" with a
   preview of the per-component difference for one employee.
### Part S1.39b: off-cycle, bonus, hold, reversal (Opus, fresh review)
6. **Off-cycle runs (PAY-15, Q4).** Create runs of kind `bonus`, `correction`, `advance` or `final_settlement` for a pay group and an open period without touching the
   regular run (several off-cycle runs per period allowed; the regular and final-settlement exclusion constraint stays); members chosen by rule or list; inputs only
   from the run's own sources (bonus batch, correction items, approved advances of S1.41, settlement lines of S1.43); no attendance lock for bonus and advance runs;
   the same stages as regular runs (S1.38), numbered from the same scheme (FR-XM-06).
7. **Bonuses (PAY-08).** A bonus batch from an S1.34 bonus definition: eligibility rule evaluated as of a date, basis (formula over components), the tax-treatment code;
   result as `pay_inputs` (source `bonus`) for a regular run or as the members and inputs of a `bonus` run; HR reviews the batch before it is released.
8. **Salary hold and release (PAY-09, Q3).** Hold an employee in a run with a reason (`hrm.salary_holds` active, member status `held`): the result is calculated, locked
   and finalized with the run but excluded from its payment batch (S1.40); release in a later period pays the **original** result through a payment batch, never
   recalculated; release needs a second person (maker-checker); a hold that spans the tax year end is listed for HR.
9. **Payslip reversal before payment (FR-XM-27, Q6).** For one result of a locked run with no payment line that is not failed (implements todo stub
   `hrm.todo_pay_result_reversals_before_payment()`): an approved reversal (`hrm.pay_result_reversals`, reason, approver) cancels its payment and payslip; the corrected
   amount is paid by a `correction` run whose result has `corrects_result_id`; after a payment line exists, only an adjustment (S1.39a) or an off-cycle run.
10. **Screens.** Pending retro adjustments per pay group with cause and drill-down to the what-if trace; off-cycle run creation (kind, members, inputs) on the S1.38 run
    screens; bonus batch review; holds list and release; reversal dialog. English and Urdu; salary permission on every amount.

## Acceptance
- PAY-06: a revision of +10,000 effective three months back, approved now, gives three arrears lines in the next run, each with its period and the hand-worked
  difference; the original runs and results are unchanged (row versions and hashes); a zero-difference revision writes nothing.
- PAY-16/FR-XM-26: an approved attendance adjustment for a locked day produces one retro adjustment and one line in the next open run; applying twice is refused
  (one-way trigger); reopening that run releases the adjustment.
- PAY-15: a bonus run in the same period as an open regular run changes nothing in the regular run; an advance run pays approved advances only.
- PAY-09: a held employee's result is locked with the run but absent from its payment batch; release pays exactly the original net; release by the person who held it
  is refused.
- FR-XM-27: a reversal before payment is accepted and a correction run pays the corrected amount linked by `corrects_result_id`; after a payment line exists the
  reversal is refused by the API and by the database.
- Tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; GHR-06; FR ids in test names; fresh Opus review per part.

## Questions (each recommendation is the ruling, CR-083, except Q5)
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts on Opus with a fresh review each. Alternative: three (bonus separate).
- **Q1 Arrears method.** *Recommendation:* recalculate each affected period from its stored snapshot with only the changed facts replaced and pay the per-component
  difference (exact, explainable, uses the same engine); never a simple "new minus old salary times months". Alternative: the simple formula (wrong for pro-rata,
  overtime and percentages).
- **Q2 Where arrears are paid.** *Recommendation:* the next open regular run; an urgent case may use a `correction` off-cycle run. Alternative: always a correction run.
- **Q3 Hold.** *Recommendation:* as item 8 (calculated and locked with the run, paid on release, never recalculated), matching the data design note. Alternative:
  exclude the employee and recalculate later (contradicts the design).
- **Q4 Off-cycle and attendance.** *Recommendation:* bonus and advance runs do not lock attendance; correction runs take their changes from retro adjustments and
  reversals only, so they need no lock either. Alternative: every run locks attendance (blocks a bonus run before month end).
- **Q5 Arrears across tax years (open, needs the professional).** No recommendation is possible: whether arrears are taxed in the year earned or the year paid, and
  how, is tax law; S1.36's tax rule holds the treatment for the `arrears` kind as pack data (AS-4, R-247).
- **Q6 Reversal approval.** *Recommendation:* permission `hrm.pay.result.reverse` plus a second approver (maker-checker); the reversal appears on the exception report
  (FR-XM-32). Alternative: one approver.
- **Q7 Retro of a leaver.** *Recommendation:* retro adjustments of a separated employee go into the final settlement (S1.43) or, after it, a correction run.
  Alternative: none.
- **Q8 Bonus batches.** *Recommendation:* a batch record in the run's stage log and inputs (no new table): bonus inputs carry the batch id in `source_id`. Alternative:
  a bonus batch table (D-35 change).
- **Q9 Change request CR-112.** *Recommendation:* approve: job `hrm.retro_calculate`; consumers of `hrm.compensation.changed`, `hrm.attendance.adjustment_approved`,
  `hrm.employee_changed`; off-cycle run creation for the four kinds; bonus batch endpoints; hold and release endpoints with maker-checker; reversal endpoint with
  permission `hrm.pay.result.reverse`; the payroll `IEmploymentChangeImpact`; the two lock functions (implemented entries replacing the todo stubs, new migration file);
  events `hrm.pay.retro_calculated`, `hrm.salary_hold.released`; expand-only.
