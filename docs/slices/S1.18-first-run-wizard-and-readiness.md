## Goal
A new customer's administrator signs in for the first time and is led, step by step, from an empty system to one ready
for use: company details, languages, the modules they bought, their structure, their first colleagues, and how
notifications are sent. They can stop and come back, skip a step, and run it again for a second company. A checklist per
module then shows what is still missing, with a link to fix each item, and a module is marked live only when its
mandatory items are done, or an administrator overrides with a reason.

## What exists already (and stays)
- `cfg.setup_tasks` (module, task key, company, required, status pending/done/skipped, who and when) with generated
  endpoints; nothing writes it yet.
- Every engine the wizard calls: companies and units (S1.14), settings and flags (S1.09a, S1.15), users and invitations
  (S1.13), numbering document types per company (S1.08), workflow routes (S1.10, S1.15), notification rules (S1.11b1), import
  centre (S1.16).
- Size profiles and editions are entitlement and setting presets (D-26, D-40).

## Found while preparing (needs a ruling)
1. **"Go-live" has no meaning yet.** FR-ADM-ONB-03 says the checklist "blocks go-live of a module"; nothing in the product
   is a module's go-live, and the specification does not say what a module may not do before it (Q4).
2. **The pack step comes before the pack loader.** The wizard's "country and industry pack" step needs S1.23 (Q3).
3. **Readiness items belong to modules** (D-10): the kernel cannot check HR items such as "leave policy assigned" (Q2).

## Build
### Part S1.18a: tasks, checks, go-live
1. **Set-up manifests (Q2).** Each module declares wizard steps and readiness items in
   `<module>.Infrastructure/Access/setup-tasks.json` (key, title key, step or checklist, required, link, company or
   customer level, check name); seeded by `migrate` and provisioning (`SetupTaskSetup`) into `cfg.setup_tasks` per company.
   Kernel items now: company profile complete, at least one location, unit structure, holiday calendar for the year,
   numbering set per document type (S1.08), approval route active per licensed document type, at least one other
   administrator, notification rules reviewed.
2. **Checks.** A check is code in the owning module behind a kernel contract (`IReadinessCheck`: done or not, with a
   message), run when the checklist opens and after each wizard step; manual items (for example "notification rules
   reviewed") are ticked by a person; every tick, skip and override is audited.
3. **Go-live (Q4).** `POST /api/cfg/readiness/{module}/go-live` {companyId, overrideReason?}: refused while a required item is
   not done unless a reason is given (stored and audited); recorded as the module's go-live task; modules may declare
   actions that need go-live (first user: payroll finalization, S1.38); nothing else is blocked.

### Part S1.18b: screens
4. **Wizard (FR-ADM-ONB-01, -02, Q1, Q5).** Shown to a tenant administrator at first sign-in and from the home page until
   finished; steps: company profile; languages and formats; modules to use (feature flags within the licence); structure
   (first units or "import"); first users and roles (invite, or "import"); notifications (rules and a test e-mail); summary.
   Each step saves through the normal endpoints, shows "done", "skipped" or "to do", and can be reopened; "Set up another
   company" runs the company steps again.
5. **Checklist (FR-ADM-ONB-03).** Per module and company: items with status, message and a link to the screen that fixes
   them; progress; "Go live" with the override dialog asking for a reason when items are open.
6. **Import offers (FR-ADM-ONB-04).** The structure and users steps link to the import centre with the right type and the
   default template preselected; later module slices add theirs (employees in S1.21).

## Acceptance
- A freshly provisioned customer's administrator signs in and sees the wizard; completes company profile and languages,
  skips modules, closes the browser, signs in again and resumes at "modules" with the first two steps shown done.
- Every step writes only through the normal endpoints (proved by the audit log source `ui` and by the same validation
  messages as the manual screens).
- The checklist shows "numbering set" open for a document type without a scheme and links to the numbering screen; after
  fixing it the item is done.
- "Go live" with an open required item is refused without a reason, accepted with one, and the override is in the audit
  log; a second company has its own checklist and go-live.
- Every screen: accessibility, keyboard, English and Urdu, three widths, states, screenshots.
- New endpoints pass the tenant-isolation suite and have permission tests; tests cite the FR ids.

## Out of scope
Everything under "Not built here". HR, attendance, leave and payroll readiness items (their slices add them to their
manifests).

## Questions for the product owner
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts (a server, b screens). Alternative: one session.
- **Q1 When the wizard appears.** *Recommendation:* automatically at a tenant administrator's first sign-in, then as a home
  page card "Finish setting up" until every step is done or skipped; never for other users. Alternative: only from the menu.
- **Q2 Module items (D-10).** *Recommendation:* each module declares its steps and items in `setup-tasks.json` with checks in
  its own code behind `IReadinessCheck`; the kernel only lists and runs them. Each later slice adds its items (for example
  S1.23 "policies assigned", S1.34 "pay groups set", S1.38 "payroll approvers set"). Alternative: one hard-coded kernel
  list (breaks D-10).
- **Q3 Pack step.** *Recommendation:* the pack step is added by S1.23 with the pack loader; until then the wizard has no pack
  step (no placeholder that pretends to work). Alternative: build S1.18 after S1.23 (delays the admin console batch).
- **Q4 What go-live means (the specification is silent).** *Recommendation:* go-live is a recorded milestone per module and
  company; a module may name actions that need it, and in 1.0 only payroll finalization does (S1.38); before go-live
  screens show "set-up mode" on the checklist only. Alternative: block all of a module's transactions before go-live
  (stops trial data entry during set-up).
- **Q5 Modules step.** *Recommendation:* lists the licensed modules and features; switching one off sets its feature flag
  for the company (FR-KRN-SET-05); unlicensed ones are shown greyed with "not in your licence". Alternative: hide
  unlicensed modules.
- **Q6 Size profile.** *Recommendation:* not changed in the wizard (set at provisioning by the vendor; D-40); shown
  read-only. Alternative: let the customer choose a profile (changes many defaults at once).
- **Q7 Fiscal year.** *Recommendation:* the wizard sets only the fiscal-year start month (`cfg.numbering.fiscal_year_start_month`);
  calendars and periods are 1.1. Alternative: none.
- **Q8 E-mail settings step.** *Recommendation:* the customer reviews notification rules and sends itself a test message;
  sender and mail server are operator settings (`mail` commands) and shown read-only. Alternative: none (customer sender
  identity is FR-KRN-TEN-08, 1.1).
- **Q9 Urdu texts.** *Recommendation:* drafted and marked for a native speaker's review (R-062).
- **Q10 Change request CR-074.** *Recommendation:* approve: manifest `setup-tasks.json`, `SetupTaskSetup` and the
  `IReadinessCheck` contract; go-live endpoint and meaning (Q4); contracts register rows added at build.
