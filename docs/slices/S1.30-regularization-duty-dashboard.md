## Goal
An employee who forgot to punch, or whose day came out wrong, asks for a correction with a reason and a photo of proof, and their
manager approves it; the day is recalculated. Employees ask in advance to be counted present while on an official visit, at a client or
working from home. Managers and HR work through a queue of attendance exceptions for their people. Everyone sees their own month at a
glance. HR and managers watch a live board of who is in, late, away or not yet punched, and print or export the standard attendance
reports, each limited to the people they may see.

## What exists already (and stays)
Tables above (generated reads, writes closed); exceptions opened and closed by the S1.29 processor; reprocessing consumers (S1.29 item 6);
workflow engine and the approvals screen that shows any registered document type (S1.12d); list and form engine (S1.12c); exports
(S1.16b); employee and manager portals with their panel and widget hosts (S1.26).

## Build
### Part S1.30a: requests, queue, my attendance
1. **Regularization requests (ATT-11).** `POST /api/hrm/attendance/regularizations` {workDate, requestedIn, requestedOut, reason, fileIds,
   exceptionId?} by the employee (from the token) or by a supervisor on behalf (acting user recorded, GHR-09); files through the file
   store with the S1.24a limits; document type `hrm.regularization` in `workflow.json` (fields: date, times asked, exception, reason;
   context `in_locked_period` for S1.31b; default route line manager); approval asks S1.29 to reprocess the day (the punches themselves are
   never changed; the request is the evidence and the processor uses the approved times, explained as "regularized"); rejection and return
   for correction; cancel while pending.
2. **Duty requests (ATT-14, BR-06, Q3).** On duty, official visit, work from home: start and end date-time, purpose; document type
   `hrm.duty_request`; on decision S1.29 reprocesses the days; an overlap with approved leave or another duty is refused unless the
   attendance policy's precedence list orders the two kinds (S1.29 item 10); counted as present only for the kinds the policy lists.
3. **Exception queue (ATT-11).** `/attendance/exceptions` for reviewers (`hrm.attendance.exception.review` with data scope): filters by
   unit, kind, date, status; actions "ask the employee to regularize" (notification), "accept as is" (with reason), and for late and early
   rows the waiver of S1.29 item 9 (never one's own, FR-XM-32); bulk select of up to 200 rows (one reason for all).
4. **My attendance (SSP-01).** Month calendar of own days (result colour and icon, never colour only), a day opens its explanation (S1.29
   read endpoint, plain-language steps), own exceptions with "Regularize", own requests and their status; registered as the attendance
   panel of the employee portal (S1.26 Q3).
5. **Team attendance (SSP-02).** Manager view of direct reports for a week or month (results grid), today's status per person, open
   exceptions count, "regularize on behalf"; registered as the team widget of the manager portal.
6. **Overtime decisions (ATT-12 screens).** Overtime entries of S1.29 appear in `/approvals` through their `workflow.json` fields (date, day
   type, calculated and asked minutes, cap applied); "My overtime" list for employees with the comp-off choice where the policy allows it.
### Part S1.30b: dashboard, reports
7. **Live dashboard (ATT-18, Q6).** `GET /api/hrm/attendance/live?date=&orgUnit=&location=`: counts and lists of present, late, on leave,
   on duty, absent and not yet punched, by unit and location, computed for today from punches, the effective shift, approved leave and
   duty (not from processed days), for past dates from processed days; within the viewer's data scope; refreshed every 60 seconds while
   the page is open (setting `hrm.att.live_refresh_seconds`); counts are links to the people behind them.
8. **Reports (ATT-19, Q7).** Hand-written read endpoints with list pages (engine `ListPage`) and filters by company, unit, location, date
   range: daily muster, monthly muster (one row per employee, one column per day with the result code), late and early, overtime (calculated,
   approved, paid or converted), absenteeism (absent days and rate per unit), punch log (raw punches with source and duplicate flag), device
   sync (device, last seen, last sync, last error, punches received per day), exception ageing (open exceptions by age band). Each respects
   data scope; none shows salary (GHR-06).
9. **Exports.** Every report exports to CSV and Excel through the S1.16b export jobs (row limits and approval settings of S1.16); the monthly
   muster also prints from the browser with a print style sheet (no PDF engine change).
10. **Screens and checks.** Menu group "Attendance": My attendance, Team attendance, Exceptions, Live board, Reports; English and Urdu labels,
    right-to-left grid, three widths, loading, empty, error and permission-denied states, axe checks, route chunks within budget.

## Acceptance
- ATT-11: an employee with a missing-out exception regularizes with an attachment; the manager approves; the day becomes present with the
  explanation "regularized"; the original punches are unchanged; the employee cannot approve their own request; return for correction works.
- ATT-14/BR-06: an official visit over two days counts as on duty; a work-from-home request overlapping approved leave is refused with the
  reason; with the precedence list set, it is accepted and the explanation names the order.
- ATT-18: with test punches at 09:05 for 3 of 5 rostered employees (one late), the board shows 2 present, 1 late, 2 not yet punched for
  the unit; a user scoped to another unit sees none of them.
- ATT-19: each report returns the expected rows for a seeded month and exports to CSV and Excel; the monthly muster prints.
- Locked date: a regularization for a locked date is refused with the lock message until S1.31b, then routed through HR.
- Browser tests (`Suite=WebE2E`): employee regularizes, manager approves, HR opens the live board; English and Urdu screenshots.
- Tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; permission and scope tests; FR ids in test names.

## Questions (each recommendation is the ruling, CR-083)
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts as above. Alternative: one (over the CR-047 size).
- **Q1 Routes.** *Recommendation:* default route line manager for both document types, HR added by the customer's route editor (S1.15c) or by
  the locked-period condition (S1.31b). Alternative: manager then HR always.
- **Q2 Regularization effect.** *Recommendation:* approved times are an input of the processor, never new punches, so evidence stays
  original (FR-XM-05). Alternative: write correcting punches (`supersedes_event_id`, S1.27c) on approval.
- **Q3 Field duty.** *Recommendation:* in 1.0 a field duty is requested as `on_duty` with purpose "field duty"; the field-duty assignment that
  drives location Mode D arrives with geo-attendance in 1.1 (its v0.3 extension is 1.1). Alternative: add a `field_duty` kind now (data
  design change with no 1.0 behaviour of its own).
- **Q4 Regularization window.** *Recommendation:* company setting `hrm.att.regularization_window_days` with no default (empty means no limit);
  the customer sets it. Alternative: a product default (an invented rule).
- **Q5 Bulk actions.** *Recommendation:* up to 200 rows, one reason, each row audited separately. Alternative: no bulk in 1.0 (ATT-61, bulk
  tools, is 1.1).
- **Q6 Live board.** *Recommendation:* today from punches and plans, past days from processed results, polling every 60 seconds (no push
  channel in 1.0). Alternative: only processed days (no "not yet punched").
- **Q7 Reports.** *Recommendation:* hand-written read endpoints over the engine list page with exports; the same queries later feed S1.51
  dashboards. Alternative: wait for a report engine (Later).
- **Q8 Notifications.** *Recommendation:* catalogue entries `hrm.regularization.decided`, `hrm.duty_request.decided` (to the employee) and the
  workflow's own task notifications to approvers. Alternative: none beyond workflow defaults.
- **Q9 Change request CR-090.** *Recommendation:* approve: document types `hrm.regularization` and `hrm.duty_request`; endpoints for requests,
  exception actions, live board and the eight reports; settings `hrm.att.regularization_window_days`, `hrm.att.live_refresh_seconds`; the two
  events; field duty as on-duty in 1.0 (Q3). Expand-only; no table.
