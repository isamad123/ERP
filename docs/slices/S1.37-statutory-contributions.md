## Goal
Each month the engine works out the employee's and the employer's share of every statutory scheme that applies to the person: old-age benefits, the provincial
social security of the province the person works in, and the provident fund, each from the base, rate and ceiling the pack's tables say for that date. HR
records which schemes apply to whom and their registration numbers. The gratuity a person has earned can be worked out at any date, both to accrue the
liability each month and to pay it on leaving. Payroll sees totals per scheme and company for the returns. No rate lives in the code.

## What exists already (and stays)
Statutory tables and the lock (S1.36a); per-employee scheme applicability and registration numbers (`hrm.employment_statutory_registrations`, S1.20d); location
`region_code` ("selects provincial tables"); provident fund accounts (ledger written by S1.44b); liability accruals table (job in S1.44b); the engine's plug-in,
snapshot and trace (S1.35); `ISettlementCalculator` (S1.43 Q7).

## Found while preparing (needs a ruling)
1. **No contribution content exists** (AS-4) (Q4).
2. **Bases differ by scheme:** some take salary components, some a fixed amount such as a minimum wage, with a ceiling (Q1).
3. **Applicability** is stored per employee (S1.20d) but the pack's eligibility conditions are not (Q2).
4. **Workers Welfare Fund and Workers Profit Participation Fund** are listed in PAY-20, but their usual base is the company's profit, which payroll does not hold
   and Finance does not exist (D-43) (Q6).
5. **Gratuity** is needed by three slices (accrual S1.44b, settlement S1.43, liability basis) and has no calculator (Q5).

## Build
### Part S1.37a: contribution schemes (Opus, fresh review)
1. **Contribution scheme (Q1).** Pack object and policy type `contribution_scheme` (code matching `employment_statutory_registrations.scheme`, effective-dated):
   base (`components` with flag codes to include, or `table` naming a table that gives a fixed base amount), ceiling (none or a table), employee and employer
   rates (tables of kind `rate`, keyed by `region_code` when the scheme is provincial), rounding (unit and mode), period basis (per pay period; pro-rata by paid
   days or not, a scheme choice), the components the results go to (employee deduction, employer contribution).
2. **Applicability (Q2).** An employee contributes to a scheme on a date when the registration row says `is_applicable`; a pack eligibility rule (S1.23 attribute
   conditions such as employee type) only **suggests** applicability on hire and in a "who may be missing" list, never switches it silently; a missing registration
   number raises the S1.38 validation flag `missing_statutory_id` (new flag value, Q8).
3. **Province.** A provincial scheme takes the rate keyed by the `region_code` of the employee's work location valid on the period end; a location without a code
   makes the employee "incomplete" for that scheme.
4. **Calculator (PAY-20).** `ContributionCalculator : IStatutoryCalculator` for components flagged by a scheme: base, ceiling, rates, rounding, both shares; trace steps
   for every number (scheme, table versions and rows, base parts); the employee share reduces net, the employer share is employer cost (S1.35 stages).
5. **Provident fund.** A provident fund scheme is a contribution scheme of the customer's fund (rates are the fund's rules, entered by the customer; legal limits of a
   recognised fund are pack checks, Q4); the run's provident fund lines are read by S1.44b to write the ledger at finalize; transfer between companies follows the
   account notes (S1.44b).
6. **Summaries.** `GET /api/hrm/pay/contributions?run=&scheme=` totals per scheme, company and region, and per employee (scoped, salary class); S1.46 returns read
   the same query; contribution events for S1.45 ride on the finalized run (no separate posting).
### Part S1.37b: gratuity, company-level contributions, screens (Opus, fresh review)
7. **Gratuity calculator (Q5).** Policy type `gratuity` (pack default, customer override): entitlement condition (S1.23 attribute rule and minimum service), service
   counting (from `service_start_date`, rounding of part years as the policy says), wage base (components with a flag, on a date the policy names: last drawn or
   average of N months), amount per year of service (a formula over those names in the S1.35 grammar, or a table), caps; `IGratuityCalculator.AmountAsync(employment,
   asOf)` returns amount and trace; registered as the `gratuity` `ISettlementCalculator` for S1.43 and as the gratuity basis for the S1.44b accrual job.
8. **Company-level contributions (Q6).** A scheme may be `company_level`: its base is an amount the customer enters per company and period (for example a figure from
   its accounts), its rate from a table; the result is a company obligation record (new table `hrm.company_contributions`: company, scheme, period, base, table
   version, amount; append-only with reversal rows) linked to its compliance task (S1.46) and the journal export, never a payslip line; built only for schemes the
   professional confirms belong in payroll (Q6 open); until then neither the table nor the code is created.
9. **Screens.** Schemes and their tables (S1.23 policy and pack screens with table grids), applicability and registration numbers per employee (on the S1.21 profile
   payroll section, encrypted number masked, reveal logged), "who may be missing" list, contribution summary per run, gratuity "as of today" on the employee's
   payroll section for HR with permission. English and Urdu.
10. **Impact preview.** The S1.36 preview endpoint accepts contribution and gratuity versions as well (one preview mechanism).

## Acceptance
- PAY-20: with `test.eobi`-style scheme data (a fixed-base scheme with a ceiling), a component-base scheme keyed by two test regions, and a test provident fund, a run
  gives the hand-worked employee and employer amounts; moving the employee to a location of the other region changes the rate from that period only.
- PAY-21: changing a rate is a new table version from a date; a referenced version refuses change; no contribution value appears in code, schema defaults or seed
  data (same scan as S1.36).
- Applicability: an employee without the registration row contributes nothing and appears in "who may be missing"; a missing number raises `missing_statutory_id`.
- Gratuity: with a test policy, the amount at three dates matches a hand-worked table; an employee below the minimum service gets zero with the reason; S1.43's test
  settlement uses the same figure; no policy blocks the settlement line ("rule not configured").
- Company-level scheme (only if Q6 confirms one): an entered base gives one obligation record, no payslip line.
- Tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; GHR-06 on every amount; FR ids in test names; fresh Opus review per part.

## Out of scope
See "Not built here". No real rate, base, ceiling, eligibility rule or gratuity formula is entered.

## Questions (each recommendation is the ruling, CR-083, except Q4 and Q6)
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts on Opus with a fresh review each. Alternative: one part per scheme (more sessions, same mechanism).
- **Q1 One mechanism.** *Recommendation:* every scheme is data of one `contribution_scheme` type with base kinds `components` or `table`; no scheme-specific code.
  Alternative: one calculator per scheme (law changes become code changes).
- **Q2 Applicability.** *Recommendation:* the S1.20d registration row decides; pack eligibility only suggests. Alternative: eligibility rules decide automatically (a
  wrong rule would silently stop contributions).
- **Q3 Province date.** *Recommendation:* the work location valid on the pay period's end date. Alternative: split the period by location (needs a statutory basis).
- **Q4 Statutory content (open, needs the professional).** No recommendation is possible for rates, bases, ceilings, eligibility, recognised-fund limits or the
  gratuity formula and entitlement: the professional supplies and validates them for the pack (AS-4, R-002, R-239).
- **Q5 Gratuity calculator.** *Recommendation:* one `gratuity` policy type and calculator serving settlement (S1.43), accrual and liability (S1.44b). Alternative: each
  slice its own formula (three answers for one question).
- **Q6 WWF and WPPF (open, needs the professional and the product owner).** No recommendation can be made without the professional: if their base is company profit,
  they are company obligations, not payroll deductions, and the product can only record a customer-entered base with a pack rate (item 8); if the professional says a
  payroll base applies, they become ordinary contribution schemes. Either way PAY-20's text is kept; how it is met is ruled when the professional answers (R-240).
- **Q7 Rounding.** *Recommendation:* per scheme in the scheme definition (pack). Alternative: the pay group's rounding mode.
- **Q8 New validation flag.** *Recommendation:* add `missing_statutory_id` to `pay_run_employees.validation_flags` values (text array; documented value, S1.38 raises
  it). Alternative: reuse `missing_tax_id`.
- **Q9 Change request CR-110.** *Recommendation:* approve: policy types and pack object types `contribution_scheme`, `gratuity`; `ContributionCalculator` and
  `IGratuityCalculator` (registered as the `gratuity` settlement calculator); table `hrm.company_contributions` (D-35, new migration file), created only
  when Q6 confirms a company-level scheme (`hrm.compliance_tasks` has no amount column); endpoint `GET /api/hrm/pay/contributions`; flag value `missing_statutory_id`;
  permission `hrm.pay.gratuity.read`; expand-only.
