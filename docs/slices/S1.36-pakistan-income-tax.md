## Goal
Every month the engine works out each employee's income tax the way the pack's tax rule says: it projects the year's taxable income from what has been
paid and what is expected, applies the slab table of the tax year, subtracts credits the employee declared and HR verified, adds what a previous employer
already deducted this year, and takes the right share this month; at year end it trues up. Bonuses, arrears and leave encashment follow their own treatment.
When the budget changes the slabs, payroll sees the effect on each employee before the new table is used. Every figure appears in the calculation trace.
No tax number lives in the code.

## What exists already (and stays)
`hrm.statutory_tables` (code, kind slab, rate, ceiling or lookup, effective-dated, pack or tenant) and rows (bounds, fixed amount, rate, key); declarations,
lines (kind from pack), previous-employer income; `pay_results.taxable_income_ytd` and `tax_ytd`; the engine's `IStatutoryCalculator` plug-in, snapshot and
trace (S1.35); component flags such as taxable from the pack (S1.34 Q1); pack loader (S1.23b).

## Build
### Part S1.36a: tax rule and calculator (Opus, fresh review)
1. **Tax rule (Q1).** A pack object `tax_rule` (code, tax year start month, effective-dated): taxable base (component flag codes to include and exclude),
   slab table code, projection method (one of a fixed list: `remaining_months_equal`, `annualise_current`, each a pure function documented in code with a
   worked example; the pack chooses), credit kinds allowed and how each reduces tax or income (`reduce_income`, `reduce_tax`, with a cap table code or
   none), surcharge (none, or a rate table applied above a threshold table), rounding of the monthly amount (unit and mode), minimum tax (none or a table).
   Stored as a policy type `tax_rule` (S1.23) so a customer can override a pack version; validated against its schema.
2. **Slab evaluation.** Slab tables of kind `slab`: rows with lower and upper bounds, fixed amount and rate on the excess over the lower bound; evaluated in
   decimal; the table version is the one valid for the tax year (BR-11); every evaluation records table code, version and row used in the trace.
3. **Monthly withholding (PAY-17).** For each employee in a run: taxable income year to date (earlier results of the tax year, not cancelled) plus this month's
   taxable lines plus previous-employer income (S1.36b) plus projected remaining income by the rule's method; annual tax from the slabs, then credits, then
   surcharge, minimum tax; this month's tax = (annual tax − tax already deducted this year including the previous employer) ÷ months left in the tax year,
   or as the method says; never negative unless the rule allows refunds in the month (flag, default from the pack).
4. **True-up (PAY-17).** In the last period of the tax year (or the employee's last period: leaver), the projection is zero and the difference is deducted or
   refunded as the rule says; the result line names "year-end adjustment".
5. **Calculator registration (S1.35 Q2).** `TaxCalculator : IStatutoryCalculator` for components flagged `income_tax`; a run whose company has no tax rule for
   the tax year makes those employees "incomplete" with "no tax rule for <year>"; trace steps for every number above.
6. **Table immutability (todo stub, Q7).** Implement `hrm.todo_statutory_table_rows_lock()` as a rules.yaml `functions` entry (new migration file): rows of a
   table version are refused update and delete once the version's `valid_from` has passed or any run references it; a correction is a new version.
### Part S1.36b: irregular payments, declarations, previous employer, preview (Opus, fresh review)
7. **Irregular payments (PAY-18, Q5).** Each component carries an irregular-payment kind code from the pack (for example bonus, arrears, leave encashment,
   gratuity, or none); the tax rule maps each kind to a treatment: `in_month` (added to the month's income), `averaged` (added to the year's income and the
   extra tax taken in the month), `separate_rate` (a rate table) or `exempt`; arrears relating to an earlier tax year use the rule's arrears treatment
   (S1.39 sends `period_start` with each arrears line).
8. **Declarations (PAY-19).** Employee portal page "My tax declarations" (S1.26 host): per tax year, lines of the pack's credit kinds with declared amounts and
   proof files (S1.24a uploads); submit through the workflow (document type `hrm.tax_declaration`, default route payroll officer); HR verifies each line
   (verified amount, never above declared) or rejects with reason; only verified amounts enter the calculation, from the next run; nobody verifies their own.
9. **Previous employer (PAY-22).** HR records employer, taxable income and tax deducted for the tax year with the certificate file; maker-checker when the S1.34
   setting `hrm.pay.change_approval` is on; included in year-to-date from the next run.
10. **Table change preview (GHR-08, section 17 AC, Q6).** For a new (not yet effective) table or tax rule version: `POST /api/hrm/pay/tax/preview` {runId,
    versionIds, employments?} recalculates the chosen employees of a calculated or locked run from their stored snapshots with the new versions substituted,
    writes nothing, and returns old and new tax per employee and the totals; the staged pack release (S1.23b) links to it.
11. **Screens.** Tax rules and tables (read and tenant override through the S1.23 policy and pack screens, with the table rows as a grid); declarations queue for
    payroll officers; previous-employer records on the employee's payroll section; the tax part of the calculation trace in plain language. English and Urdu.

## Acceptance
- PAY-17: with a `test.slab` table and a `test.tax_rule`, twelve monthly runs for a constant salary give tax totals equal to the annual tax of the table (worked
  in the test by hand); a raise in month 7 changes the remaining months only; the last month trues up to the annual figure exactly; a leaver trues up on exit.
- PAY-18: a bonus under each treatment gives the expected tax in the month (hand-worked table in the test).
- PAY-19: an unverified declaration changes nothing; a verified one reduces the next month's tax as the rule says; a declarant cannot verify their own.
- PAY-22: previous-employer tax reduces this employer's withholding for the rest of the year.
- PAY-21 and FR-XM-05: a referenced table version refuses update and delete for every login; a new version applies only from its date; no tax value appears in
  code, schema defaults or seed data (a test scans the generated seeds and the migration files for the `test.*` codes only).
- Section 17 AC: the preview on 20 sample employees lists old and new tax per employee and writes no row.
- Missing rule: an employee in a company without a tax rule is "incomplete" and the run cannot finish (S1.35 rule).
- Tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; GHR-06 on declarations and previous-employer amounts; FR ids in test names; fresh Opus review per part.

## Out of scope
See "Not built here". No real slab, rebate, credit kind, surcharge or treatment is entered.

## Questions (each recommendation is the ruling, CR-083, except Q4)
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts on Opus with a fresh review each. Alternative: one part (over the CR-047 size).
- **Q1 Tax rule as data.** *Recommendation:* the `tax_rule` policy type of item 1, with a closed list of projection methods implemented in code (a method is
  arithmetic, not law; which method applies is pack data). Alternative: one hard-coded Pakistan method (a law change would need a release).
- **Q2 Year-to-date source.** *Recommendation:* computed from results of the tax year in runs not cancelled, plus previous-employer income; never a stored running
  total. Alternative: a running total column (can drift).
- **Q3 Refunds in the month.** *Recommendation:* a flag in the tax rule (the pack decides); without it a negative month gives zero and the difference waits for
  true-up. Alternative: always allow refunds.
- **Q4 Statutory content (open, needs the professional).** No recommendation is possible for slabs, rebates, credits and caps, surcharge, exemptions, irregular
  payment treatment, the legally required projection method or rounding: a practising Pakistani payroll and tax professional supplies and validates them for
  the pack (AS-4, R-002). Until then nothing but `test.*` tables exists, and no customer payroll runs (R-235).
- **Q5 Irregular kinds.** *Recommendation:* a kind code per component (pack-defined list) and a treatment per kind in the tax rule. Alternative: treatment on the
  component itself (repeats the rule in every company).
- **Q6 Preview.** *Recommendation:* the read-only preview of item 10 over stored snapshots (it satisfies the section 17 criterion and GHR-08; the general what-if
  tool stays 1.1). Alternative: wait for PAY-36 (1.1; the criterion unmet in 1.0).
- **Q7 Table immutability.** *Recommendation:* the trigger of item 6. Alternative: application check only.
- **Q8 Timing.** *Recommendation:* build the mechanism now with `test.*` tables (the acceleration plan's Wave 4 may start without the professional); the pack
  content, its validation and any customer go-live wait for the professional. Alternative: wait for the rules before building (Wave 4 waits too).
- **Q9 Change request CR-109.** *Recommendation:* approve: policy type `tax_rule`; pack object type `tax_rule` (S1.23 handler); component attribute
  `irregular_kind`; `TaxCalculator` registration; document type `hrm.tax_declaration`; endpoint `POST /api/hrm/pay/tax/preview`; the statutory-table lock function
  (implemented entry replacing the todo stub, new migration file); permissions `hrm.pay.tax.verify`, `hrm.pay.tax.preview`; expand-only.
