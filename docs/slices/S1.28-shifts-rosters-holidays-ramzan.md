## Goal
HR defines the shifts the company works (fixed, flexible with core hours, rotating, split, night, 24-hour), the weekly off
days, and who works which shift on which day. A roster is built for a unit and a period, checked for clashes and too little
rest, then published, and each employee is told. Holiday calendars show Gregorian and Hijri dates; some holidays are optional
and an employee picks theirs. During Ramzan or a season the shift times change for the people it applies to, without editing
every shift.

## What exists already (and stays)
- Tables `hrm.shifts`, `hrm.rosters`, `hrm.roster_lines` (generated reads; writes closed), `ref.calendars`, `ref.holidays`
  (`kind`, `is_tentative`); calendar screens (S1.14a); policy resolution (S1.23a).

## Found while preparing (needs a ruling)
1. **Weekly-off patterns and "rest hours"** have no column; the minimum rest between shifts is a working-time limit (Q2, Q3).
2. **Hijri dates** need a calendar conversion; the moon-sighting day differs by a day in practice (Q4).
3. **Optional holidays** need a place to store the employee's choice and a yearly limit (Q5).
4. **Ramzan timing** is a rule by date range and group, not a shift; it needs a policy type, not a copy of shifts (Q6).

## Build
### Part S1.28a
1. **Shifts (ATT-06).** Create and change shifts; six types; start, end, core hours (flexible), segments (split), crosses midnight,
   minimum minutes for a full day; effective from a date (new row, old row kept for history).
2. **Weekly-off patterns (Q2).** A shift or an employee assignment names a pattern in the shift policy: fixed day(s), rotating
   (cycle of N days with off days), alternating weeks; the generator of days applies it to a period.
3. **Shift policy type (Q3).** `policy-types.json` registers `shift` (parameter schema: pattern, grace shown only, minimum rest minutes
   as an optional number the customer enters; no default). Resolution by S1.23 rules.
4. **Rosters (ATT-07).** Create a roster for a unit and period; assign a shift to employees, to a group (unit, grade, custom
   field) or to everyone; copy-forward from the previous period; edit single days; day types (working, off, holiday, leave later).
   Checks returned together: employee with two shifts the same day, rest below the policy minimum (only when set), shift outside
   an employee's employment dates, working on a rest day without a reason.
5. **Publication.** Publish locks the roster lines for normal edit, writes events `hrm.roster.published` (catalogue entry,
   audience the rostered employees and their managers); a change after publication needs a reason and notifies again.
### Part S1.28b
6. **Holiday additions (ATT-15, Q4).** Each holiday shows its Hijri date computed with the .NET Umm al-Qura calendar (no package);
   the company setting `hrm.att.hijri_offset_days` (-1, 0, +1, default 0) corrects moon-sighting; mandatory or optional (`kind`).
7. **Optional holidays (Q5).** Table `hrm.optional_holiday_choices` (employment, holiday, year); the company setting
   `hrm.att.optional_holidays_per_year` limits how many each employee may choose; choices are made in self-service (S1.26)
   and editable until the cut-off setting.
8. **Seasonal and Ramzan timing (ATT-16, Q6).** Policy type `seasonal_timing`: date range (Gregorian or "Hijri month"),
   shift overrides (start, end, break minutes) or a percentage shortening, applies to employee groups through S1.23 rules.
   `GET /api/hrm/shifts/effective?employment=&date=` returns the shift the employee works that day and why (policy, roster or default).
9. **Screens (FR-ADM-NAV, UXA).** Shifts list and form (engine), roster grid by unit and month (drag fill, copy-forward, check
   messages), holiday additions on the calendar screen, timing policies through the policy screens. English and Urdu labels.

## Acceptance
- ATT-06: each of the six types saved and read back; a rotating pattern gives the right off days across a month boundary.
- ATT-07: roster for 40 employees built, copied forward, a double assignment and (with a minimum set) a short rest are reported; publishing
  notifies once per employee; editing after publication needs a reason. Tenant-isolation test for every endpoint.
- ATT-15: a holiday shows its Hijri date; offset -1 moves it; an optional holiday chosen beyond the limit is refused.
- ATT-16: a Ramzan range for the "Lahore factory" group changes the effective shift for them only, with the reason shown.
- Every test name cites its FR id; no hard-coded text; checks for Urdu keys pass.

## Out of scope
Punch processing, lateness and overtime (S1.29); regularization and duty (S1.30); locking (S1.31); statutory limits.

## Questions (each recommendation is the ruling, CR-083)
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts as above.
- **Q1 Group assignment.** *Recommendation:* a group is resolved to individual roster lines at save time (lines stay editable).
- **Q2 Weekly-off patterns.** *Recommendation:* stored as a small JSON pattern on the shift policy, expanded by code; no table.
- **Q3 Rest hours.** *Recommendation:* optional policy number with no default; the check runs only when the customer sets it.
- **Q4 Hijri.** *Recommendation:* Umm al-Qura with a company offset setting; display only, never used to decide pay.
- **Q5 Optional holidays.** *Recommendation:* new table and two settings as above.
- **Q6 Seasonal timing.** *Recommendation:* a policy type resolved per day, not copied shifts; roster-locked periods follow S1.31.
- **Q7 Change request CR-085.** *Recommendation:* approve: `hrm.optional_holiday_choices`, settings `hrm.att.hijri_offset_days`,
  `hrm.att.optional_holidays_per_year`, `hrm.att.optional_holiday_cutoff_days`, policy types `shift` and `seasonal_timing`, event
  `hrm.roster.published`, effective-shift endpoint; expand-only new migration files.
