## Goal
One trustworthy record of every person the customer employs. A person exists once, even if they leave and come back or
move to a sister company. Each employment carries its job details as a history: every change (hire, promotion, transfer,
correction) is a dated event with a reason, the record can be read as it was on any date, and a change dated in the past
is checked against what it would disturb. National identity numbers and bank accounts are encrypted, shown masked, and
checked for duplicates, so the same person or the same bank account cannot be entered twice by accident. The kernel's
approvals, data scopes and licence limits get their answers about employees from here.

## What exists already (and stays)
- Tables of `docs/data-design/hrm-people.yaml` are migrated; **generated writes are closed** on all of them (CR-020, CR-021):
  every write goes through services this slice builds. Generated reads exist with data scopes and field classes.
- `WorkforceDirectory` (HR) answers the data-scope questions from employments and assignments.
- Numbering types `hrm.person_no` (tenant-wide) and `hrm.employee_no` (per company, override allowed) are registered.
- The per-person audit encryption keys (`aud.subject_keys`, S1.07) for personal fields.
- Maker-checker requests, the workflow engine and document-type manifests (`workflow.json`), outbox events.
- `employment_events.status` has draft, pending approval, approved, effective, cancelled; assignments carry `event_id`.

## Build
### Part S1.20a: persons
1. **Person service.** Create and change a person (name parts, Urdu name, father or spouse name, gender, date of birth,
   marital status from an option list, nationality, religion only where a pack field requirement asks for it, photo);
   person number from numbering; contacts, addresses (effective-dated by kind), relations (dependents and emergency
   contacts, BR-03), qualifications; custom fields of S1.15b; every change audited with the acting user (GHR-09).
2. **Identifiers (FR-HRM-EMP-05, -16, Q3).** Kinds and validation rules per country as data (`hrm` identifier-kind
   catalogue seeded for Pakistan: CNIC 13 digits with check of the format, NICOP, passport, NTN, EOBI number, social
   security number; expiry where the kind has one); value encrypted, blind index, last four digits for display; masked by
   default; full view only with the permission and in the access log.
3. **Duplicate detection (FR-HRM-EMP-18).** On create and import: exact match on any identifier's blind index blocks;
   possible matches (same normalized name and date of birth; same bank account blind index from S1.20d) are returned as a
   warning that HR confirms or sends to the review queue; `GET /api/hrm/duplicates` lists open cases.
4. **Merge (Q4).** `POST /api/hrm/persons/{id}/merge` {intoPersonId, reason}: moves employments, identifiers, relations and
   documents to the surviving person, retires the other, records the merge (Q5), audited, held for a second person when
   maker-checker is on; refused when both persons have an active primary employment.

### Part S1.20b: employments
5. **Hire (FR-HRM-EMP-02, -04, -07, FR-KRN-ENT-05).** Create an employment for a person in a company with employee type,
   joining date, first assignment (unit, branch, optional location, title, manager, cost allocation summing to 100
   percent, FTE) and a hire event; employee number from numbering or typed by a holder of the override permission (never
   reused); status `pre_joining` before the joining date, `active` from it; the active employee limit checked; event
   `hrm.employee_hired`.
6. **Changes as events (FR-HRM-EMP-03, BR-02).** `POST /api/hrm/employments/{id}/events` {kind, reasonCode, effectiveDate,
   changes}: one service for promotion, demotion, transfer within the company, grade change, acting, secondment,
   confirmation, data correction; it closes the assignment row valid on the date and opens the new one (exclusion
   constraint kept); a change between two existing rows splits correctly; confirmation date not before joining date;
   reason codes from an option list per kind. An event of a kind the customer configured for approval starts a workflow
   (document type `hrm.employment_event`) and applies on approval; an effective event is never edited (FR-XM-05):
   "correct" makes a new `data_correction` event.
7. **As-of reads (GHR-03, FR-HRM-EMP-11).** `GET /api/hrm/employments/{id}?asOf=` and `/timeline` (events with what changed,
   who and when, filterable by kind); every scoped list query takes `asOf`.
8. **Backdated change check (GHR-04, Q6).** A change dated before today asks every registered `IEmploymentChangeImpact`
   (HR module contract; attendance, leave and payroll register theirs in their slices) and shows their answers before it
   is saved; with none registered the answer says "no locked periods or calculated results are affected" and is true.
   The check also refuses changes before a payroll-locked period once FR-XM-26 exists (contract method ready, Q6).
9. **Kernel contracts.** `IApproverDirectory`: manager and manager's manager from `manager_employment_id` of the valid
   assignment, through the employment's user link (department head and position with S1.22, Q7); `IActiveEmployeeCounter`;
   `WorkforceDirectory` kept consistent with the new rows; events `hrm.employee_hired`, `hrm.employee_changed`,
   `hrm.employee_separated` (the last raised by S1.25) carry ids only.
10. **User link (FR-HRM-EMP-17, Q8).** Link and unlink an employment to a kernel user (one active employment per user per
    company); setting `hrm.user_link.disable_on_separation` (default on) read by S1.25.

### Part S1.20c: transfers, rehire, contracts, probation
11. **Transfer between companies (FR-XM-30, Q9).** `POST /api/hrm/employments/{id}/transfer` {toCompanyId, effectiveDate,
    assignment, carryOver}: ends the employment on the day before (status `separated`, reason transfer), starts a linked one
    in the other company (new employee number of that company, `previous_employment_id`, `service_start_date` carried when
    the rule says so); the carry-over choice for service, leave and loans is recorded on the event (`carry_over`) and later
    slices read it (S1.32 leave, S1.41 loans); one transaction; refused when the person has open items the rule does not
    carry (checked through the same impact contract).
12. **Rehire (BR-01).** A new employment for an existing person (found by the duplicate check), linked to the earlier one,
    with a `rehire` event; former employment untouched.
13. **Contracts and probation (FR-HRM-EMP-09, -10, Q10).** Contracts (number from numbering, start and end, renewal notice
    days, renew as a new linked contract); reminders before end through notifications; probation record at hire for the
    employee types the setting names, with period from `hrm.probation.default_months` until S1.23 policies replace it;
    extend, confirm (a confirmation event), not confirm (S1.25 separation); reminders to manager and HR.

### Part S1.20d: bank accounts and statutory registrations
14. **Bank accounts (FR-HRM-EMP-06).** Several accounts per employment, effective-dated, payment mode, bank from `ref.banks`,
    IBAN validated (country prefix, length and checksum), account number blind index (duplicate warning across employees:
    a "ghost employee" check), split by remainder, percent or amount with one remainder account; every new or changed
    account through maker-checker; masked by default and access-logged.
15. **Statutory registrations.** Per employment and scheme (EOBI, provincial social security, provident fund): applicable
    or exempt with reason, registration number encrypted; schemes from the pack catalogue (S1.23) or typed until then; no
    contribution rules here (S1.37).

## Acceptance
- **EMP AC (spec 06a section 6):** hire an employee, promote with a future date, correct the promotion date backward:
  the as-of read shows the old title the day before and the new one from the corrected date; the timeline lists hire,
  promotion and correction with who and when; the backdated change check runs and reports; nothing is edited in place.
- An identical CNIC on a second person is refused; same name and date of birth gives a warning and a review case; a merge
  moves the employments, keeps the audit trail and the retired person cannot be used; a merge by the person who asked is
  refused when maker-checker is on.
- A promotion of a kind set to need approval waits; the requester cannot approve it; after approval it is effective; with
  approval off it applies at once.
- Transfer to a sister company from 1 March ends the first employment on 28 February, starts a linked one with a new
  employee number of the second company, keeps the service start date when the rule says so, and records the carry-over
  choice; both companies' data scopes see only their own employment.
- A rehire links to the earlier person and employment; the earlier employee number is never reused.
- Hiring beyond the active employee limit is refused with the licence message; the monthly meter counts active employees.
- An approval route with "line manager" resolves the employee's manager on the document's date; manager's manager works;
  an employee with no manager gives the engine's "cannot resolve" answer.
- A new bank account waits for a second person; an invalid IBAN is refused; the same account number on two employees
  warns; amounts and identifiers are masked for roles without the field permission and full views are in the access log.
- Every endpoint: tenant-isolation suite, permission tests, data-scope tests (own, team, subtree, company), field-security
  tests; tests cite the FR ids; invariants 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 green.
- Fresh Opus review of each part with high and medium findings fixed or ruled on before its merge.

## Questions as put to the product owner
Each question has a recommendation; answering "accept all" takes every recommendation.

- **Q0 Parts and reviews.** *Recommendation:* four parts (a persons, b employments, c transfers and terms, d bank and
  statutory), each on Opus with its own fresh Opus review before merge. Alternative: three parts (c and d together).
- **Q1 API shape.** *Recommendation:* hand-written HR endpoints under `/api/hrm/persons`, `/api/hrm/employments` with the
  same paging, sorting and problem answers as generated ones; generated reads stay; generated writes stay closed.
  Alternative: open some generated writes (would bypass events and history).
- **Q2 What a "change" may touch.** *Recommendation:* assignment fields change only through events; employment fields
  (type, joining date, expected end) through a `data_correction` event; person fields through the person service with
  audit (no event). Alternative: events for person changes too (heavier, no requirement asks for it).
- **Q3 Identifier rules (GHR-15).** *Recommendation:* identifier kinds and their format rules are pack data from the start;
  this slice seeds the Pakistan kinds as a provisional catalogue marked "to be confirmed by the pack" and S1.23 moves them
  into the Pakistan pack unchanged. The CNIC rule checks 13 digits and the dash layout only. Alternative: wait for S1.23
  (no identifiers until then).
- **Q4 Merge.** *Recommendation:* merge allowed only when at most one of the two persons has an active primary employment;
  a second person approves when maker-checker is on; the merged-away person keeps its id, marked retired, with a pointer to
  the survivor, so audit history stays readable. Alternative: no merge in 1.0 (duplicates are only blocked or flagged).
- **Q5 Merge record (D-35).** *Recommendation:* add `merged_into_person_id uuid` to `hrm.persons` (new migration; data
  design change under CR-076). Alternative: keep the pointer only in the audit log (harder to follow in reports).
- **Q6 Backdated change check (GHR-04).** *Recommendation:* a module contract `IEmploymentChangeImpact` that each later
  module implements (attendance, leave, payroll), called before a backdated change and shown to the user; with no module
  registered it says so truthfully. Payroll's lock (FR-XM-26) refuses changes inside a locked period when it exists.
  Alternative: build the impact view later with payroll (GHR-04 would be unmet until S1.35).
- **Q7 Department head and position approvers.** *Recommendation:* line manager and manager's manager here; department head
  (head position of the unit) and position holders in S1.22, which has positions. Routes using them cannot be activated
  until then (the engine already refuses). Alternative: department head as "the manager of the unit's most senior person"
  (a guess the specification does not make).
- **Q8 User link.** *Recommendation:* optional; one active employment linked per user per company; unlinking keeps history;
  `hrm.user_link.disable_on_separation` default on (the user is disabled on the last working day by S1.25, open tasks
  reassigned first). Alternative: default off.
- **Q9 Transfer carry-over (FR-XM-30).** *Recommendation:* the transfer screen asks three explicit choices, each recorded on
  the event: service continuity (keep the service start date or restart), leave balances (carry, encash or lapse; applied by
  S1.32), loan balances (carry or settle; applied by S1.41); a company-level setting gives the default for each.
  Alternative: always carry everything (the rule says "by explicit rule").
- **Q10 Probation and notice before policies.** *Recommendation:* settings `hrm.probation.default_months` (default 3) and
  `hrm.probation.employee_types` (default probation, permanent) until S1.23 adds a policy type that replaces them; no
  statutory meaning is claimed. Alternative: no probation tracking until S1.23.
- **Q11 Approval of employment events.** *Recommendation:* a company setting lists the event kinds that need approval
  (default: promotion, demotion, transfer, grade change); document type `hrm.employment_event` with fields kind, effective
  date, employee, unit for route conditions. Alternative: no approvals in 1.0 (HR applies directly).
- **Q12 Future-dated events.** *Recommendation:* a daily job (`hrm.employment_events_due`) marks approved events effective on
  their date, moves status `pre_joining` to `active`, and raises `hrm.employee_changed` once; reads are as-of anyway.
  Alternative: raise events at approval time (consumers would act before the date).
- **Q13 Privacy erasure (GHR-05).** *Recommendation:* not in this slice: identity fields are already erasable in the audit
  log through per-person keys (S1.07); erasure and anonymization of HR rows needs retention rules from packs and counsel
  and is proposed for a later privacy slice. Alternative: build erasure now (rules not specified).
- **Q14 Change request CR-076.** *Recommendation:* approve: HR person and employment endpoints (Q1); identifier catalogue
  (Q3); merge endpoint and `hrm.persons.merged_into_person_id` (Q4, Q5); `IEmploymentChangeImpact` contract (Q6); settings
  of Q8 to Q11; document type `hrm.employment_event` (Q11); daily job (Q12); HR events of item 9; contracts register rows
  added at build.
