## Goal
Every night (and on demand) the system turns each employee's punches, shift, holidays, leave and duty into one result per day with a plain sentence saying why
("Late 22 minutes: shift starts 09:00, first punch 09:22, grace 10"). Missing or odd punches go to a queue. Lateness, early leaving and overtime follow
the company's policy; an approver can waive a penalty; approved overtime can turn into compensatory leave. Results are repeatable: the same inputs give the
same result.

## What exists already (and stays)
Tables above (generated reads; writes only through the engine); event store (S1.27); effective-shift service (S1.28); leave days (S1.32); policy resolution (S1.23).

## Found while preparing (needs a ruling)
1. **Policy parameter schema** is defined here (Q1). 2. **Rounding and thresholds have no defaults**; customer must set them (Q2). 3. **Overnight shifts** assign punches to the shift's start date (Q3).
4. **Multiple punches** pairing rule (Q4). 5. **Absence thresholds vs statutory limits** are customer values, not product constants (Q2).

## Build
### Part S1.29a
1. **Policy type `attendance` (ATT-09, Q1).** Schema: grace minutes (late, early), late and early thresholds, half-day and absence thresholds (minutes worked), break and lunch handling
   (deduct fixed, deduct by punches, none), minimum minutes for a full day, rounding (mode and step), weekly-off and holiday treatment. Effective-dated versions through S1.23; each day result stores `policy_version_id`.
2. **Daily processor (ATT-10).** For employment and date: effective shift (S1.28), holiday and weekly off, approved leave (S1.32), approved duty (S1.30), then punches (not duplicates, not superseded) paired in order (Q4);
   result among present, absent, late, half-day, on leave, holiday, weekly off, on duty; first in, last out, worked, late, early minutes.
3. **Explanation (ATT-10).** One English sentence built from message keys with parameters, stored as keys and values (translated at display), naming the rule and policy version that decided.
4. **Exceptions (ATT-11).** Missing out punch, missing in punch, odd count, outside tolerance of the shift, unmapped punch: a row per exception with kind; resolves when a result changes after a correction or an approved regularization.
5. **Job `hrm.attendance_process` (Q5).** Nightly and on demand for a date range and unit; chunked, resumable, run-once per employment and date; skips dates in a locked period (FR-XM-26).
6. **Reprocess rule.** A late punch or an approved request for a day re-queues that day only; a processed day is replaced by a new row version (history kept), never edited in place.
### Part S1.29b
7. **Late and early rules (ATT-13).** Per policy: count of lates allowed per month, then action: deduct a leave unit (S1.32 usage entry through its contract), pay deduction input (`pay_inputs` through S1.35's contract), or warning only.
8. **Waiver (ATT-13, FR-XM-32).** An approver waives a penalty with a reason through the workflow; the original penalty stays visible as waived; the approver is never the employee.
9. **Overtime (ATT-12).** Policy per day type (working, rest, holiday): multiplier, daily cap, monthly cap, pre-approval required or post-approval; entries created from worked minutes beyond the shift; approval through workflow; caps enforced on approval.
10. **Comp-off conversion (LVE-08).** Policy option converts approved overtime or holiday work to comp-off; publishes `hrm.attendance.comp_off_earned` (contract of S1.32 Q6).
11. **Overtime to payroll.** Approved overtime exposed through `IOvertimeInputs` (employment, period, minutes, multiplier) for S1.35; locked with the period.

## Acceptance
- ATT-10: a table of 30 scenarios (on time, grace edge, late, early, half day, absent, overnight, holiday, weekly off, leave, duty, missing out punch, duplicate punches, unmapped) gives the expected result and explanation; same input twice gives identical output.
- ATT-11: a missing out punch creates one exception; an approved regularization (double from S1.30) clears it.
- ATT-12/13: caps stop approval beyond the limit; the fourth late in a month deducts one leave unit through the contract; a waiver removes the effect and keeps the record; own waiver refused.
- A locked date is not re-processed (double); job interrupted and resumed gives no duplicate rows.
- Tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; permission tests; FR ids in test names; fresh Opus review per part.

## Questions (recommendations are rulings, CR-083)
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts, Opus, fresh review each.
- **Q1 Parameters.** *Recommendation:* the `attendance` policy type as above, in JSON Schema, no default numbers.
- **Q2 Defaults.** *Recommendation:* none; a company with no attendance policy gets "no attendance policy applies; ask HR" and day results stay unprocessed.
- **Q3 Overnight.** *Recommendation:* a night shift belongs to its start date; the next calendar day's early punches before the shift's end belong to it.
- **Q4 Pairing.** *Recommendation:* first punch of the day is in, last is out, middle punches counted for breaks only when the policy says "by punches"; odd count raises an exception.
- **Q5 Schedule.** *Recommendation:* nightly at a setting time (default 02:00 company time), plus on demand.
- **Q6 Rounding.** *Recommendation:* `numeric` minutes; rounding mode and step from the policy.
- **Q7 Explanations.** *Recommendation:* stored as keys and values so Urdu and English both render.
- **Q8 Payroll hand-off.** *Recommendation:* contracts `IOvertimeInputs` and a lateness-deduction input defined here, consumed by S1.35.
- **Q9 Change request CR-089.** *Recommendation:* approve: policy type `attendance`; jobs `hrm.attendance_process`; contracts `IOvertimeInputs`, `ILatenessDeductions`; settings `hrm.att.process_time`;
  events `hrm.attendance.exception_raised`, `hrm.attendance.comp_off_earned`; day result row versions (column `superseded_at`, expand-only).
