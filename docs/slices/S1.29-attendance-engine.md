## Goal
Every night, and whenever something changes, each employee's day is worked out from their punches, the shift they were rostered,
the holiday calendar, approved leave and approved duty: present, late, half-day, absent, on leave, on duty, holiday or weekly off,
with the minutes worked, late, early and over time. Anyone allowed can open a day and read, step by step, why it came out that way
and which policy version decided it. Missing punches and lateness become exceptions to sort out; overtime is calculated, approved by a
person (never automatically) and either paid or turned into compensatory leave; repeated lateness becomes the leave or pay deduction
or warning the company's policy says, unless an approver waives it. Payroll reads one summary per employee and period.

## What exists already (and stays)
Tables above (generated reads, writes closed; `attendance_events` append-only; `attendance_days` partitioned by month with
`explanation` json, `policy_version_id`, required `period_id`); policy resolution (S1.23a); effective shift per employee and day with
its reason (S1.28b `GET /api/hrm/shifts/effective`, and its service); leave days and ledger service (S1.32); pay groups and periods
(S1.34a); jobs with chunks and run-once keys (S1.11a); workflow engine (S1.10).

## Found while preparing (needs a ruling)
1. **No policy parameters or defaults** exist for attendance or overtime (S1.23 left the schemas to this slice) (Q1, Q8).
2. **Two policy sources:** `hrm.shifts.policy_id` names an attendance policy, and S1.23 resolves policies by rules (Q2).
3. **Work date of a night shift** and pairing of punches with unknown direction are not specified (Q3).
4. **Result precedence** when leave, duty, holiday and punches meet on one day is only "configured precedence" (BR-06) (Q4, Q5).
5. **`attendance_days.period_id` is required**, but nothing creates `hrm.attendance_periods`, and a customer without the payroll
   module has no pay groups (Q7).
6. **Late and early actions** (ATT-13) need a record of what was counted and applied, and the exception table has no waiver fields (Q9).
7. **Statutory values:** overtime multipliers, caps, working-hour limits and some Ramzan hours are labour law (Q10).

## Build
### Part S1.29a: policy, processing, explanation, periods (Opus, fresh review)
1. **Policy type `attendance` (ATT-09, Q1).** Registered in `Hrm.Infrastructure/Access/policy-types.json` with a JSON Schema: punch
   window before and after the shift (minutes), late grace and late threshold, early-leave grace and threshold, half-day below worked
   minutes, absent below worked minutes, full day minimum (else the shift's `min_full_day_minutes`), break handling (fixed deduction
   minutes or punched breaks), rounding of worked minutes (unit and direction), on-duty kinds counted as present, precedence list for
   BR-06 (Q4). No default numbers: an employee with no policy gets result `incomplete` with the explanation step "no attendance policy
   applies; ask HR" (S1.23 ruling, never a silent default). Registers the readiness check `attendance_policy_assigned` (S1.18
   `IReadinessCheck`: every active employee resolves an attendance policy today; R-159).
2. **Day processor (ATT-10, BR-05, Q3 to Q5).** For one employment and date: effective shift and day type (S1.28, including seasonal and
   Ramzan timing), holiday, approved leave days (S1.32, full or half), approved duty requests, non-duplicate punches inside the window;
   pairing first in and last out (unknown directions alternate; break punches per policy); worked, late, early and overtime minutes;
   result by the precedence; policy version resolved **as of the work date**; integer minutes and the design's numeric types only.
3. **Explanation (ATT-10, Q12).** `explanation` holds ordered steps `{step, rule, input, outcome, policy_version_id}` (shift chosen and
   why, punches used and ignored, thresholds compared, precedence applied); `GET /api/hrm/attendance/days/{employment}/{date}` returns
   the day with its explanation in the reader's language (step codes are message keys).
4. **Attendance periods (Q7).** The processor finds the employee's pay group on the date (assignment `pay_group_id`) and the pay period
   containing it, and creates the open `hrm.attendance_periods` row once (unique on pay period, advisory lock); an employee with no pay
   group on the date is not processed and is listed in the job result as "no pay group". A company with no pay group gets a default
   monthly pay group and periods from the S1.34a generator when its first employee is processed (real default record, D-40), so an
   attendance-only customer still has a lock unit.
5. **Processing job (Q6, GHR-01).** Job `hrm.attendance_process` (nightly for the previous day per company, on demand for a date range
   and a set of employees; chunked by employee, run-once per employee and date range, resumable); a day is processed only after its
   shift end plus the punch window; reprocessing is idempotent (same inputs, same row and explanation). Locked dates are skipped and
   reported. `IAttendancePeriodLock.IsLockedAsync` (S1.32 Q9) gets its real read side here, from `hrm.attendance_periods.status`,
   because this slice creates the periods; S1.31 adds locking and unlocking.
6. **Triggers for reprocessing.** Consumers queue affected days: new or late punches, roster publication and changes
   (`hrm.roster.published`), leave decided or cancelled, duty decided (S1.30), `hrm.employee_changed` from its effective date.
### Part S1.29b: exceptions, overtime, late and early, summary (Opus, fresh review)
7. **Exceptions (ATT-11).** The processor opens, updates or closes `hrm.attendance_exceptions` for missing in, missing out, late, early,
   absent and unscheduled punches (one open row per employment, date and kind); a reprocessed day closes exceptions it no longer has;
   event `hrm.attendance.exception_raised` (catalogue entry: employee and line manager).
8. **Overtime (ATT-12, Q8).** Policy type `overtime` per day type (working, weekly off, holiday): eligible, minimum minutes before
   counting, rate multiplier, daily cap, monthly cap, approval mode `pre` or `post`, comp-off allowed with leave type and conversion
   ratio; all values entered by the customer (Q10). The processor writes or updates one `hrm.overtime_entries` row per day
   (`calculated`); approval is a workflow (document type `hrm.overtime`): post-approval submits the calculated entry; pre-approval is an
   entry created ahead with planned minutes and approved before the day, then the calculated minutes are capped at it; caps apply on
   approval. Approved minutes become a `hrm.pay_inputs` row (source `overtime`, units in hours, the multiplier recorded) for S1.35, or,
   when the employee or approver chooses comp-off and the policy allows, the event `hrm.attendance.comp_off_earned` for S1.32 (status
   `converted_to_comp_off`). Never auto-approved; the approver is never the employee (FR-XM-32).
9. **Late and early rules (ATT-13, Q9).** Policy part `late_early_rules`: count of late or early occurrences in the attendance period that
   triggers an action (`leave_deduction` with leave type and quantity, `pay_deduction` with component and days, `warning`), repeated per
   multiple of the count. Table `hrm.attendance_rule_actions` (proposed, applied, waived, cancelled) records each action with the
   exceptions it counted; actions stay `proposed` and are recalculated until the period locks, when S1.31 applies them (leave ledger
   usage through the S1.32 service, pay input, or a warning notification). An approver with `hrm.attendance.exception.review` in scope
   waives an occurrence or an action with a reason (new columns `resolution`, `resolution_reason`, `resolved_by` on exceptions), never
   their own.
10. **Precedence (BR-06).** A second overlapping approved request is refused at decision time by S1.30 unless the policy's precedence
    list orders the two kinds; the processor applies that order and explains it.
11. **Attendance summary for payroll.** HR contract `IAttendanceSummary.ForPeriodAsync(employmentId, payPeriodId)`: scheduled days,
    present, absent (unapproved), half days, on leave paid and unpaid, holidays, weekly off, on duty, worked minutes, late and early
    counts, approved overtime by day type, and whether every day is processed; read by S1.34's readiness item and S1.35's snapshot.
12. **Change impact (GHR-04).** Register an `IEmploymentChangeImpact` answering "N processed days from <date> will be reprocessed; M are
    in locked periods" (locked part through `IAttendancePeriodLock`).

## Acceptance
- ATT-09/10, BR-05: a test timeline of 40 cases (fixed, flexible with core hours, split, night across midnight, rotating with weekly
  off, Ramzan timing, half-day leave plus punches, holiday worked, missing out, duplicates) gives the expected result, minutes and
  explanation steps; changing the policy from the 15th reprocesses only days from the 15th with the new version.
- GHR-01 (AC of section 9.5): 500 employees on three rotating shifts across two sites for one month are processed in under five minutes
  in CI (timed integration test with the figure in the output); a second run changes no row.
- ATT-11: a missing-out day opens one exception; adding the out punch and reprocessing closes it.
- ATT-12: post-approval overtime is capped by the daily and monthly caps the test policy sets; approval writes one pay input; comp-off
  conversion publishes `hrm.attendance.comp_off_earned` once; the employee cannot approve their own entry; nothing is auto-approved.
- ATT-13: three late days with a rule "3 lates = 0.5 day of test leave type" give one proposed action; a waiver of one late removes it.
- FR-XM-26: a day in a period whose row a test sets to `locked` is not reprocessed and is reported.
- No policy gives `incomplete` with the explanation; tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; FR ids in test names; fresh review per part.

## Questions (each recommendation is the ruling, CR-083, except Q10)
- **Q0 Parts.** *Recommendation:* two parts, Opus with a fresh review each. Alternative: three (exceptions separate), more sessions.
- **Q1 Attendance parameters.** *Recommendation:* the `attendance` schema of item 1; no default numbers; missing policy gives `incomplete`.
  Alternative: product defaults (would be invented business rules).
- **Q2 Policy source.** *Recommendation:* the shift's `policy_id` when set wins (the shift is designed with its own grace), else
  `IPolicyResolver` type `attendance`; the explanation names which. Alternative: resolver only (leave `policy_id` unused).
- **Q3 Work date and pairing.** *Recommendation:* a punch belongs to the shift whose window (start minus "before", end plus "after")
  contains it, so a night shift's out punch belongs to the start date; days without a shift use the location's local calendar day;
  first in and last out, unknown directions alternate. Alternative: calendar date only (breaks night shifts).
- **Q4 Result precedence.** *Recommendation:* default order full-day approved leave, approved full-day duty, holiday, weekly off, then
  punches (absent, half-day, late, present); the policy's precedence list may reorder leave and duty only; punches on a leave day are shown
  in the explanation. Alternative: punches always win.
- **Q5 Work on a rest day or holiday.** *Recommendation:* the result stays `holiday` or `weekly_off`, the minutes are worked and overtime
  minutes with that day type. Alternative: result `present`.
- **Q6 When a day is final.** *Recommendation:* processed after shift end plus the window, again on any trigger, until the period locks;
  the live dashboard (S1.30) reads punches directly. Alternative: process only at period end (no daily view).
- **Q7 Attendance periods.** *Recommendation:* created lazily per pay period as in item 4, with the default monthly pay group for a company
  that has none. Alternative: S1.34a creates them with pay periods (changes an approved slice; attendance-only customers still need groups).
- **Q8 Overtime approval.** *Recommendation:* pre and post approval through document type `hrm.overtime`; caps on approval; pay input or
  comp-off. Alternative: post only.
- **Q9 Late and early actions.** *Recommendation:* new table `hrm.attendance_rule_actions` and three exception columns as above; proposed
  until lock, applied by S1.31. Alternative: apply at once (a later waiver would need reversals in ledger and pay).
- **Q10 Statutory values (open, needs the professional).** No recommendation is possible: the legal overtime rate, the daily and weekly
  limits, overtime caps, night-work and any legally reduced Ramzan hours under federal and provincial law must be supplied and validated
  by a Pakistani payroll and labour professional (AS-4, spec 06a section 34.3). Until then the customer enters its own policy values and
  the pack carries none (R-157).
- **Q11 Performance proof.** *Recommendation:* the GHR-01 attendance test runs in CI on the PostgreSQL service with set-based reads per
  chunk (punches, leave, duty, roster for all employees of the chunk); S1.52 repeats it at 50,000 employees. Alternative: on demand only.
- **Q12 Explanation format.** *Recommendation:* ordered steps with message keys and inputs (no free text); size-limited; part of the
  row so it is locked with it. Alternative: a separate table (more joins).
- **Q13 Change request CR-089.** *Recommendation:* approve: policy types `attendance` and `overtime`; document type `hrm.overtime`;
  table `hrm.attendance_rule_actions` and columns `resolution`, `resolution_reason`, `resolved_by` on `hrm.attendance_exceptions`
  (D-35, new migration file); job `hrm.attendance_process`; events `hrm.attendance.exception_raised`, publication of
  `hrm.attendance.comp_off_earned` (S1.32); contracts `IAttendanceSummary` and the attendance `IEmploymentChangeImpact`; readiness check
  `attendance_policy_assigned`; day read endpoint; `pay_inputs.source_type` values `overtime` and `attendance`; default monthly pay group per company (item 4). Expand-only.
