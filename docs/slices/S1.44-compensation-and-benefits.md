## Goal
HR keeps the pay ranges for each grade and sees who is below, inside or above their range; runs an annual increment cycle with a budget, collects manager recommendations, reviews and approves them and applies the raises in bulk; manages medical and insurance plans; keeps each employee's provident fund balance with a statement; computes yearly liabilities for Finance; and gives each employee a total rewards statement.

## Build
### Part S1.44a
1. **Bands (CMP-01).** Per grade and currency, effective-dated; compa-ratio (salary / midpoint) and range penetration ((salary - min) / (max - min)) reports with decimal arithmetic; shown on the grade screen (POS-06).
2. **Increment cycles (CMP-02, Q1).** Cycle with budget, eligibility rule, guidelines; proposals per employee (manager recommendation, HR review, approval by workflow, budget used vs left); outcomes import from reviews (S1.49) as suggestions; **apply in bulk** creates compensation revisions effective on the cycle date (S1.34b) and flags the backdated ones for S1.39.
3. **Benefits (CMP-05).** Plans (kind, provider, eligibility, coverage, premium components for employee and employer), enrolment with dependants, premium deduction through the component, claims register with status.
4. **Allowance reporting (CMP-03).** Report of allowances and perquisites by employee and type.
### Part S1.44b
5. **Provident fund (CMP-04, Q2).** Accounts per employee and scheme; append-only ledger: employee and employer contributions (from the S1.37 plug-in per run), profit allocation entries by customer-entered rate and date, withdrawals with approval; statements as documents.
6. **Liabilities (CMP-07, Q3).** Job `hrm.liability_accrue` per period: gratuity and leave encashment liability per employee from policy calculators (S1.37 and S1.32 contracts; **no formula here**), `liability_accruals` append-only with reversal rows; event `hrm.liability.accrued` for Finance when licensed.
7. **Total rewards (CMP-06).** Statement per employee from pay results, benefits, employer contributions and liabilities by template; generated for a year on demand; restricted data (GHR-06).

## Acceptance
- CMP-01: compa-ratio and range penetration match hand-computed values; a grade with no band is reported, not guessed.
- CMP-02: a cycle with budget 100 and proposals totalling 110 shows the overrun and blocks approval beyond budget unless an exception is approved; bulk apply creates revisions and flags backdated ones.
- CMP-04/07: ledger and accruals cannot be updated or deleted (database); statement balances equal ledger sums; a recalculated accrual posts a reversal and a new row.
- GHR-06 in every view and export. Tenant-isolation test for every endpoint; FR ids in tests; fresh Opus review of part b.
