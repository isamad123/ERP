## Rules used

- HRMIS areas in Phase 1 (D-42): POL, EMP, POS, DOC, ATT-01 to ATT-26 (including one circular office fence), LVE, ONB, SSP, RPT basic, PAY, LON, EXP, CPL, the Android M1 groups (MOB, NTF, OFF, MSEC, MUX, API), ROL, DAT, PRV, ROA, and all GHR and NFR items. Must-have items in these areas are R; Should and Could items are L; Won't-have items are X.
- Inside Phase 1 areas, items that depend on field tasks, maps, polygon fences, helpdesk, recruitment, learning, executive workspaces, device risk signals or iOS are L.
- HRMIS areas after Phase 1: TFA, WSP, full geo-attendance (ATT-27 onward), REC, PRF, LND, ERL, AST, WFP, KSA, US, ANA, AIA, EXT.
- Kernel: the minimum kernel of D-42 is R; parties, catalog and kernel tax are L (Phase 2); rules engine, AI gateway, portal, search, collaboration, metadata designer and configuration packages are L; pooled-tenancy requirements TEN-13 to TEN-17 are X (D-47).
- Admin console: user, role, security, organization, settings, localization, operations, platform console and admin experience essentials are R; the configuration studio (except custom fields), AI controls and extensions are L.
- Cross-module rules: FR-XM-05, 06, 26 to 30 and 32 are R; the rest need Finance or later modules.

The tagging was done by rule and then spot-checked by hand; individual tags can be changed by any reviewer before approval.
